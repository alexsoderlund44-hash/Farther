Getting around

LTG Link trains and frequent buses connect Vilnius, Kaunas and Klaipėda, and a short local train goes to Trakai. Reaching the Curonian Spit means a ferry from Klaipėda, then a bus to Nida; Bolt works in the cities.

Where to sleep

Vilnius has many hostels, and the Curonian Spit has guesthouses and campsites that fill up in July and August.

What to eat

  • Cepelinai: large potato dumplings stuffed with meat
  • Šaltibarščiai: bright pink cold beet soup with potatoes
  • Kepta duona: fried rye bread strips with garlic
  • Kibinai: Karaim pastries filled with meat, a Trakai specialty

Money

Cards and contactless payments are accepted almost everywhere. Tipping is optional, and around 10 percent is generous in restaurants.

Phone data

Prepaid SIMs from Telia, Bitė or Tele2 are cheap and easy to get, EU SIMs roam free, and coverage is excellent.

Heads up

Pedestrians are required to wear a reflector when walking on unlit roads at night.

Lithuania travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Lithuania cost on a budget?

About $46 a day for one person, from $38 in Hill of Crosses to $56 in Curonian Spit. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $322 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Lithuania?

May to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Lithuania?

Hill of Crosses, at about $38 a day. Hundreds of thousands of crosses, rosaries and statues cover this small hill near Šiauliai, a moving pilgrimage site.

How long do you need in Lithuania?

Vilnius, Trakai and the Curonian Spit. 5 days.

How much is $1 in Lithuania?

One US dollar is about 0.889 Euro (EUR), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 17.78 EUR, and a typical budget day of $46 comes to about 41.00 EUR.

LTG Link trains and frequent buses connect Vilnius, Kaunas and Klaipėda, and a short local train goes to Trakai. Reaching the Curonian Spit means a ferry from Klaipėda, then a bus to Nida; Bolt works in the cities.
