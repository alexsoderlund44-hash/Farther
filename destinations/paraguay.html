Getting around

Long-distance buses from Asunción's main terminal are cheap and reach Encarnación in about 6 hours. Bolt and Uber work well in Asunción and are cheaper than taxis. The Jesuit missions near Encarnación are reachable by local bus, though a taxi helps for combining Trinidad and Jesús.

Where to sleep

Asunción and Encarnación have a few hostels and many cheap hotels, and booking ahead is rarely needed.

What to eat

  • Chipa: chewy cheese and cassava bread ring
  • Sopa paraguaya: dense cornbread with cheese and onion, despite the name
  • Tereré: cold yerba mate infusion with herbs, drunk everywhere
  • Mbejú: cassava starch and cheese pancake

Money

Paraguay is cheap, and cards work in the cities, but carry guaraníes in cash for buses and small places, as ATM fees per withdrawal can be high. Tipping about 10% is normal in restaurants.

Phone data

Tigo, Personal and Claro SIMs are cheap and need passport registration, and coverage is good in towns.

Heads up

Summer heat often passes 35°C, so plan outdoor visits for early morning and carry water.

Paraguay travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Paraguay cost on a budget?

About $32 a day for one person, from $27 in Asunción to $39 in Filadelfia. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $224 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Paraguay?

April to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Paraguay?

Asunción, at about $27 a day. A low-key capital of colonial buildings, the sprawling Mercado 4 and warm chipa cheese rolls sold from street baskets.

How long do you need in Paraguay?

Asunción and the Jesuit missions near Encarnación. 1 week.

How much is $1 in Paraguay?

One US dollar is about 5,855 Paraguayan guaraní (PYG), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 117,098 PYG, and a typical budget day of $32 comes to about 190,000 PYG.

How do you get around Paraguay cheaply?

Long-distance buses from Asunción's main terminal are cheap and reach Encarnación in about 6 hours. Bolt and Uber work well in Asunción and are cheaper than taxis. The Jesuit missions near Encarnación are reachable by local bus, though a taxi helps for combining Trinidad and Jesús.
