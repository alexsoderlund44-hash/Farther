Getting around

Marshrutkas (shared minibuses) link almost every town cheaply, and slow but comfortable trains run from Tbilisi to Batumi and Zugdidi. Bolt and Yandex Go work well in Tbilisi and Batumi and spare you taxi haggling. Book the Tbilisi to Batumi train a few days ahead in summer, and shared jeeps or minibuses to Svaneti a day ahead.

Where to sleep

Family guesthouses are the standard cheap bed, usually with a big home-cooked breakfast or dinner on offer, and Tbilisi has plenty of hostels. Mestia and Kazbegi fill up in July and August, so book ahead then.

What to eat

  • Khachapuri: bread filled with melted cheese, the Adjarian one topped with egg
  • Khinkali: big soup dumplings, eaten by hand, leave the knot
  • Lobio: stewed kidney beans with herbs, often served in a clay pot
  • Churchkhela: walnuts strung and dipped in thickened grape juice

Money

Cards work in cities, but carry lari in cash for marshrutkas, guesthouses and mountain villages, and use bank ATMs like TBC or Bank of Georgia. Tipping is not expected beyond the service charge restaurants often add.

Phone data

A Magti or Silknet SIM is cheap and quick to buy with your passport, and coverage is good in towns and along main roads but patchy in high valleys.

Heads up

Georgian supra feasts mean many toasts with homemade wine or chacha, so pace yourself and it is fine to sip rather than drain the glass.

Georgia travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Georgia cost on a budget?

About $30 a day for one person, from $25 in Chiatura to $43 in Tusheti. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $210 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Georgia?

May to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Georgia?

Chiatura, at about $25 a day. Rattling Soviet cable cars still swing across the gorge of this manganese-mining town, close to the monk's perch atop the Katskhi Pillar.

How long do you need in Georgia?

Tbilisi, up the Military Highway to Kazbegi, then west to Svaneti. 2 weeks.
