Mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. Cash machines and exchange desks pay a little less.

Getting around

JETT coaches link Amman with Petra, Aqaba and the Dead Sea, and cheap minibuses cover most other routes but leave when full. Careem and Uber work well in Amman. Book the JETT bus to Petra a day or two ahead, and arrange Wadi Rum camps and jeep tours before you arrive.

Where to sleep

Amman and Wadi Musa have hostels and small family hotels with dorms, and Wadi Rum has Bedouin desert camps. In Dana, simple guesthouses in the village are the budget option.

What to eat

  • Mansaf: lamb cooked in fermented yogurt sauce over rice
  • Falafel: fried chickpea balls, usually in flatbread with tahini
  • Knafeh: warm cheese pastry soaked in sweet syrup
  • Maqluba: upside-down pot of rice, vegetables and chicken or lamb

Money

Cards work in Amman hotels and bigger shops, but carry dinars in cash for minibuses, small cafes and rural areas; ATMs are common in towns. Haggle in souvenir shops, and leave about 10% in sit-down restaurants if no service charge is added.

Phone data

Prepaid SIMs from Zain, Orange and Umniah are easy to buy at the airport with your passport, and coverage is good except in deep desert and wadis.

Jordan travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Jordan cost on a budget?

About $49 a day for one person, from $33 in As-Salt to $80 in Dead Sea. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $343 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Jordan?

March to May and October to November is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

As-Salt, at about $33 a day. UNESCO-listed Ottoman town of honey-coloured limestone houses stacked on hillsides, with a busy souq selling olives, spices and sweets.
