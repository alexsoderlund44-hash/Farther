Getting around

Zémidjans (moto-taxis) are the cheapest way around Lomé, and the Gozem app works there for motos and cars. Bush taxis and minibuses run to Kpalimé and up the main road north toward Kara, which is the jumping-off point for Koutammakou. A shared or private car is the easiest way to reach Koutammakou itself.

Where to sleep

Look for auberges, small guesthouses and simple hotels, plus a few hostel-style places in Lomé. Kpalimé has friendly family-run guesthouses that can arrange hiking guides.

What to eat

  • Fufu: pounded yam dough served with spicy soup
  • Akoumé: maize dough eaten with okra or tomato sauce
  • Ablo: slightly sweet steamed maize and rice cakes
  • Tchoukoutou: sour millet beer brewed in the north

Money

Cash is essential outside Lomé, and ATMs in larger towns usually accept Visa. Haggle at markets and agree moto fares first; tips are welcome but modest.

Phone data

Togocom and Moov SIMs are easy to buy with a passport, and coverage is good in Lomé and along the main roads.

Heads up

Lomé's beach has strong currents and undertow, so swimming there is risky.

Togo travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Togo cost on a budget?

About $27 a day for one person, from $23 in Aného to $46 in Fazao-Malfakassa National Park. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $189 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Togo?

November to February is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Togo?

Aného, at about $23 a day. A faded colonial town on a narrow strip between lagoon and sea, with crumbling old buildings and a relaxed pace.

How long do you need in Togo?

Lomé, Kpalimé and Koutammakou. 1 week.

How much is $1 in Togo?

One US dollar is about 583 West African CFA franc (XOF), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 11,661 XOF, and a typical budget day of $27 comes to about 16,000 XOF.
