Getting around

Comfortable long-distance buses link most cities, but domestic flights on Avianca, LATAM or JetSMART are often cheap and save long mountain drives. Medellín's Metro and cable cars are cheap and easy, and apps like Uber, DiDi and inDrive are widely used. Book flights to Cartagena early for December and January.

Where to sleep

Medellín, Salento and Cartagena all have plenty of hostel dorms, and the coffee region also has rooms on working fincas (farms).

What to eat

  • Bandeja paisa: big plate of beans, rice, pork, egg and arepa
  • Ajiaco: Bogotá chicken and potato soup with corn and capers
  • Arepa: grilled corn cake, plain or stuffed with cheese
  • Aguapanela: drink of dissolved cane sugar, served hot or cold

Money

Cards work in cities, but carry cash for buses, small shops and the coffee region, and expect ATM fees per withdrawal. Restaurants often add a voluntary 10% service charge to the bill, which you can decline, and light haggling is fine at markets.

Phone data

Claro has the best coverage and prepaid SIMs are easy to buy with a passport, though an eSIM skips the store visit.

Heads up

Locals say "no dar papaya" (don't make yourself an easy target), so keep phones out of sight on the street and be careful with drinks and dating-app meetups.

Colombia travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Colombia cost on a budget?

About $35 a day for one person, from $27 in Popayán to $129 in Ciudad Perdida. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $245 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Colombia?

July to August and December to March is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Colombia?

Popayán, at about $27 a day. The White City, famous for its candlelit Holy Week processions and some of Colombia's best regional cooking.

How long do you need in Colombia?

Medellín and Guatapé, south to Salento, then fly to Cartagena. 3 weeks.

How much is $1 in Colombia?

One US dollar is about 3,309 Colombian peso (COP), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 66,175 COP, and a typical budget day of $35 comes to about 120,000 COP.

Comfortable long-distance buses link most cities, but domestic flights on Avianca, LATAM or JetSMART are often cheap and save long mountain drives. Medellín's Metro and cable cars are cheap and easy, and apps like Uber, DiDi and inDrive are widely used. Book flights to Cartagena early for December and January.
