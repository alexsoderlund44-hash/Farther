Japan

Japan by Rail

Big cities, mountain towns and old capitals linked by fast trains, a clear first look at Japan for travelers watching their yen.

Length19 days
Total, flights included$2,756
All-in per day$145
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 4 · Japan

    Tokyo 4 nights · $316

    Neon-lit Shinjuku, the Shibuya Crossing scramble, quiet Meiji Shrine and steaming ramen counters tucked into every neighbourhood.

    Budget tip: Convenience-store meals and standing sushi bars keep food costs low. Many of the best sights, like shrines and Shibuya Crossing, are free.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  2. Days 5 to 6 · Japan

    Hakone 2 nights · $166

    Hot-spring loop of mountain railway, a ropeway over Owakudani's sulphur vents, a pirate-ship cruise on Lake Ashi and Mount Fuji views.

    Budget tip: The Hakone Freepass covers the trains, buses, ropeway and boat; day-use onsen baths cost far less than a ryokan stay.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  3. Days 7 to 8 · Japan

    Takayama 2 nights · $122

    Dark wooden merchant houses, morning markets and sake breweries with cedar balls over their doors fill this old town in the Japanese Alps.

    Budget tip: Take a day bus to Shirakawa-go's thatched farmhouses. Try Hida beef skewers from street stalls.

    Getting here: about $70 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  4. Days 9 to 10 · Japan

    Kanazawa 2 nights · $120

    Stroll Kenroku-en garden, the old geisha and samurai districts and Omicho Market, where fresh crab and sea urchin come straight off the boats.

    Budget tip: Omicho Market has cheap seafood rice bowls in the morning. A loop bus pass covers the main sights.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  5. Days 11 to 14 · Japan

    Kyoto 4 nights · $296

    Thousands of vermilion torii gates at Fushimi Inari, the Arashiyama bamboo grove and lantern-lit wooden teahouses in Gion.

    Budget tip: Arrive at major temples at opening time to beat the crowds. Fushimi Inari is free and open around the clock, so hike it early.

    Getting here: about $45 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  6. Days 15 to 15 · Japan

    Nara 1 night · $57

    Bow to the tame deer in Nara Park and stand before Todai-ji's giant bronze Buddha in one of the largest wooden halls in the world.

    Budget tip: An easy day trip from Kyoto or Osaka. The park itself is free.

    Getting here: about $6 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  7. Days 16 to 17 · Japan

    Hiroshima 2 nights · $132

    Reflect at the Peace Memorial Park and Atomic Bomb Dome, then eat layered Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki sizzling on a hot griddle.

    Budget tip: The Peace Park is free and the museum is cheap. Eat at the Okonomimura food building.

    Getting here: about $85 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  8. Days 18 to 19 · Japan

    Osaka 2 nights · $126

    Takoyaki and okonomiyaki sizzling under Dotonbori's neon signs, plus an easy base for bowing deer and giant temples in Nara.

    Budget tip: Nara makes a cheap day trip from Osaka, and its deer-filled parks are free. Eat your way through Kuromon Market for lunch.

    Getting here: about $75 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,100.
