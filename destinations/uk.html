Getting around

Trains are fast but costly, so book Advance tickets early or use a Railcard if you qualify, and National Express and Megabus are cheaper for longer routes. Uber and Bolt work in major cities. In London, tap a contactless card for transit fares.

Where to sleep

Hostels are common in cities, and YHA hostels cover rural areas. Book ahead in Edinburgh during the August festivals.

What to eat

  • Fish and chips: battered fish with thick fries
  • Full English breakfast: eggs, bacon, sausage, beans and toast
  • Sunday roast: roast meat with potatoes and gravy
  • Haggis: Scottish dish of spiced sheep offal and oats

Money

Cards and contactless are accepted almost everywhere. Tipping is about 10 to 12.5 percent in restaurants, often added as a service charge.

Phone data

EE, Vodafone, O2 and Three SIMs are easy to buy without registration, and coverage is good in cities but patchy in the Highlands.

Heads up

Traffic drives on the left, so look right first when crossing.

United Kingdom travel: quick answers

How much does a day in United Kingdom cost on a budget?

About $80 a day for one person, from $61 in Glasgow to $85 in London. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $560 before flights.

When is the best time to visit United Kingdom?

May to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in United Kingdom?

Glasgow, at about $61 a day. Kelvingrove Art Gallery, Mackintosh architecture, the Necropolis, Barras market and a live music scene centred on venues like King Tut's.
