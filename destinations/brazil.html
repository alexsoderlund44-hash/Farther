Getting around

Long-distance buses leave from each city's rodoviária and can be booked online, but for big jumps like Rio to Iguaçu or Salvador, budget flights on Azul, GOL or LATAM are often worth it. Uber and 99 work in all major cities. Ilha Grande is reached by ferry or speedboat from Conceição de Jacareí or Angra dos Reis.

Where to sleep

Hostels and pousadas (small guesthouses) are found in every stop on this route, and Ilha Grande and Paraty fill up on holidays and during Carnival.

What to eat

  • Feijoada: black bean and pork stew served with rice and farofa
  • Pão de queijo: chewy cheese bread rolls made with cassava flour
  • Coxinha: teardrop-shaped fried snack filled with shredded chicken
  • Açaí na tigela: frozen açaí berry bowl topped with granola and banana

Money

Cards and the Pix payment system are used for almost everything, so you need little cash, and many restaurants add a 10% service charge. Use ATMs inside banks or malls rather than on the street.

Phone data

Claro, Vivo and TIM SIMs can be registered with a passport, but the process can be fiddly, so an eSIM is often simpler, and coverage is good in cities.

Heads up

Phone theft is common in big cities, so avoid using your phone near the street edge and carry a cheap spare if you can.

Brazil travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Brazil cost on a budget?

About $42 a day for one person, from $32 in Belém to $120 in Fernando de Noronha. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $294 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Brazil?

April to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Brazil?

Belém, at about $32 a day. Amazon port city with the bustling Ver-o-Peso market, açaí stalls and dishes like pato no tucupi and tacacá.

How long do you need in Brazil?

Rio, Ilha Grande, Paraty, then Iguaçu and Salvador. 3 to 4 weeks.

How much is $1 in Brazil?

One US dollar is about 5.22 Brazilian real (BRL), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 104 BRL, and a typical budget day of $42 comes to about 220 BRL.

How do you get around Brazil cheaply?

Long-distance buses leave from each city's rodoviária and can be booked online, but for big jumps like Rio to Iguaçu or Salvador, budget flights on Azul, GOL or LATAM are often worth it. Uber and 99 work in all major cities. Ilha Grande is reached by ferry or speedboat from Conceição de Jacareí or Angra dos Reis.
