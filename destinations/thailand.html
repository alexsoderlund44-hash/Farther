Getting around

State Railway trains, long-distance buses and minivans reach almost everywhere, ferries serve the islands, and AirAsia and other budget airlines fly domestic routes cheaply. Grab and Bolt work in Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Phuket and other big towns. Book overnight sleeper train berths ahead, especially in the cooler high season.

Where to sleep

Hostels are everywhere and cheap, with simple bungalows and guesthouses on the islands; book ahead on the Andaman islands from December to March.

What to eat

  • Pad kra pao: stir-fried meat with holy basil, chili and rice
  • Som tam: pounded green papaya salad, spicy and sour
  • Khao soi: northern curry noodle soup topped with crispy noodles
  • Mango sticky rice: sweet coconut sticky rice with ripe mango

Money

Cards work in malls and chain stores, but street food, markets and songthaews are cash only, and Thai ATMs charge a flat fee on foreign cards. Tipping is optional, and haggling is expected at markets but not in shops with price tags.

Phone data

Tourist SIMs from AIS or True are sold at airports and convenience stores with passport registration, coverage is excellent, and eSIMs work well too.

Heads up

If a friendly stranger says a temple or palace is closed today and offers a cheap tuk-tuk tour, it usually ends at a gem or tailor shop.

Thailand travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Thailand cost on a budget?

About $40 a day for one person, from $28 in Lopburi to $48 in Koh Lipe. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $280 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Thailand?

November to February is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Thailand?

Lopburi, at about $28 a day. Khmer-era temples like Phra Prang Sam Yot overrun by macaques, plus King Narai's palace and sunflower fields in the cool season.

How long do you need in Thailand?

Bangkok, night train to Chiang Mai, a loop through Pai, then down to the Andaman islands. 3 weeks.

How much is $1 in Thailand?

One US dollar is about 33.57 Thai baht (THB), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 671 THB, and a typical budget day of $40 comes to about 1,300 THB.

How do you get around Thailand cheaply?

State Railway trains, long-distance buses and minivans reach almost everywhere, ferries serve the islands, and AirAsia and other budget airlines fly domestic routes cheaply. Grab and Bolt work in Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Phuket and other big towns. Book overnight sleeper train berths ahead, especially in the cooler high season.
