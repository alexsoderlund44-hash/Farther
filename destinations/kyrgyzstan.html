Mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. Cash machines and exchange desks pay a little less.

Getting around

Marshrutkas (shared minibuses) and shared taxis link towns cheaply, leaving when full from bus stations. Yandex Go works in Bishkek and some other towns. For remote spots like Song-Kul, arrange a driver or tour through a guesthouse a day or two ahead.

Where to sleep

Guesthouses and homestays are cheap and common, and yurt camps run at mountain lakes in summer; Community Based Tourism offices can arrange stays.

What to eat

  • Beshbarmak: boiled meat with flat noodles, a festive dish
  • Plov: rice cooked with carrots, onions and lamb
  • Lagman: hand-pulled noodles with meat and vegetables
  • Kymyz: fermented mare's milk, slightly sour and fizzy

Money

Carry cash som outside Bishkek and Karakol, since cards are rarely accepted and ATMs are scarce in rural areas. Tipping isn't expected, and light haggling is fine at bazaars and with taxis.

Phone data

Beeline, MegaCom and O! SIMs are cheap and need a passport, with good coverage in towns and none in many mountain valleys.

Heads up

Mountain weather changes fast even in summer, so bring warm layers for treks and yurt stays.

Kyrgyzstan travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Kyrgyzstan cost on a budget?

About $24 a day for one person, from $19 in Osh to $49 in Kel-Suu Lake. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $168 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Kyrgyzstan?

June to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Kyrgyzstan?

Osh, at about $19 a day. Climb sacred Sulaiman-Too mountain for city views, then get lost in one of Central Asia's biggest and oldest bazaars.

How long do you need in Kyrgyzstan?

Bishkek, east to Karakol for treks, then a yurt stay at Song-Kul. 2 to 3 weeks.

How much is $1 in Kyrgyzstan?

One US dollar is about 87.47 Kyrgyz som (KGS), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 1,749 KGS, and a typical budget day of $24 comes to about 2,100 KGS.

How do you get around Kyrgyzstan cheaply?

Marshrutkas (shared minibuses) and shared taxis link towns cheaply, leaving when full from bus stations. Yandex Go works in Bishkek and some other towns. For remote spots like Song-Kul, arrange a driver or tour through a guesthouse a day or two ahead.

What food should you try in Kyrgyzstan?

Beshbarmak, boiled meat with flat noodles, a festive dish. Plov, rice cooked with carrots, onions and lamb. Lagman, hand-pulled noodles with meat and vegetables. Kymyz, fermented mare's milk, slightly sour and fizzy.
