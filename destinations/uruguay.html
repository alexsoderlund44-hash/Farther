Getting around

Buquebus and other ferries cross from Buenos Aires to Colonia and Montevideo, and comfortable buses link Colonia, Montevideo and the coast. Uber works in Montevideo. Cabo Polonio has no road, so you take a 4x4 truck from the entrance on Route 10.

Where to sleep

Hostels are common in Colonia and Montevideo, and Cabo Polonio has simple hostels without mains electricity, which fill up fast in January.

What to eat

  • Chivito: steak sandwich loaded with ham, cheese, egg and more
  • Asado: beef and sausages slow-grilled over wood embers
  • Mate: herbal tea sipped from a gourd, often carried with a thermos
  • Torta frita: fried dough, traditionally eaten on rainy days

Money

Cards are accepted almost everywhere and Uruguay is one of the pricier countries in South America, so expect higher costs than its neighbors. Tipping about 10% in restaurants is normal.

Phone data

Antel, Claro and Movistar SIMs are easy to buy, and coverage is good in towns, though Cabo Polonio's signal is weak.

Heads up

Cabo Polonio has no ATM and limited card machines, so bring enough cash for your stay.

Uruguay travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Uruguay cost on a budget?

About $54 a day for one person, from $48 in Salto to $60 in Punta del Este. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $378 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Uruguay?

November to March is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Uruguay?

Salto, at about $48 a day. A relaxed riverside city on the Uruguay River, with the steaming outdoor pools of the Termas del Daymán hot springs nearby.

How long do you need in Uruguay?

Colonia, Montevideo and Cabo Polonio. 1 week.

How much is $1 in Uruguay?

One US dollar is about 40.55 Uruguayan peso (UYU), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 811 UYU, and a typical budget day of $54 comes to about 2,200 UYU.

How do you get around Uruguay cheaply?

Buquebus and other ferries cross from Buenos Aires to Colonia and Montevideo, and comfortable buses link Colonia, Montevideo and the coast. Uber works in Montevideo. Cabo Polonio has no road, so you take a 4x4 truck from the entrance on Route 10.
