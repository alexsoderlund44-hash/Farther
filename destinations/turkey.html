Getting around

Overnight intercity buses go almost everywhere, with seat-back screens and tea service, and you can compare companies on the obilet app. High-speed YHT trains link Istanbul, Ankara and Konya, and cheap domestic flights are worth checking for long jumps east. In Istanbul use an Istanbulkart for metro, trams and ferries, and the BiTaksi or Uber apps for taxis.

Where to sleep

Hostels with dorms are easy to find in Istanbul, Cappadocia and the coast, and family-run pensions are the classic cheap stay elsewhere. In Cappadocia, cave rooms in Göreme can be surprisingly affordable outside peak season.

What to eat

  • Lahmacun: thin flatbread with spiced minced meat, rolled with herbs
  • Simit: sesame-crusted bread ring sold from street carts
  • Menemen: eggs scrambled with tomatoes and peppers
  • Çay: strong black tea served in small tulip glasses

Money

Cards are accepted almost everywhere, but carry some lira for dolmuş minibuses and small shops, and use ATMs of big Turkish banks rather than standalone Euronet machines. Haggling is normal in bazaars, and a tip of 5 to 10% is customary in restaurants.

Phone data

An eSIM is the simpler choice, since tourist SIMs need passport registration in an official store and phones on a local SIM must be registered after a few months; coverage is good in towns and along main roads.

Heads up

In Istanbul, be wary of friendly strangers who suggest a drink at a bar they know, as this often ends with a huge bill.

Turkey travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Turkey cost on a budget?

About $54 a day for one person, from $34 in Konya to $71 in Ephesus. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $378 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Turkey?

April to June and September to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

Konya, at about $34 a day. Spiritual home of Rumi, with the turquoise-domed Mevlana Museum, Seljuk tilework and whirling dervish ceremonies on Saturday evenings.
