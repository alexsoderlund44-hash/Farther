Getting around

Long-distance buses connect Lusaka with Livingstone and Chipata, and minibuses cover shorter trips. For South Luangwa, take a bus to Chipata and then shared transport to Mfuwe, or fly. The TAZARA train runs from Kapiri Mposhi to Tanzania, but it is slow and often delayed.

Where to sleep

Lusaka and Livingstone have hostels with dorms and camping, and camps along the Luangwa River offer campsites at a fraction of lodge prices.

What to eat

  • Nshima: stiff maize porridge, eaten by hand
  • Ifisashi: greens cooked in peanut sauce
  • Kapenta: tiny dried fish, fried with tomato
  • Chikanda: savory cake of orchid root and peanuts

Money

Cards work in Lusaka and Livingstone, and ATMs are common in towns, but carry kwacha cash elsewhere. Tips for guides and staff are customary.

Phone data

Airtel, MTN and Zamtel SIMs need your passport, and coverage is good in towns but drops away in the parks.

Heads up

Malaria is a risk across the country, so ask a doctor about prevention.

Zambia travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Zambia cost on a budget?

About $85 a day for one person, from $35 in Lusaka to $102 in Lower Zambezi. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $595 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Zambia?

May to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Zambia?

Lusaka, at about $35 a day. A busy capital and the country's main transport hub, with craft markets and a growing café scene.

How long do you need in Zambia?

Livingstone, Lusaka and South Luangwa. 10 days.

How much is $1 in Zambia?

One US dollar is about 19.75 Zambian kwacha (ZMW), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 395 ZMW, and a typical budget day of $85 comes to about 1,700 ZMW.

How do you get around Zambia cheaply?

Long-distance buses connect Lusaka with Livingstone and Chipata, and minibuses cover shorter trips. For South Luangwa, take a bus to Chipata and then shared transport to Mfuwe, or fly. The TAZARA train runs from Kapiri Mposhi to Tanzania, but it is slow and often delayed.

What food should you try in Zambia?

Nshima, stiff maize porridge, eaten by hand. Ifisashi, greens cooked in peanut sauce. Kapenta, tiny dried fish, fried with tomato. Chikanda, savory cake of orchid root and peanuts.
