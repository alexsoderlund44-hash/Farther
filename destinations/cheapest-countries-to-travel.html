Ranked by daily cost

Cheapest countries to travel

What a typical day costs one person on a budget in 2026, in US dollars: a dorm bed or simple room, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees. Countries under a travel warning are left out.

Whole worldAsiaEuropeAfricaAmericasMiddle EastOceania

  1. Bangladesh $22/day

    Very few tourists, river life everywhere, tea hills and the Sundarbans, the world's largest mangrove forest, all at rock-bottom prices.

    Best time: November to February. Cheapest stops: Puthia ($19) · Dhaka ($20) · Srimangal ($20)

  2. India $23/day

    Overwhelming, endlessly varied and very cheap, from Rajasthan's forts to Himalayan valleys, with sleeper trains, street food and budget hostels linking it all.

    Best time: October to March. Cheapest stops: Amritsar ($14) · Pushkar ($17) · Varanasi ($18)

  3. Kyrgyzstan $24/day

    Wild alpine lakes, nomad yurts, horse treks and free camping almost anywhere, with cheap guesthouses and some of the best hiking in Central Asia.

    Best time: June to September. Cheapest stops: Osh ($19) · Ala-Kul ($22) · Bishkek ($23)

  4. Bolivia $25/day

    The cheapest country in South America, with high-altitude cities, the Uyuni salt flat, Amazon jungle and $2 market lunches.

    Best time: April to October. Cheapest stops: Copacabana ($20) · Sucre ($21) · Cochabamba ($21)

  5. Malawi $25/day

    The 'warm heart of Africa', with a freshwater lake as big as a sea, cheap lakeside lodges and few other travelers.

    Best time: May to October. Cheapest stops: Zomba Plateau ($21) · Nkhata Bay ($23) · Livingstonia ($23)

  6. Malaysia $26/day

    Street-food capitals, cool tea highlands, one of the world's oldest rainforests and easy-going islands, all linked by cheap buses and hawker centres.

    Best time: March to September. Cheapest stops: Ipoh ($21) · Melaka ($23) · George Town, Penang ($26)

  7. Nepal $27/day

    The cheapest big-mountain trekking in the world, walking teahouse to teahouse under Himalayan peaks, with dal bhat refills and cheap guesthouses in Kathmandu and Pokhara.

    Best time: March to April and October to November. Cheapest stops: Lumbini ($19) · Tansen ($19) · Kathmandu ($23)

  8. Togo $27/day

    A thin sliver of a country with Lomé's fetish market, forest hikes and waterfalls around Kpalimé, and the mud tower houses of Koutammakou in the north.

    Best time: November to February. Cheapest stops: Aného ($23) · Atakpamé ($23) · Kpalimé ($24)

  9. Nicaragua $28/day

    Colonial cities, volcano adventures, a lake island and Caribbean beaches at Central America's lowest prices, with cheap hostels and street food.

    Best time: December to April. Cheapest stops: Matagalpa ($25) · Estelí ($25) · Granada ($26)

  10. Kosovo $28/day

    Europe's youngest country, with Ottoman towns, strong macchiato, mountain trails and very low prices.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Prizren ($28) · Pristina ($28) · Peja ($28)

  11. Vietnam $29/day

    Steaming pho on every corner, sleeper buses running the length of the country and some of the cheapest hostels and homestays in Asia. Vietnam on a budget is easy.

    Best time: October to April. Cheapest stops: Quy Nhơn ($24) · Châu Đốc ($24) · Đà Lạt ($25)

  12. Laos $29/day

    Slow river journeys, quiet mountain towns, cheap guesthouses and the most laid-back pace in Southeast Asia. Laos rewards travelers who are in no hurry.

    Best time: November to March. Cheapest stops: Muang Ngoi Neua ($20) · Pakse ($23) · Nong Khiaw ($24)

  13. Cambodia $29/day

    Angkor's temples, a sobering modern history, riverside capitals and a sleepy south coast, all with cheap guesthouses and simple street food.

    Best time: November to March. Cheapest stops: Kampong Cham ($21) · Battambang ($22) · Kratie ($23)

  14. Uzbekistan $29/day

    Silk Road cities of blue-tiled domes and madrasas, connected by fast trains and fueled by plov, with family guesthouses and big breakfasts at low prices.

    Best time: April to May and September to October. Cheapest stops: Rishtan ($23) · Margilan ($25) · Kokand ($25)

  15. Armenia $29/day

    Ancient monasteries on cliff edges, wine caves and a café-filled capital with views of Mount Ararat, all at low prices and with very few crowds.

    Best time: May to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Gyumri ($24) · Etchmiadzin ($25) · Dilijan ($26)

  16. Ecuador $29/day

    Volcanoes, cloud forest, Andean markets and the Amazon in a country you can cross in a day, with cheap buses and set lunches everywhere.

    Best time: June to September and December to January. Cheapest stops: Otavalo ($24) · Vilcabamba ($24) · Cajas National Park ($24)

  17. Georgia $30/day

    Caucasus peaks, cheap natural wine, cheesy khachapuri and guesthouses run by famously generous hosts. Georgia on a budget is one of Europe's best deals.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Chiatura ($25) · Mtskheta ($26) · Borjomi ($28)

  18. Egypt $30/day

    Pharaonic temples, Red Sea reefs and remote desert oases, with cheap koshari, sleeper trains and some of the lowest prices around the Mediterranean.

    Best time: October to April. Cheapest stops: Nuweiba ($25) · Rosetta ($26) · Alexandria ($27)

  19. Ghana $30/day

    Friendly, English-speaking and an easy start to West Africa, Ghana mixes Accra's markets and beach bars, the Cape Coast castles and the waterfalls and hills of the Volta Region.

    Best time: November to March. Cheapest stops: Larabanga ($24) · Kumasi ($25) · Tamale ($25)

  20. Sri Lanka $31/day

    Tea country train rides, ancient cities, surf beaches and wildlife on a compact island where cheap guesthouses and rice and curry are never far away.

    Best time: December to April. Cheapest stops: Jaffna ($26) · Ella ($27) · Adam's Peak ($27)

  21. Morocco $31/day

    Labyrinthine medinas, Atlas mountain hikes and Saharan dunes within a few cheap bus rides of each other, with riad hostels and mint tea at every stop.

    Best time: March to May and October to November. Cheapest stops: Sidi Ifni ($27) · Chefchaouen ($28) · Tafraoute ($28)

  22. Tunisia $31/day

    Roman ruins, Saharan oases and blue-and-white villages, with cheap couscous, shared louages and far fewer tourists than Morocco.

    Best time: March to May and October to November. Cheapest stops: Kairouan ($28) · Mahdia ($28) · Sousse ($29)

  23. Bulgaria $31/day

    Roman ruins, painted monasteries, mountain lakes and some of the EU's cheapest food, from banitsa breakfasts to shopska salad.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Veliko Tarnovo ($30) · Sofia ($31) · Plovdiv ($31)

  24. Benin $31/day

    Benin is the birthplace of vodun, with the stilt village of Ganvié, the python temple at Ouidah and the royal palaces of Abomey, all an easy trip from Togo.

    Best time: November to February. Cheapest stops: Possotomé ($25) · Dassa-Zoumé ($25) · Abomey ($26)

  25. North Macedonia $32/day

    A lake older than most on Earth, Ottoman bazaars, mountain villages and almost no crowds, with cheap burek and private rooms.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Tetovo ($28) · Skopje ($29) · Bitola ($29)
