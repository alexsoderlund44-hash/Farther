Ranked by daily cost

Cheapest countries in the Americas

What a typical day costs one person on a budget in 2026, in US dollars: a dorm bed or simple room, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees. Countries under a travel warning are left out.

Whole worldAsiaEuropeAfricaAmericasMiddle EastOceania

  1. Bolivia $25/day

    The cheapest country in South America, with high-altitude cities, the Uyuni salt flat, Amazon jungle and $2 market lunches.

    Best time: April to October. Cheapest stops: Copacabana ($20) · Sucre ($21) · Cochabamba ($21)

  2. Nicaragua $28/day

    Colonial cities, volcano adventures, a lake island and Caribbean beaches at Central America's lowest prices, with cheap hostels and street food.

    Best time: December to April. Cheapest stops: Matagalpa ($25) · Estelí ($25) · Granada ($26)

  3. Ecuador $29/day

    Volcanoes, cloud forest, Andean markets and the Amazon in a country you can cross in a day, with cheap buses and set lunches everywhere.

    Best time: June to September and December to January. Cheapest stops: Otavalo ($24) · Vilcabamba ($24) · Cajas National Park ($24)

  4. El Salvador $32/day

    Surf beaches, volcano hikes and pupusas on every corner, in a small, cheap country that is now much safer than its reputation.

    Best time: November to April. Cheapest stops: Perquín ($27) · Suchitoto ($29) · Ruta de las Flores ($30)

  5. Paraguay $32/day

    South America's least-visited country, with Jesuit mission ruins, tereré on every street corner and the wild Chaco, all very cheap.

    Best time: April to September. Cheapest stops: Asunción ($27) · Encarnación ($27) · Areguá ($27)

  6. Guatemala $33/day

    Smoking volcanoes, Mayan ruins and lake villages linked by colorful chicken buses, with cheap Spanish schools and hostels all along the way.

    Best time: November to April. Cheapest stops: Todos Santos Cuchumatán ($25) · Nebaj ($25) · Quetzaltenango (Xela) ($26)

  7. Peru $35/day

    Inca ruins, high Andes treks and some of the best food in South America, with menu del día lunches and cheap hostels keeping Peru affordable on a budget.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Ayacucho ($25) · Huanchaco ($28) · Arequipa ($30)

  8. Colombia $35/day

    Friendly cities, coffee country, cloud forest and the Caribbean coast at budget prices, with good hostels and filling set lunches almost everywhere.

    Best time: July to August and December to March. Cheapest stops: Popayán ($27) · Cali ($28) · San Agustín ($29)

  9. Mexico $41/day

    World-class food at street prices, ancient pyramids, colonial towns and comfortable night buses everywhere, making Mexico one of the easiest countries to travel on a budget.

    Best time: October to March. Cheapest stops: Cuetzalan ($34) · Mazunte ($35) · Campeche ($35)

  10. Brazil $42/day

    Beaches, rainforest and waterfalls on a huge scale, with good-value hostels, pay-by-weight buffet restaurants and long-distance buses.

    Best time: April to September. Cheapest stops: Belém ($32) · São Luís ($32) · Olinda ($33)

  11. Dominican Republic $45/day

    Beyond the all-inclusive resorts, there's a colonial capital, cool mountain towns for hiking and rafting, and bays where humpback whales gather in winter.

    Best time: December to April. Cheapest stops: Constanza ($36) · Jarabacoa ($37) · Santo Domingo ($39)

  12. Panama $46/day

    Watch ships squeeze through the Panama Canal, drink highland coffee in Boquete and sail between the tiny islands of San Blas.

    Best time: December to April. Cheapest stops: Boquete ($37) · El Valle de Antón ($38) · Cerro Punta ($38)

  13. Honduras $49/day

    Cheap Caribbean diving and Mayan ruins, best kept to the well-traveled route between Copán and the Bay Islands. Check current travel advice before going.

    Best time: February to May and August. Cheapest stops: Comayagua ($26) · Gracias ($27) · Lago de Yojoa ($28)

  14. Cuba $49/day

    Classic cars, salsa and family-run casas particulares, in a country going through a hard economic time with frequent blackouts and shortages.

    Best time: November to April. Cheapest stops: Remedios ($40) · Gibara ($40) · Camagüey ($41)

  15. Uruguay $54/day

    A relaxed, small country of colonial towns, steak grills and Atlantic beach villages, where everyone carries a thermos of mate.

    Best time: November to March. Cheapest stops: Salto ($48) · La Paloma ($49) · Piriápolis ($49)

  16. Trinidad and Tobago $55/day

    Trinidad's Carnival and doubles street food, then Tobago's quiet reefs, rainforest and beaches, a Caribbean trip with more local life than resorts.

    Best time: January to May. Cheapest stops: Port of Spain ($50) · Maracas Bay ($51) · Pitch Lake ($54)

  17. Jamaica $59/day

    Reggae, smoky jerk chicken, rainforest waterfalls and the coffee-growing Blue Mountains, with cheaper guesthouses away from the resort strips.

    Best time: December to April. Cheapest stops: Treasure Beach ($53) · Port Antonio ($54) · Cockpit Country ($54)

  18. Belize $64/day

    Snorkel the reef off Caribbean cayes, see the Great Blue Hole and wade into Mayan caves. Pricier than its neighbors, but guesthouses help.

    Best time: December to April. Cheapest stops: Cockscomb Basin ($40) · Punta Gorda ($42) · Hopkins ($43)

  19. Costa Rica $64/day

    Sloths, toucans and rainforest everywhere, plus volcanoes and two coasts. It's the priciest country in Central America, so hostels and local sodas matter.

    Best time: December to April. Cheapest stops: Cahuita ($41) · San José ($43) · Puerto Viejo ($45)

  20. Suriname $65/day

    A wooden Dutch colonial capital, Javanese warungs and some of the world's most intact rainforest along wide jungle rivers.

    Best time: February to March and September to November. Cheapest stops: Paramaribo ($41) · Commewijne ($45) · Jodensavanne ($50)

  21. Argentina $73/day

    Steak, Malbec and Patagonia, with some of the best hiking on Earth and free trails from town, though no longer cheap in dollars.

    Best time: November to March. Cheapest stops: Iruya ($38) · Quebrada de Humahuaca ($40) · Córdoba ($41)

  22. Chile $75/day

    From the Atacama desert's salt flats to Patagonia's granite towers. Pricier than its neighbors, but hostels and comfortable long-distance buses help.

    Best time: October to April. Cheapest stops: Putre ($39) · Arica ($39) · La Serena ($42)

  23. Canada $76/day

    Turquoise Rocky Mountain lakes, French-speaking Montreal and Quebec City, and huge wilderness, with hostels and campgrounds keeping costs down.

    Best time: June to September. Cheapest stops: Quebec City ($67) · Ottawa ($67) · Montreal ($68)

  24. United States $95/day

    Pricey cities balanced by world-class national parks. Road trips with campsites, motels and hostels make the US doable on a budget.

    Best time: April to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Las Vegas ($74) · Great Smoky Mountains ($76) · Big Bend ($77)

  25. Guyana $100/day

    English-speaking, little-visited and covered in rainforest, with Kaieteur Falls, savanna ranches and community-run eco-lodges.

    Best time: February to March and September to November. Cheapest stops: Georgetown ($54) · Bartica ($72) · Surama ($77)
