Getting around

Minibuses run around Georgetown and along the coast, and the Rupununi is reached by small plane or a long, rough road trip to Lethem. Kaieteur Falls is usually visited on a day flight from Georgetown, which runs when enough seats fill. Ride-hailing apps aren't a practical option, so use taxis recommended by your lodging.

Where to sleep

Georgetown has guesthouses and small hotels, while the Rupununi has eco-lodges and ranches that cost more than elsewhere in the region.

What to eat

  • Pepperpot: dark meat stew flavored with cassareep, a cassava sauce
  • Cook-up rice: rice cooked with beans, coconut milk and meat
  • Roti and curry: flatbread with curried chicken, goat or duck
  • Metemgee: hearty stew of root vegetables and dumplings in coconut milk

Money

Carry Guyanese dollars in cash, especially for the interior, as cards work mainly in Georgetown hotels and supermarkets. US dollars can be exchanged at cambios in the capital.

Phone data

Local SIMs are easy to buy in Georgetown with a passport, but coverage drops sharply once you leave the coast.

Heads up

Malaria is present in the interior, so ask a doctor about prevention and use insect repellent.

Guyana travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Guyana cost on a budget?

About $100 a day for one person, from $54 in Georgetown to $112 in Orinduik Falls. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $700 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Guyana?

February to March and September to November is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Guyana?

Georgetown, at about $54 a day. A capital of Dutch-built canals, a towering wooden cathedral and the iron-roofed Stabroek Market with its clock tower.

How long do you need in Guyana?

Georgetown, Kaieteur Falls and the Rupununi savanna. 10 days.

How much is $1 in Guyana?

One US dollar is about 209 Guyanese dollar (GYD), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 4,183 GYD, and a typical budget day of $100 comes to about 21,000 GYD.

How do you get around Guyana cheaply?

Minibuses run around Georgetown and along the coast, and the Rupununi is reached by small plane or a long, rough road trip to Lethem. Kaieteur Falls is usually visited on a day flight from Georgetown, which runs when enough seats fill. Ride-hailing apps aren't a practical option, so use taxis recommended by your lodging.

What food should you try in Guyana?
