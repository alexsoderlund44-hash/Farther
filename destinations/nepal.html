Getting around

Tourist buses run between Kathmandu, Pokhara and Chitwan, local buses and shared jeeps reach trailheads, and domestic flights save time but are often delayed by weather. Pathao and inDrive work in Kathmandu and Pokhara. Book tourist buses a day ahead and allow buffer days around mountain flights.

Where to sleep

Guesthouses in Kathmandu and Pokhara are cheap, and on trekking routes you stay in teahouses, simple lodges that expect you to eat your meals there.

What to eat

  • Dal bhat: rice, lentil soup and curry, with free refills
  • Momos: steamed dumplings filled with meat or vegetables
  • Thukpa: Tibetan-style noodle soup, good at altitude
  • Masala chiya: sweet milky tea with spices

Money

Nepal is mostly cash, and ATMs give small amounts with a fee, so withdraw in Kathmandu or Pokhara before trekking. Tip trekking guides and porters at the end of a trek, and expect to haggle in Thamel shops.

Phone data

SIMs from Ncell or Nepal Telecom need a passport and photo, and coverage is good in cities but patchy on trails.

Heads up

Altitude sickness is a real risk on the Annapurna and Everest routes, so climb slowly and descend if symptoms get worse.

Nepal travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Nepal cost on a budget?

About $27 a day for one person, from $19 in Lumbini to $88 in Upper Mustang. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $189 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Nepal?

March to April and October to November is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Nepal?

Lumbini, at about $19 a day. The birthplace of the Buddha, where the Maya Devi Temple stands among peaceful monasteries built by Buddhist countries from around the world.

How long do you need in Nepal?

Kathmandu, bus to Pokhara, then the Annapurna Circuit. 3 to 4 weeks.

How much is $1 in Nepal?

One US dollar is about 154 Nepalese rupee (NPR), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 3,086 NPR, and a typical budget day of $27 comes to about 4,200 NPR.

How do you get around Nepal cheaply?

Tourist buses run between Kathmandu, Pokhara and Chitwan, local buses and shared jeeps reach trailheads, and domestic flights save time but are often delayed by weather. Pathao and inDrive work in Kathmandu and Pokhara. Book tourist buses a day ahead and allow buffer days around mountain flights.

What food should you try in Nepal?

Dal bhat, rice, lentil soup and curry, with free refills. Momos, steamed dumplings filled with meat or vegetables. Thukpa, Tibetan-style noodle soup, good at altitude. Masala chiya, sweet milky tea with spices.
