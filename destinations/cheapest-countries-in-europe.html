Ranked by daily cost

Cheapest countries in Europe

What a typical day costs one person on a budget in 2026, in US dollars: a dorm bed or simple room, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees. Countries under a travel warning are left out.

Whole worldAsiaEuropeAfricaAmericasMiddle EastOceania

  1. Kosovo $28/day

    Europe's youngest country, with Ottoman towns, strong macchiato, mountain trails and very low prices.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Prizren ($28) · Pristina ($28) · Peja ($28)

  2. Armenia $29/day

    Ancient monasteries on cliff edges, wine caves and a café-filled capital with views of Mount Ararat, all at low prices and with very few crowds.

    Best time: May to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Gyumri ($24) · Etchmiadzin ($25) · Dilijan ($26)

  3. Georgia $30/day

    Caucasus peaks, cheap natural wine, cheesy khachapuri and guesthouses run by famously generous hosts. Georgia on a budget is one of Europe's best deals.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Chiatura ($25) · Mtskheta ($26) · Borjomi ($28)

  4. Bulgaria $31/day

    Roman ruins, painted monasteries, mountain lakes and some of the EU's cheapest food, from banitsa breakfasts to shopska salad.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Veliko Tarnovo ($30) · Sofia ($31) · Plovdiv ($31)

  5. North Macedonia $32/day

    A lake older than most on Earth, Ottoman bazaars, mountain villages and almost no crowds, with cheap burek and private rooms.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Tetovo ($28) · Skopje ($29) · Bitola ($29)

  6. Bosnia and Herzegovina $33/day

    Ottoman bazaars, emerald rivers and mountain villages at some of Europe's lowest prices, with ćevapi, strong coffee and welcoming guesthouses.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Travnik ($30) · Banja Luka ($31) · Jajce ($32)

  7. Serbia $35/day

    Nightlife, river fortresses and some of Europe's lowest prices, with hearty grilled food and cheap hostels in Belgrade.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Niš ($31) · Subotica ($31) · Sremski Karlovci ($32)

  8. Albania $36/day

    Europe's cheapest coastline and some of its wildest mountains, with Ottoman towns, byrek bakeries and family guesthouses that make Albania easy on a budget.

    Best time: May to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Korçë ($31) · Shkodër ($32) · Pogradec ($32)

  9. Azerbaijan $36/day

    Flame-shaped towers on the Caspian, bubbling mud volcanoes and Silk Road villages in the Caucasus, with cheap guesthouses and homestays.

    Best time: April to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Ganja ($27) · Lankaran ($27) · Quba ($28)

  10. Romania $37/day

    Medieval Saxon towns, Carpathian hikes, painted wooden churches and villages where time seems to have stopped, all at low prices.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Timișoara ($34) · Cluj-Napoca ($35) · Sibiu ($36)

  11. Moldova $42/day

    Europe's least-visited country, with enormous underground wine cellars, cliffside cave monasteries and very low prices.

    Best time: May to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Chișinău ($28) · Soroca ($29) · Bender ($29)

  12. Slovakia $45/day

    Castles and big mountains at Central Europe's lower prices, with mountain huts, cheap trains and some of the region's best hiking.

    Best time: June to September. Cheapest stops: Bardejov ($36) · Košice ($37) · Vlkolínec ($39)

  13. Lithuania $46/day

    Baroque Vilnius, a red-brick island castle and the sand dunes of the Curonian Spit, all at friendly Baltic prices.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Hill of Crosses ($38) · Kaunas ($39) · Klaipėda ($39)

  14. Latvia $46/day

    Art nouveau Riga, Baltic beaches at Jūrmala and the forests and castles of Gauja National Park, all on a budget.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Daugavpils ($36) · Liepāja ($39) · Ventspils ($40)

  15. Poland $48/day

    Big-city culture, mountain hikes and Baltic beaches at a fraction of Western European prices, with cheap hostels, pierogi and milk bars.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Lublin ($37) · Zamość ($37) · Toruń ($40)

  16. Hungary $50/day

    Thermal baths, ruin bars and wine country, with cheap set lunches and some of the best-value cities in Central Europe.

    Best time: April to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Pécs ($41) · Debrecen ($42) · Szeged ($42)

  17. Montenegro $51/day

    A fjord-like bay, wild mountain canyons and Balkan prices just inland from the coast, with cheap private rooms, campsites and hearty mountain food.

    Best time: May to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Ostrog Monastery ($33) · Stari Bar ($38) · Ulcinj ($39)

  18. Czechia $51/day

    Fairytale old towns, cheap Czech beer and sandstone national parks, an easy and affordable trip from Prague.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Olomouc ($41) · Plzeň ($44) · Český ráj ($44)

  19. Estonia $53/day

    A medieval walled capital, boardwalk bog walks and windmill-dotted islands, cheaper than its Nordic neighbors.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Tartu ($44) · Viljandi ($44) · Narva ($45)

  20. Malta $53/day

    A tiny island nation of golden stone cities, prehistoric temples and clear-water swimming spots, easy to explore by bus.

    Best time: April to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Marsaxlokk ($49) · Mellieħa ($49) · Birgu ($50)

  21. Cyprus $57/day

    Beaches, Roman mosaics and painted mountain churches on a divided island with cheap buses and good-value food.

    Best time: April to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Famagusta ($52) · Nicosia ($53) · Larnaca ($53)

  22. Greece $61/day

    Ancient ruins, slow island ferries and gyros for a few euros, with cheap guesthouses and quiet beaches once you leave the famous islands.

    Best time: May to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Thessaloniki ($51) · Mount Olympus ($51) · Meteora ($56)

  23. Portugal $62/day

    Western Europe's best value on a budget: tiled Lisbon and Porto, Atlantic surf beaches, cheap wine and custard tarts, and friendly hostels in every town.

    Best time: April to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Braga ($47) · Aveiro ($48) · Coimbra ($50)

  24. Slovenia $62/day

    Alpine lakes, show caves and a green little capital, easy to see in a week by bus with hostels and guesthouses everywhere.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Ptuj ($50) · Idrija ($55) · Ljubljana ($56)

  25. Germany $63/day

    Big-city culture at fair prices, fairytale castles, beer gardens and cheap regional train tickets that make Germany easy on a budget.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Leipzig ($52) · Quedlinburg ($53) · Saxon Switzerland ($55)
