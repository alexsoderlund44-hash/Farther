Egypt

Egypt along the Nile

The pyramids and museums of Cairo, temples and tombs in Luxor, Nubian villages in Aswan and a dawn trip to Abu Simbel, ending on the Red Sea in Dahab.

Length17 days
Total, flights included$1,555
All-in per day$91
Stops7

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Egypt

    Cairo 3 nights · $90

    Treasures in the Egyptian Museum, minarets of Islamic Cairo, haggling in Khan el-Khalili and cheap bowls of koshari in Downtown.

    Budget tip: Use Uber instead of street taxis to avoid haggling. The metro costs under $0.50 and skips the city's notorious traffic.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 4 · Egypt

    Giza 1 night · $42

    Stand beneath the Great Pyramid and the Sphinx, then see Tutankhamun's treasures at the new Grand Egyptian Museum on the desert edge.

    Budget tip: Walk onto the plateau yourself rather than paying for a horse or camel. Cheap guesthouses facing the Sphinx have rooftop views of the light show.

    Getting here: about $5 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 5 to 8 · Egypt

    Luxor 4 nights · $144

    Walk among Karnak's giant columns, descend into painted tombs in the Valley of the Kings and drift over the West Bank by dawn balloon.

    Budget tip: Cross the Nile on the public ferry for a few cents. Entry tickets add up quickly, so pick your temples.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 9 to 9 · Egypt

    Edfu & Kom Ombo 1 night · $30

    Egypt's best-preserved temple at Edfu, then the riverside temple of Kom Ombo with its mummified crocodiles, both easy stops on the Nile.

    Budget tip: Break the train journey between Luxor and Aswan here instead of booking a cruise. Cheap microbuses link the two temples.

    Getting here: about $6 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 10 to 12 · Egypt

    Aswan 3 nights · $90

    Sail the Nile by felucca at sunset and wander the colorfully painted Nubian villages on Elephantine Island.

    Budget tip: Sleep in a Nubian guesthouse on the west bank. Abu Simbel is a long but worthwhile day trip from here.

    Getting here: about $6 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 13 to 13 · Egypt

    Abu Simbel 1 night · $45

    Ramses II's colossal rock temples, cut from the cliff and moved block by block to safety above Lake Nasser.

    Budget tip: Stay overnight in the village to see the temples after the Aswan day-trip buses leave. Public buses from Aswan are the cheapest way there.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 14 to 17 · Egypt

    Dahab 4 nights · $112

    A laid-back Red Sea town where you snorkel coral reefs straight from the shore and climb Mount Sinai for sunrise.

    Budget tip: Snorkel the Lighthouse and Eel Garden reefs for free. Dive courses here are among the cheapest anywhere in the world.

    Getting here: about $45 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $900.
