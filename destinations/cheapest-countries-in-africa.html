Ranked by daily cost

Cheapest countries in Africa

What a typical day costs one person on a budget in 2026, in US dollars: a dorm bed or simple room, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees. Countries under a travel warning are left out.

Whole worldAsiaEuropeAfricaAmericasMiddle EastOceania

  1. Malawi $25/day

    The 'warm heart of Africa', with a freshwater lake as big as a sea, cheap lakeside lodges and few other travelers.

    Best time: May to October. Cheapest stops: Zomba Plateau ($21) · Nkhata Bay ($23) · Livingstonia ($23)

  2. Togo $27/day

    A thin sliver of a country with Lomé's fetish market, forest hikes and waterfalls around Kpalimé, and the mud tower houses of Koutammakou in the north.

    Best time: November to February. Cheapest stops: Aného ($23) · Atakpamé ($23) · Kpalimé ($24)

  3. Egypt $30/day

    Pharaonic temples, Red Sea reefs and remote desert oases, with cheap koshari, sleeper trains and some of the lowest prices around the Mediterranean.

    Best time: October to April. Cheapest stops: Nuweiba ($25) · Rosetta ($26) · Alexandria ($27)

  4. Ghana $30/day

    Friendly, English-speaking and an easy start to West Africa, Ghana mixes Accra's markets and beach bars, the Cape Coast castles and the waterfalls and hills of the Volta Region.

    Best time: November to March. Cheapest stops: Larabanga ($24) · Kumasi ($25) · Tamale ($25)

  5. Morocco $31/day

    Labyrinthine medinas, Atlas mountain hikes and Saharan dunes within a few cheap bus rides of each other, with riad hostels and mint tea at every stop.

    Best time: March to May and October to November. Cheapest stops: Sidi Ifni ($27) · Chefchaouen ($28) · Tafraoute ($28)

  6. Tunisia $31/day

    Roman ruins, Saharan oases and blue-and-white villages, with cheap couscous, shared louages and far fewer tourists than Morocco.

    Best time: March to May and October to November. Cheapest stops: Kairouan ($28) · Mahdia ($28) · Sousse ($29)

  7. Benin $31/day

    Benin is the birthplace of vodun, with the stilt village of Ganvié, the python temple at Ouidah and the royal palaces of Abomey, all an easy trip from Togo.

    Best time: November to February. Cheapest stops: Possotomé ($25) · Dassa-Zoumé ($25) · Abomey ($26)

  8. The Gambia $33/day

    The Gambia is a narrow river country of rich birdlife, Atlantic beaches and easygoing villages, with chimps and hippos upriver near Janjanbureh.

    Best time: November to April. Cheapest stops: Tanji ($28) · Kartong ($29) · Banjul ($32)

  9. Madagascar $35/day

    Lemurs, baobabs and wildlife found nowhere else on Earth, reached by long, bumpy taxi-brousse rides and rewarded with cheap guesthouses and rice dishes.

    Best time: April to May and September to November. Cheapest stops: Antsirabe ($21) · Fianarantsoa ($22) · Ambositra ($22)

  10. Eswatini $35/day

    Eswatini is a small kingdom of game reserves, mountain hikes and cultural festivals, easy to reach from South Africa and cheap to explore on foot or by bike.

    Best time: April to September. Cheapest stops: Manzini ($28) · Sibebe Rock ($31) · Ezulwini Valley ($33)

  11. Senegal $36/day

    Senegal blends mbalax music, Atlantic surf, the island of Gorée and the colonial charm of Saint-Louis, with mangrove channels in the Sine-Saloum Delta to slow things down.

    Best time: November to May. Cheapest stops: Touba ($27) · Ziguinchor ($29) · Joal-Fadiouth ($30)

  12. Lesotho $36/day

    Lesotho is a mountain kingdom entirely above 1,000 m, with pony treks, village homestays, the Sani Pass and huge waterfalls, all surprisingly cheap to explore.

    Best time: March to May and September to November. Cheapest stops: Thaba-Bosiu ($31) · Morija ($31) · Maseru ($34)

  13. Kenya $37/day

    Classic safari country on a budget, with cheap camping safaris to the Maasai Mara, cycling among zebras at Hell's Gate and a laid-back Swahili coast in Lamu and Diani.

    Best time: January to February and July to October. Cheapest stops: Iten ($25) · Mombasa ($30) · Kakamega Forest ($31)

  14. Uganda $37/day

    Track mountain gorillas in Bwindi, raft the Nile near its source at Jinja and swim in Fort Portal's crater lakes. Uganda is green, friendly and good value outside the permit fees.

    Best time: June to August and December to February. Cheapest stops: Lake Bunyonyi ($25) · Fort Portal crater lakes ($27) · Sipi Falls ($27)

  15. Sierra Leone $40/day

    Sierra Leone has empty white beaches, the Tacugama chimp sanctuary and quiet islands like Banana Islands, a destination rebuilding steadily and still refreshingly uncrowded.

    Best time: November to April. Cheapest stops: River Number Two ($35) · Freetown Peninsula ($37) · Banana Islands ($40)

  16. Côte d'Ivoire $42/day

    Côte d'Ivoire has booming Abidjan with its maquis food scene, surf beaches at Assinie and Grand-Bassam, and one of the largest churches in the world at Yamoussoukro.

    Best time: November to March. Cheapest stops: Man ($30) · Korhogo ($30) · Sassandra ($33)

  17. Eritrea $43/day

    Eritrea preserves Asmara's 1930s Italian modernist architecture, frozen in time, with espresso cafés, art deco cinemas and a dramatic mountain road down to the Red Sea.

    Best time: October to March. Cheapest stops: Keren ($35) · Debre Bizen ($37) · Senafe ($38)

  18. Cape Verde $45/day

    Cape Verde's volcanic islands offer mountain hikes on Santo Antão, a crater village on Fogo, soulful morna music in Mindelo and quiet Atlantic beaches.

    Best time: November to June. Cheapest stops: Tarrafal ($37) · Brava ($37) · Praia ($41)

  19. Angola $48/day

    Angola is one of Africa's least-touristed countries, with the huge Kalandula Falls, the Tundavala cliffs near Lubango, the Serra da Leba road and a wild desert coast.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Benguela ($42) · Lubango ($43) · Baía Azul ($43)

  20. Mauritius $50/day

    Mauritius is cheaper than its resort image, with turquoise lagoons, the Le Morne hike, rainforest trails in Black River Gorges and Port Louis street food like dholl puri.

    Best time: May to November. Cheapest stops: Curepipe ($40) · Mahébourg ($41) · Pamplemousses ($44)

  21. South Africa $52/day

    Hike Table Mountain in Cape Town, drive the Garden Route, climb into the Drakensberg and self-drive safari in Kruger. South Africa has great hostels and is easy on a budget.

    Best time: October to April. Cheapest stops: Coffee Bay ($41) · Port St Johns ($41) · Durban ($42)

  22. Mozambique $54/day

    Mozambique has long Indian Ocean beaches, whale sharks off Tofo, dhow trips to the Bazaruto islands and the historic coral-stone town of Ilha de Moçambique.

    Best time: May to November. Cheapest stops: Inhambane ($29) · Gurúè ($31) · Xai-Xai ($31)

  23. Algeria $55/day

    Africa's biggest country pairs Roman ruins like Timgad with the Sahara's wildest scenery, from Algiers' old casbah to the rock art and dunes of Tassili n'Ajjer, all with very few tourists.

    Best time: October to April. Cheapest stops: Constantine ($39) · Tlemcen ($39) · Béjaïa ($39)

  24. Zimbabwe $64/day

    Zimbabwe has the best views of Victoria Falls, the medieval stone city of Great Zimbabwe and some of Africa's finest walking safaris along the Zambezi at Mana Pools.

    Best time: May to October. Cheapest stops: Bvumba Mountains ($34) · Chinhoyi Caves ($34) · Bulawayo ($35)

  25. São Tomé and Príncipe $67/day

    São Tomé and Príncipe are tiny cocoa islands on the equator, with jungle peaks like Pico Cão Grande, old plantation houses, nesting turtles and empty beaches.

    Best time: June to September. Cheapest stops: Roça Agostinho Neto ($51) · São Tomé Island ($52) · São João dos Angolares ($52)
