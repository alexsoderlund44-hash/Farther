Kenya · Tanzania

Kenya, Tanzania and Zanzibar

Big cats in the Maasai Mara, the Ngorongoro Crater, coffee farms below Kilimanjaro and Zanzibar's Stone Town and beaches, a classic safari plus coast.

Length19 days
Total, flights included$2,811
All-in per day$148
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 2 · Kenya

    Nairobi 2 nights · $66

    Giraffes graze against a skyline backdrop in Nairobi National Park, and baby elephants come in for their morning feed at the elephant orphanage.

    Budget tip: Use matatus (minibuses) by day and taxis at night. Book the elephant orphanage's morning visiting hour ahead, as it is short and popular.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 3 to 4 · Kenya

    Lake Naivasha 2 nights · $84

    A freshwater lake full of hippos and fish eagles, with walking safaris among giraffes and zebras on Crescent Island.

    Budget tip: Camp on the lakeshore for the cheapest nights. Share a boat to Crescent Island with other travellers to split the cost.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 5 to 7 · Kenya

    Maasai Mara 3 nights · $555

    Lions, cheetahs and leopards stalk the open savanna, and the wildebeest migration thunders across the Mara River from July to October.

    Budget tip: Budget camping safaris from Nairobi include food and park fees. Three-day trips are the best value, as the long drive eats into shorter ones.

    Getting here: about $40 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 8 to 9 · Tanzania

    Arusha 2 nights · $50

    The safari base, with busy markets, coffee farm tours and Mount Meru looming over town, plus easy day hikes nearby.

    Budget tip: Compare several safari agencies before booking and read recent reviews. Stay in a hostel while you shop around for group departures.

    Getting here: about $70 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 10 to 11 · Tanzania

    Ngorongoro Crater 2 nights · $400

    Descend into a collapsed volcano packed with lions, elephants, flamingos and rare black rhinos, a wildlife haven within crater walls.

    Budget tip: Park fees are high, so keep your visit short. Budget camping safaris that include the crater start in Arusha.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 12 to 13 · Tanzania

    Moshi 2 nights · $52

    Coffee-growing town at the foot of Kilimanjaro, with clear mountain views at dawn, Chagga village walks and waterfall trips to Materuni.

    Budget tip: Compare several Kilimanjaro operators in person. Day trips to Materuni waterfall and coffee farms are cheap by dala dala plus a local guide.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 14 to 16 · Tanzania

    Zanzibar 3 nights · $114

    Get lost in Stone Town's carved-door alleys and spice markets, then head to the powder-white beaches and turquoise lagoons of the east coast.

    Budget tip: Eat at the Forodhani night food market for cheap grilled seafood and Zanzibar pizza. Shared dala-dala minibuses reach the beaches for very little.

    Getting here: about $90 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 17 to 19 · Tanzania

    Paje 3 nights · $132

    East coast village on Zanzibar with powder sand, shallow lagoons, kitesurfing schools and seaweed farmers working the tidal flats.

    Budget tip: Kite lessons are cheaper in multi-day packages. Note that the tide goes out far, so swimming depends on timing; check tide tables.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,100.
