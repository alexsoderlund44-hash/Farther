Getting around

Buses leave from Kigali's Nyabugogo station for all major towns, with tickets bought on the spot. In Kigali, moto taxis are everywhere and public buses use a tap card. Roads are good, but the drive to Nyungwe is long and winding.

Where to sleep

Kigali has several hostels and guesthouses, and towns on Lake Kivu like Gisenyi and Kibuye have simple lakeside lodges and camping. Cheap options near Nyungwe are few.

What to eat

  • Brochettes: grilled goat or beef skewers, often with fries
  • Isombe: mashed cassava leaves cooked with peanut paste
  • Ibihaza: pumpkin cooked with beans
  • Akabenz: fried or grilled pork, a popular bar dish

Money

Cards and mobile money work widely in Kigali, while smaller towns need cash, and ATMs are found in towns. Prices are mostly fixed, and haggling is light.

Phone data

MTN and Airtel SIMs need your passport, and coverage is strong across most of this small country.

Heads up

On the last Saturday morning of each month, Umuganda community work pauses most traffic and businesses for a few hours.

Rwanda travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Rwanda cost on a budget?

About $88 a day for one person, from $25 in Huye to $136 in Volcanoes National Park. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $616 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Rwanda?

June to August and December to February is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Rwanda?

Huye, at about $25 a day. A leafy university town with Rwanda's best ethnographic museum, packed with royal artefacts, crafts and traditional dance displays.

How long do you need in Rwanda?

Kigali, Lake Kivu and Nyungwe Forest. 1 week.

How much is $1 in Rwanda?

One US dollar is about 1,479 Rwandan franc (RWF), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 29,574 RWF, and a typical budget day of $88 comes to about 130,000 RWF.

How do you get around Rwanda cheaply?

Buses leave from Kigali's Nyabugogo station for all major towns, with tickets bought on the spot. In Kigali, moto taxis are everywhere and public buses use a tap card. Roads are good, but the drive to Nyungwe is long and winding.

What food should you try in Rwanda?

Brochettes, grilled goat or beef skewers, often with fries. Isombe, mashed cassava leaves cooked with peanut paste. Ibihaza, pumpkin cooked with beans. Akabenz, fried or grilled pork, a popular bar dish.
