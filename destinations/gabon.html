Getting around

The Transgabonais railway runs from Libreville (Owendo) into the interior and stops at Lopé, making it the cheapest way to reach the park; book seats ahead at the station or online. In Libreville, shared taxis are cheap if you call out your destination. Loango is usually reached by boat or a lodge transfer via Port-Gentil or Omboué, which is where costs climb.

Where to sleep

There are no real hostels, so budget beds are simple hotels and church-run guesthouses. In the parks, lodges dominate, though Lopé has some cheaper rooms and camping.

What to eat

  • Poulet nyembwe: chicken in a palm nut sauce
  • Bâton de manioc: fermented cassava sticks wrapped in leaves
  • Atanga: butterfruit (safou), often boiled or roasted
  • Régab: the local lager, cheap and everywhere

Money

Gabon is one of Africa's pricier countries, and cash is needed almost everywhere outside big Libreville hotels. ATMs in Libreville and Port-Gentil take Visa; tipping is modest and haggling is light.

Phone data

Airtel and Moov SIMs need passport registration, and coverage is solid in towns but disappears in the parks.

Heads up

Avoid photographing government buildings, police or the presidential palace.

Gabon travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Gabon cost on a budget?

About $85 a day for one person, from $55 in Lambaréné to $150 in Loango National Park. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $595 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Gabon?

June to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Gabon?

Lambaréné, at about $55 a day. Albert Schweitzer's historic hospital and museum on the Ogooué River, surrounded by lakes and forest.

How long do you need in Gabon?

Libreville, then Loango or Lopé. 10 days.

How much is $1 in Gabon?

One US dollar is about 583 Central African CFA franc (XAF), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 11,661 XAF, and a typical budget day of $85 comes to about 50,000 XAF.
