Indonesia

Bali, Lombok and the Gilis

Rice terraces and temples around Ubud, snorkelling off the Gili Islands, quiet Lombok beaches and the cliffs of Nusa Penida.

Length20 days
Total, flights included$1,793
All-in per day$90
Stops6

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 4 · Indonesia

    Ubud, Bali 4 nights · $128

    Walk through emerald rice terraces, catch temple ceremonies and start a 2 a.m. hike up Mount Batur to watch the sunrise.

    Budget tip: Warungs off the main road are half the price of tourist cafes. Avoid the paid swing photo spots, which are overpriced.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  2. Days 5 to 7 · Indonesia

    Amed, Bali 3 nights · $102

    Row of black-sand fishing villages on Bali's east coast with shore snorkelling, the USAT Liberty wreck at Tulamben and views of Mount Agung.

    Budget tip: Snorkel the small Japanese shipwreck straight from the beach with your own mask; rooms here cost much less than in south Bali.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  3. Days 8 to 11 · Indonesia

    Gili Air 4 nights · $124

    No cars, just bicycles and horse carts, with sea turtles grazing on seagrass a short swim off the beach.

    Budget tip: It's quieter and cheaper than Gili Trawangan. Snorkel gear rental is a couple of dollars, so skip the boat tours.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  4. Days 12 to 15 · Indonesia

    Kuta Lombok 4 nights · $120

    Surf breaks and horseshoe bays line southern Lombok, a calmer and cheaper alternative to the busy beaches of Bali.

    Budget tip: Rent a scooter to reach Selong Belanak and Mawun. Warungs inland are the cheapest places to eat.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  5. Days 16 to 17 · Indonesia

    Nusa Penida 2 nights · $74

    Dramatic cliffs at Kelingking Beach, swimming manta rays and crystal-clear snorkeling make this rugged island a popular day trip from Bali.

    Budget tip: Stay over a night or two instead of a rushed day trip. Roads are rough, so ride carefully.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  6. Days 18 to 20 · Indonesia

    Uluwatu, Bali 3 nights · $105

    Cliff-edge temple with an evening Kecak fire dance, surf breaks at Padang Padang and Bingin, and steep stairways down to small coves.

    Budget tip: Rent a scooter, as public transport barely exists on the Bukit peninsula; guesthouses in Bingin cost much less than clifftop villas.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,050.
