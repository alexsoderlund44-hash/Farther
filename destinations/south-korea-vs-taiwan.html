South Korea vs Taiwan: which is cheaper?

Taiwan is the cheaper of the two, at about $39 a day against $54 in South Korea, which is roughly 28% less each day. Two weeks costs about $546 in Taiwan and $756 in South Korea, before flights. Both are at their best in April to May and October to November, so you could combine them on one trip.
