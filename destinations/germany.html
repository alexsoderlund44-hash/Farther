Mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. Cash machines and exchange desks pay a little less.

Getting around

Deutsche Bahn trains link the whole country, with cheap Sparpreis fares if booked early, and the Deutschlandticket is a monthly subscription for regional transit. FlixBus is often cheaper for longer routes, and Uber and Bolt work in big cities. Book long-distance trains ahead for the best prices.

Where to sleep

Hostels are common in cities, and youth hostels (Jugendherbergen) are spread across the country. Book ahead during festivals like Oktoberfest.

What to eat

  • Currywurst: sliced sausage with curry ketchup
  • Brezel: soft pretzel with coarse salt
  • Döner kebab: spiced meat in flatbread, a cheap city staple
  • Schnitzel: breaded and fried thin pork or veal cutlet

Money

Cash is still common, though cards are now widely accepted. Rounding up or tipping about 5 to 10 percent is normal in restaurants.

Phone data

Telekom, Vodafone and O2 SIMs need ID registration, and EU visitors can roam at home rates; coverage is good in cities but patchy on some trains.

Heads up

Most shops and supermarkets close on Sundays, so stock up on Saturday or use station shops.

Germany travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Germany cost on a budget?

About $63 a day for one person, from $52 in Leipzig to $75 in Füssen. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $441 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Germany?

May to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Germany?

Leipzig, at about $52 a day. Bach's city mixes old shopping arcades with converted factories, studios and a creative arts scene.

How long do you need in Germany?

Berlin, Dresden, Munich and the Bavarian Alps. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in Germany?

One US dollar is about 0.889 Euro (EUR), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 17.78 EUR, and a typical budget day of $63 comes to about 56.00 EUR.

Deutsche Bahn trains link the whole country, with cheap Sparpreis fares if booked early, and the Deutschlandticket is a monthly subscription for regional transit. FlixBus is often cheaper for longer routes, and Uber and Bolt work in big cities. Book long-distance trains ahead for the best prices.
