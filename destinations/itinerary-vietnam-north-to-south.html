Vietnam

Vietnam North to South

Hanoi to Saigon in a month, with limestone karsts, the old imperial capital, lantern-lit Hoi An and the Mekong Delta, all linked by sleeper buses and trains.

Length24 days
Total, flights included$1,758
All-in per day$73
Stops9

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Vietnam

    Hanoi 3 nights · $81

    Slurp bun cha on plastic stools in the Old Quarter, sip bia hoi on street corners, brave Train Street, then day trip to Ninh Binh.

    Budget tip: Pho and bun cha cost about $2 a bowl at street food stalls. Grab bikes are cheaper than taxis and spare you the haggling.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 5 · Vietnam

    Ninh Bình 2 nights · $58

    Karst towers rise straight out of rice paddies here, with rowing-boat trips through the river caves of Tràng An and the old capital at Hoa Lư.

    Budget tip: Stay in Tam Cốc and cycle the back roads on a cheap rental bike. Tràng An boats are cheaper and quieter than the Tam Cốc ones.

    Getting here: about $6 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  3. Days 6 to 7 · Vietnam

    Ha Long Bay 2 nights · $110

    Thousands of limestone karst islands rise from green water, explored on overnight junk cruises with kayaking through sea caves and past floating fishing villages.

    Budget tip: The cheapest day cruises crowd the main route; overnight boats into Lan Ha or Bai Tu Long bays feel quieter for similar money.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  4. Days 8 to 10 · Vietnam

    Phong Nha 3 nights · $90

    Home to some of the world's biggest caves, where Paradise Cave and Phong Nha Cave can be visited without a tour.

    Budget tip: Rent a bicycle to ride the valley loop past rice paddies. Multi-day cave expeditions cost far more and book out early, so plan ahead.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  5. Days 11 to 12 · Vietnam

    Huế 2 nights · $56

    Vietnam's old royal capital, with the walled Imperial City, ornate emperors' tombs and dragon boats drifting along the Perfume River.

    Budget tip: Rent a bicycle to reach the tombs instead of hiring a car. A steaming bowl of bún bò Huế at a local stall is about $1.50.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  6. Days 13 to 16 · Vietnam

    Hoi An 4 nights · $108

    Lanterns glow over the yellow old town at night, tailors stitch cheap custom suits, and An Bang beach is a short bike ride away.

    Budget tip: The old town ticket covers 5 sights, so pick your favorites. Rent a bicycle for about $1 a day from your guesthouse.

    Getting here: about $6 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  7. Days 17 to 19 · Vietnam

    Đà Lạt 3 nights · $75

    Escape the lowland heat in this cool highland town of pine forests, waterfalls, coffee farms and a buzzing night market full of street food.

    Budget tip: Canyoning trips start around $30 and are the main splurge. At the night market, grilled rice paper (bánh tráng nướng) makes a cheap, filling snack.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  8. Days 20 to 22 · Vietnam

    Ho Chi Minh City 3 nights · $87

    Dodge a river of motorbikes, spend a sobering hour at the War Remnants Museum and reach the Mekong Delta by cheap local bus.

    Budget tip: District 3 and Binh Thanh have cheaper street food and banh mi than touristy District 1, and they feel more local too.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  9. Days 23 to 24 · Vietnam

    Cần Thơ 2 nights · $50

    The Mekong Delta's biggest city, where boats piled with pineapples and pumpkins crowd the Cái Răng floating market at dawn.

    Budget tip: Hire a small boat at the pier for a sunrise market trip instead of an expensive tour from Saigon. Slurp hủ tiếu noodles aboard the floating stalls.

    Getting here: about $6 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $950.
