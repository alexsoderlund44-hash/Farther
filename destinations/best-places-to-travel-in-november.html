Where to go in November

Cheap places to travel in November

46 countries with good weather in November, cheapest first. Daily budgets are for one person in US dollars.

JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec

  1. Bangladesh $22/day

    Very few tourists, river life everywhere, tea hills and the Sundarbans, the world's largest mangrove forest, all at rock-bottom prices.

    Cheapest stops: Puthia ($19) · Dhaka ($20) · Srimangal ($20)

  2. India $23/day

    Overwhelming, endlessly varied and very cheap, from Rajasthan's forts to Himalayan valleys, with sleeper trains, street food and budget hostels linking it all.

    Cheapest stops: Amritsar ($14) · Pushkar ($17) · Varanasi ($18)

  3. Nepal $27/day

    The cheapest big-mountain trekking in the world, walking teahouse to teahouse under Himalayan peaks, with dal bhat refills and cheap guesthouses in Kathmandu and Pokhara.

    Cheapest stops: Lumbini ($19) · Tansen ($19) · Kathmandu ($23)

  4. Togo $27/day

    A thin sliver of a country with Lomé's fetish market, forest hikes and waterfalls around Kpalimé, and the mud tower houses of Koutammakou in the north.

    Cheapest stops: Aného ($23) · Atakpamé ($23) · Kpalimé ($24)

  5. Vietnam $29/day

    Steaming pho on every corner, sleeper buses running the length of the country and some of the cheapest hostels and homestays in Asia. Vietnam on a budget is easy.

    Cheapest stops: Quy Nhơn ($24) · Châu Đốc ($24) · Đà Lạt ($25)

  6. Laos $29/day

    Slow river journeys, quiet mountain towns, cheap guesthouses and the most laid-back pace in Southeast Asia. Laos rewards travelers who are in no hurry.

    Cheapest stops: Muang Ngoi Neua ($20) · Pakse ($23) · Nong Khiaw ($24)

  7. Cambodia $29/day

    Angkor's temples, a sobering modern history, riverside capitals and a sleepy south coast, all with cheap guesthouses and simple street food.

    Cheapest stops: Kampong Cham ($21) · Battambang ($22) · Kratie ($23)

  8. Egypt $30/day

    Pharaonic temples, Red Sea reefs and remote desert oases, with cheap koshari, sleeper trains and some of the lowest prices around the Mediterranean.

    Cheapest stops: Nuweiba ($25) · Rosetta ($26) · Alexandria ($27)

  9. Ghana $30/day

    Friendly, English-speaking and an easy start to West Africa, Ghana mixes Accra's markets and beach bars, the Cape Coast castles and the waterfalls and hills of the Volta Region.

    Cheapest stops: Larabanga ($24) · Kumasi ($25) · Tamale ($25)

  10. Morocco $31/day

    Labyrinthine medinas, Atlas mountain hikes and Saharan dunes within a few cheap bus rides of each other, with riad hostels and mint tea at every stop.

    Cheapest stops: Sidi Ifni ($27) · Chefchaouen ($28) · Tafraoute ($28)

  11. Tunisia $31/day

    Roman ruins, Saharan oases and blue-and-white villages, with cheap couscous, shared louages and far fewer tourists than Morocco.

    Cheapest stops: Kairouan ($28) · Mahdia ($28) · Sousse ($29)

  12. Benin $31/day

    Benin is the birthplace of vodun, with the stilt village of Ganvié, the python temple at Ouidah and the royal palaces of Abomey, all an easy trip from Togo.

    Cheapest stops: Possotomé ($25) · Dassa-Zoumé ($25) · Abomey ($26)

  13. El Salvador $32/day

    Surf beaches, volcano hikes and pupusas on every corner, in a small, cheap country that is now much safer than its reputation.

    Cheapest stops: Perquín ($27) · Suchitoto ($29) · Ruta de las Flores ($30)

  14. Guatemala $33/day

    Smoking volcanoes, Mayan ruins and lake villages linked by colorful chicken buses, with cheap Spanish schools and hostels all along the way.

    Cheapest stops: Todos Santos Cuchumatán ($25) · Nebaj ($25) · Quetzaltenango (Xela) ($26)

  15. The Gambia $33/day

    The Gambia is a narrow river country of rich birdlife, Atlantic beaches and easygoing villages, with chimps and hippos upriver near Janjanbureh.

    Cheapest stops: Tanji ($28) · Kartong ($29) · Banjul ($32)

  16. Madagascar $35/day

    Lemurs, baobabs and wildlife found nowhere else on Earth, reached by long, bumpy taxi-brousse rides and rewarded with cheap guesthouses and rice dishes.

    Cheapest stops: Antsirabe ($21) · Fianarantsoa ($22) · Ambositra ($22)

  17. Senegal $36/day

    Senegal blends mbalax music, Atlantic surf, the island of Gorée and the colonial charm of Saint-Louis, with mangrove channels in the Sine-Saloum Delta to slow things down.

    Cheapest stops: Touba ($27) · Ziguinchor ($29) · Joal-Fadiouth ($30)

  18. Lesotho $36/day

    Lesotho is a mountain kingdom entirely above 1,000 m, with pony treks, village homestays, the Sani Pass and huge waterfalls, all surprisingly cheap to explore.

    Cheapest stops: Thaba-Bosiu ($31) · Morija ($31) · Maseru ($34)

  19. Taiwan $39/day

    Night markets, marble gorges and mountain tea villages, all linked by spotless trains, with cheap street food and hostels that make Taiwan easy on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Tainan ($32) · Kaohsiung ($32) · Hualien ($33)

  20. Thailand $40/day

    The classic first big trip on a budget: easy transport, cheap guesthouses and hostels, legendary street food, tropical islands and misty northern mountains.

    Cheapest stops: Lopburi ($28) · Chiang Rai ($29) · Phimai ($29)

  21. Sierra Leone $40/day

    Sierra Leone has empty white beaches, the Tacugama chimp sanctuary and quiet islands like Banana Islands, a destination rebuilding steadily and still refreshingly uncrowded.

    Cheapest stops: River Number Two ($35) · Freetown Peninsula ($37) · Banana Islands ($40)

  22. Mexico $41/day

    World-class food at street prices, ancient pyramids, colonial towns and comfortable night buses everywhere, making Mexico one of the easiest countries to travel on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Cuetzalan ($34) · Mazunte ($35) · Campeche ($35)

  23. Côte d'Ivoire $42/day

    Côte d'Ivoire has booming Abidjan with its maquis food scene, surf beaches at Assinie and Grand-Bassam, and one of the largest churches in the world at Yamoussoukro.

    Cheapest stops: Man ($30) · Korhogo ($30) · Sassandra ($33)

  24. Eritrea $43/day

    Eritrea preserves Asmara's 1930s Italian modernist architecture, frozen in time, with espresso cafés, art deco cinemas and a dramatic mountain road down to the Red Sea.

    Cheapest stops: Keren ($35) · Debre Bizen ($37) · Senafe ($38)

  25. Cape Verde $45/day

    Cape Verde's volcanic islands offer mountain hikes on Santo Antão, a crater village on Fogo, soulful morna music in Mindelo and quiet Atlantic beaches.

    Cheapest stops: Tarrafal ($37) · Brava ($37) · Praia ($41)

  26. Jordan $49/day

    Rose-red Petra, Mars-like Wadi Rum and a long-distance hiking trail that crosses the whole country, with falafel stands and Bedouin camps along the way.

    Cheapest stops: As-Salt ($33) · Little Petra (Siq al-Barid) ($36) · Karak ($37)

  27. Cuba $49/day

    Classic cars, salsa and family-run casas particulares, in a country going through a hard economic time with frequent blackouts and shortages.

    Cheapest stops: Remedios ($40) · Gibara ($40) · Camagüey ($41)

  28. Mauritius $50/day

    Mauritius is cheaper than its resort image, with turquoise lagoons, the Le Morne hike, rainforest trails in Black River Gorges and Port Louis street food like dholl puri.

    Cheapest stops: Curepipe ($40) · Mahébourg ($41) · Pamplemousses ($44)

  29. South Africa $52/day

    Hike Table Mountain in Cape Town, drive the Garden Route, climb into the Drakensberg and self-drive safari in Kruger. South Africa has great hostels and is easy on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Coffee Bay ($41) · Port St Johns ($41) · Durban ($42)

  30. United Arab Emirates $53/day

    Glitzy and expensive, but cheap if you stick to old Dubai, metro rides, free beaches and South Asian canteens.

    Cheapest stops: Al Ain ($47) · Sharjah ($48) · Abu Dhabi ($53)

  31. South Korea $54/day

    Royal palaces, mountain temples and sizzling street food, linked by fast KTX trains and great-value guesthouses that make South Korea easy on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Daegu ($38) · Suwon ($41) · Jeonju ($42)

  32. Mozambique $54/day

    Mozambique has long Indian Ocean beaches, whale sharks off Tofo, dhow trips to the Bazaruto islands and the historic coral-stone town of Ilha de Moçambique.

    Cheapest stops: Inhambane ($29) · Gurúè ($31) · Xai-Xai ($31)

  33. Uruguay $54/day

    A relaxed, small country of colonial towns, steak grills and Atlantic beach villages, where everyone carries a thermos of mate.

    Cheapest stops: Salto ($48) · La Paloma ($49) · Piriápolis ($49)

  34. Algeria $55/day

    Africa's biggest country pairs Roman ruins like Timgad with the Sahara's wildest scenery, from Algiers' old casbah to the rock art and dunes of Tassili n'Ajjer, all with very few tourists.

    Cheapest stops: Constantine ($39) · Tlemcen ($39) · Béjaïa ($39)

  35. Oman $56/day

    Turquoise wadis, mud-brick forts and empty desert, best seen on a budget by rental car with free wild camping under the stars.

    Cheapest stops: Wadi Shab ($40) · Bimmah Sinkhole ($45) · Wadi Tiwi ($46)

  36. Suriname $65/day

    A wooden Dutch colonial capital, Javanese warungs and some of the world's most intact rainforest along wide jungle rivers.

    Cheapest stops: Paramaribo ($41) · Commewijne ($45) · Jodensavanne ($50)

  37. Japan $66/day

    Pricier than its neighbours, but convenience-store meals, capsule hostels and rail passes make Japan workable on a budget, from Tokyo to Kyoto's temples.

    Cheapest stops: Naha, Okinawa ($52) · Fukuoka ($53) · Nara ($57)

  38. Argentina $73/day

    Steak, Malbec and Patagonia, with some of the best hiking on Earth and free trails from town, though no longer cheap in dollars.

    Cheapest stops: Iruya ($38) · Quebrada de Humahuaca ($40) · Córdoba ($41)

  39. Chile $75/day

    From the Atacama desert's salt flats to Patagonia's granite towers. Pricier than its neighbors, but hostels and comfortable long-distance buses help.

    Cheapest stops: Putre ($39) · Arica ($39) · La Serena ($42)

  40. New Zealand $76/day

    Fjords, volcanoes and Great Walks, best seen by campervan with hostels and cheap Department of Conservation campsites in between.

    Cheapest stops: Napier ($56) · Christchurch ($58) · Dunedin ($58)

  41. Australia $77/day

    Expensive, but hostels, campervans and working holiday visas make long trips possible, from Sydney's beaches to the Outback and the Reef.

    Cheapest stops: Blue Mountains ($57) · Adelaide ($59) · Melbourne ($61)

  42. Saudi Arabia $90/day

    Open to tourists only since 2019, with carved Nabataean tombs, coral-stone old towns and vast desert landscapes.

    Cheapest stops: Taif ($58) · Al-Ahsa Oasis ($58) · Abha ($59)

  43. Djibouti $100/day

    Djibouti is a small, expensive country with otherworldly landscapes, from the salt flats of Lake Assal to the limestone chimneys of Lac Abbé, plus whale sharks in the bay.

    Cheapest stops: Obock ($67) · Djibouti City ($75) · Day Forest National Park ($77)

  44. Guyana $100/day

    English-speaking, little-visited and covered in rainforest, with Kaieteur Falls, savanna ranches and community-run eco-lodges.

    Cheapest stops: Georgetown ($54) · Bartica ($72) · Surama ($77)

  45. Seychelles $106/day

    The Seychelles has granite boulder beaches on remote islands. It is pricey, but self-catering guesthouses, local buses and ferries make Mahé, Praslin and La Digue doable on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Victoria ($75) · Mahé ($94) · La Digue ($101)

  46. Bhutan $230/day

    A Himalayan kingdom of cliffside monasteries and fortress dzongs, where a daily tourist fee makes short trips the budget option.

    Cheapest stops: Paro ($230) · Punakha ($230) · Tiger's Nest ($230)
