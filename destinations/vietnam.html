Getting around

Sleeper buses and the north-south Reunification Railway cover most of the country, and budget airlines like VietJet make long hops cheap. In cities, Grab, Be and Xanh SM are easy for motorbike and car rides. Book sleeper train berths a few days ahead, and further ahead around Tet.

Where to sleep

Hostel dorms and family guesthouses are cheap and plentiful, and homestays are common in Ha Giang and the northern mountains.

What to eat

  • Pho: beef or chicken noodle soup with fresh herbs
  • Banh mi: baguette sandwich with pork, pate and pickled vegetables
  • Bun cha: grilled pork with rice noodles, Hanoi style
  • Ca phe sua da: strong iced coffee with condensed milk

Money

Cash rules at street stalls and small guesthouses, while bigger hotels and cafes take cards; most ATMs charge a fee per withdrawal. Tipping isn't expected, and light haggling is normal at markets.

Phone data

A local SIM from Viettel or Vinaphone is cheap and needs your passport for registration, coverage is good across most of the country, and an eSIM saves the airport queue.

Heads up

Riding a motorbike without a valid license usually voids travel insurance, so check your cover before renting one.

Vietnam travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Vietnam cost on a budget?

About $29 a day for one person, from $24 in Quy Nhơn to $55 in Ha Long Bay. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $203 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Vietnam?

October to April is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Vietnam?

Quy Nhơn, at about $24 a day. Uncrowded beaches, ancient Cham towers and colorful fishing villages give this central-coast city the feel Vietnam's resort towns had decades ago.

How long do you need in Vietnam?

Hanoi, then north to Ha Giang, then fly or bus south via Hoi An to Ho Chi Minh City. 3 to 4 weeks.

How much is $1 in Vietnam?

One US dollar is about 25,949 Vietnamese dong (VND), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 518,982 VND, and a typical budget day of $29 comes to about 750,000 VND.

How do you get around Vietnam cheaply?

Sleeper buses and the north-south Reunification Railway cover most of the country, and budget airlines like VietJet make long hops cheap. In cities, Grab, Be and Xanh SM are easy for motorbike and car rides. Book sleeper train berths a few days ahead, and further ahead around Tet.

What food should you try in Vietnam?

Pho, beef or chicken noodle soup with fresh herbs. Banh mi, baguette sandwich with pork, pate and pickled vegetables. Bun cha, grilled pork with rice noodles, Hanoi style. Ca phe sua da, strong iced coffee with condensed milk.
