Nepal

Nepal Valleys and Peaks

Temple squares in the Kathmandu Valley, a week on the Annapurna trail and rhino spotting in Chitwan, for active travelers on a modest budget.

Length23 days
Total, flights included$1,840
All-in per day$80
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Nepal

    Kathmandu 3 nights · $69

    Wander the temple-filled Durbar squares, circle Boudhanath stupa with spinning prayer wheels and kit out for your trek in Thamel.

    Budget tip: Buy or rent trekking gear in Thamel instead of bringing it from home. Dal bhat comes with free refills, so one plate is a full meal.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 5 · Nepal

    Bhaktapur 2 nights · $68

    Brick squares, pagoda temples and pottery workshops fill this medieval Newar town, an easy day trip from Kathmandu.

    Budget tip: The entry fee is high but valid for a week if you show your passport. Try juju dhau (king curd) from a street shop.

    Getting here: about $3 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  3. Days 6 to 6 · Nepal

    Nagarkot 1 night · $24

    Hilltop village on the Kathmandu Valley rim with sunrise views over the Himalaya, from Langtang to Everest on clear mornings.

    Budget tip: Visit October to April when views are clearest. Walk down to Changu Narayan Temple and catch a bus back rather than hiring a car.

    Getting here: about $3 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  4. Days 7 to 8 · Nepal

    Bandipur 2 nights · $46

    A car-free Newari hill town of old brick houses with wide Himalayan views, halfway between Kathmandu and Pokhara.

    Budget tip: Get off the Kathmandu to Pokhara bus at Dumre and take a cheap local jeep up the hill.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  5. Days 9 to 11 · Nepal

    Pokhara 3 nights · $78

    Lakeside cafes, sunrise over the Annapurnas from Sarangkot and paragliding above Phewa Lake make this the easiest place to unwind after a trek.

    Budget tip: Tourist buses from Kathmandu are about $10. Rent a rowboat on the lake for a few dollars instead of a guided trip.

    Getting here: about $4 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  6. Days 12 to 18 · Nepal

    Annapurna Circuit 7 nights · $189

    Walk 12 to 18 days through Gurung villages and high desert, crossing the Thorong La pass at 5,416 m.

    Budget tip: Teahouse rooms are often free if you eat dinner and breakfast there. A licensed guide is officially required, though checks vary along the route.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  7. Days 19 to 21 · Nepal

    Chitwan National Park 3 nights · $108

    Go on jungle walks and dugout canoe trips past one-horned rhinos, crocodiles and, with luck, tracks of Bengal tigers.

    Budget tip: Stay in Sauraha's budget guesthouses and book jungle walks directly with licensed guides instead of through Kathmandu agencies.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  8. Days 22 to 23 · Nepal

    Patan 2 nights · $58

    Durbar Square crowded with Newar pagodas and stone temples, the excellent Patan Museum, metalworking workshops and the Golden Temple monastery courtyard.

    Budget tip: One ticket covers the square and museum; keep it for repeat entries. Stay overnight to see the temples quiet at dawn before tour groups arrive.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,150.
