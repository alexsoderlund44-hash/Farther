China

Yunnan and the Karst Country

Old towns, snow peaks and the Tiger Leaping Gorge hike in Yunnan, then bamboo rafting between the karst peaks of Yangshuo.

Length18 days
Total, flights included$2,028
All-in per day$113
Stops7

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · China

    Chengdu 3 nights · $111

    Giant pandas munching bamboo, bamboo-chair teahouses in People's Park and fiery, numbing Sichuan hotpot.

    Budget tip: Visit the panda base at opening time, when the pandas are most active. A cup of tea in People's Park costs little and lasts hours.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 6 · China

    Dali 3 nights · $111

    Stroll a walled old town of Bai architecture between shimmering Erhai Lake and the Cangshan mountains.

    Budget tip: Rent an e-bike to ride around Erhai Lake. Stay outside the old town walls for cheaper rooms.

    Getting here: about $90 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  3. Days 7 to 8 · China

    Lijiang 2 nights · $78

    Naxi canals, willow-lined stone lanes and wooden houses sit below the jagged peaks of Jade Dragon Snow Mountain.

    Budget tip: Stay in quieter Shuhe or Baisha villages nearby, where guesthouses are cheaper and calmer.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  4. Days 9 to 10 · China

    Tiger Leaping Gorge 2 nights · $78

    Hike the high trail above the roaring Jinsha River through one of the deepest gorges in the world.

    Budget tip: The classic 2-day hike has guesthouses along the way, so you only need a light pack. Start from Qiaotou.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  5. Days 11 to 12 · China

    Shangri-La 2 nights · $78

    Tibetan town in northwest Yunnan with Songzanlin Monastery, the rebuilt Dukezong old town and high grasslands above 3,000 m.

    Budget tip: Acclimatise for a day before hiking at this altitude; buses from Lijiang are far cheaper than private cars.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  6. Days 13 to 14 · China

    Guilin 2 nights · $74

    A riverside city surrounded by limestone hills and the gateway to the Longji rice terraces, the Dragon's Backbone.

    Budget tip: Stay a night in Ping'an village at Longji instead of a day trip, and catch the terraces at sunrise.

    Getting here: about $120 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  7. Days 15 to 18 · China

    Yangshuo 4 nights · $140

    Drift on a bamboo raft or cycle past rice paddies and water buffalo among the karst peaks of the Li and Yulong rivers.

    Budget tip: Cycle the Yulong River valley on a rented bike instead of joining a tour. Hostels in town are cheap.

    Getting here: about $5 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,100.
