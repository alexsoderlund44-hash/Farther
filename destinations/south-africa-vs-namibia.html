South Africa vs Namibia: which is cheaper?

South Africa is the cheaper of the two, at about $52 a day against $70 in Namibia, which is roughly 26% less each day. Two weeks costs about $728 in South Africa and $980 in Namibia, before flights. Both are at their best in October, so you could combine them on one trip.

South Africa

Hike Table Mountain in Cape Town, drive the Garden Route, climb into the Drakensberg and self-drive safari in Kruger. South Africa has great hostels and is easy on a budget.

Popular: Cape Town · Garden Route · Kruger National Park · Johannesburg · Drakensberg

Namibia

Red dunes at Sossusvlei, rhinos at Etosha's waterholes and long empty roads. Namibia is best seen on a budget by splitting a rental car and camping under huge desert skies.

Popular: Sossusvlei · Swakopmund · Etosha · Windhoek
