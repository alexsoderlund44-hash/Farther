Uganda · Rwanda

Uganda and Rwanda

Rafting the Nile at Jinja, crater lakes and chimpanzees around Fort Portal, Lake Bunyonyi and tidy, green Kigali.

Length17 days
Total, flights included$1,811
All-in per day$107
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 1 · Uganda

    Entebbe 1 night · $31

    Lakeside town by Lake Victoria with botanical gardens, the Uganda Wildlife Education Centre and boat trips to Ngamba Island chimpanzees.

    Budget tip: Stay here at the start or end of a trip instead of Kampala to avoid traffic. The botanical gardens make a cheap birding walk.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 2 to 4 · Uganda

    Jinja 3 nights · $111

    Big-volume white-water rafting on the Nile near its source, plus kayaking, river tubing and sunset cruises from riverside camps.

    Budget tip: Rafting trips usually include lunch and a free dorm night. Book directly with a Jinja operator rather than through an agent in Kampala.

    Getting here: about $6 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 5 to 5 · Uganda

    Kampala 1 night · $29

    A busy capital spread over hills, with bustling markets, lively nightlife and the Kasubi royal tombs of the Buganda kings.

    Budget tip: Boda-bodas are fast and cheap; book through an app and wear a helmet. Try a rolex, the street food chapati egg roll.

    Getting here: about $6 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 6 to 8 · Uganda

    Fort Portal crater lakes 3 nights · $81

    Swim in clear crater lakes ringed by tea plantations, then track chimpanzees in Kibale's forest just down the road.

    Budget tip: Stay at a lakeside camp near Kasenda. Walking or cycling between the crater lakes costs nothing and the views are excellent.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 9 to 9 · Uganda

    Kibale Forest 1 night · $68

    Rainforest park near Fort Portal famed for chimpanzee tracking, plus red colobus monkeys and the Bigodi wetland walk.

    Budget tip: Chimpanzee permits are expensive; book well ahead. The community-run Bigodi Wetland walk is cheap and great for primates and birds.

    Getting here: about $5 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 10 to 12 · Uganda

    Lake Bunyonyi 3 nights · $75

    A calm lake with 29 islands, steep terraced hills and dugout canoes gliding through the morning mist.

    Budget tip: Swimming is safe here, unlike many African lakes. Many lodges lend canoes for free, so paddle yourself between islands.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 13 to 15 · Rwanda

    Kigali 3 nights · $87

    The moving Kigali Genocide Memorial anchors a calm, spotless capital of green hills, craft coffee shops and lively Kimironko market.

    Budget tip: Moto taxis are cheap; drivers carry a spare helmet. Entry to the Genocide Memorial is free, though a donation is appreciated.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 16 to 17 · Rwanda

    Lake Kivu 2 nights · $62

    Swim, kayak and watch fishermen sing as they row out at dusk from Gisenyi and Kibuye, linked by the hilly Congo Nile Trail.

    Budget tip: Hike or bike sections of the Congo Nile Trail, sleeping in simple guesthouses along the way. Public buses link the lakeside towns cheaply.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,200.
