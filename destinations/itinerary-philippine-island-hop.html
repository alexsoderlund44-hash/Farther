Philippines

Philippine Island Hopping

Limestone lagoons in Palawan, sardine runs off Cebu, Bohol's hills and Siargao surf, a sea-heavy trip for swimmers, divers and beach readers.

Length22 days
Total, flights included$2,374
All-in per day$108
Stops9

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 1 · Philippines

    Manila 1 night · $27

    Walk the old walls of Intramuros, eat your way through Binondo, the world's oldest Chinatown, and dive into big-city nightlife.

    Budget tip: Ride jeepneys or use the LRT. Binondo food walks are cheap if done on your own.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  2. Days 2 to 2 · Philippines

    Puerto Princesa 1 night · $41

    Palawan's capital and base for the Underground River boat trip through a cave system, Honda Bay island-hopping and seafood at the Baywalk.

    Budget tip: Underground River permits are capped daily, so book a few days ahead; arranging your own van to Sabang costs less than a packaged tour.

    Getting here: about $60 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  3. Days 3 to 4 · Philippines

    Port Barton 2 nights · $70

    A quiet bay village between El Nido and Puerto Princesa, with sandy island beaches, sea turtles and only a few roads.

    Budget tip: Island-hopping tours cost about half of El Nido's. Beach huts and simple guesthouses are cheap.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  4. Days 5 to 8 · Philippines

    El Nido 4 nights · $176

    Island-hop through limestone lagoons and secret beaches in the Bacuit archipelago, snorkeling over coral between towering cliffs.

    Budget tip: Shared boat tours A to D have fixed prices. Take the multi-day expedition boat to Coron for island camping.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  5. Days 9 to 11 · Philippines

    Coron 3 nights · $132

    Swim in clear lagoons walled by limestone cliffs, like Kayangan Lake, and dive on Japanese shipwrecks from World War II.

    Budget tip: Join a shared island-hopping tour. Wreck dives are cheaper here than at most dive spots.

    Getting here: about $40 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  6. Days 12 to 12 · Philippines

    Cebu City 1 night · $27

    Magellan's Cross, the Basilica del Santo Niño, Fort San Pedro and famous lechon, plus the hillside Temple of Leah and Sirao flower farms.

    Budget tip: Use jeepneys and Grab around town; Ceres buses from the South Bus Terminal reach Moalboal and Oslob cheaply.

    Getting here: about $60 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  7. Days 13 to 15 · Philippines

    Moalboal 3 nights · $90

    Snorkel through a swirling sardine run just off the shore on Cebu, with sea turtles often cruising past the reef wall.

    Budget tip: You only need a mask and snorkel to see the sardines. Kawasan Falls is a short ride away.

    Getting here: about $5 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  8. Days 16 to 18 · Philippines

    Bohol 3 nights · $108

    Rolling Chocolate Hills, wide-eyed tarsiers and white sand beaches on Panglao make Bohol an easy island to love.

    Budget tip: Rent a scooter for the countryside loop instead of a van tour. Stay in Panglao hostels for cheaper beds.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  9. Days 19 to 22 · Philippines

    Siargao 4 nights · $136

    Surf the famous Cloud 9 break, swim in the rock pools at Magpupungko and island hop to sandbars and lagoons.

    Budget tip: Rent a scooter to explore the island. Surf lessons with a board cost about $10.

    Getting here: about $60 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,300.
