Getting around

Louages, shared minibuses that leave when full, are the fastest cheap way between towns, and SNCFT trains run south from Tunis toward Sousse, El Jem and Sfax. In cities, yellow taxis use meters, so ask the driver to switch it on. Ferries and short flights reach Djerba, and there is also a road causeway.

Where to sleep

Small hotels and family-run guesthouses are the usual budget beds, with traditional dar houses in the medinas and a few hostels in Tunis. Djerba has many beach hotels that drop prices outside summer.

What to eat

  • Brik: crisp pastry filled with egg, tuna and parsley
  • Lablabi: spicy chickpea soup poured over torn bread
  • Couscous: steamed semolina with spicy sauce, fish or lamb
  • Makroudh: semolina pastry stuffed with dates, soaked in syrup

Money

Bring cash and change it inside the country, since the dinar can't be taken in or out, and keep exchange receipts if you want to change leftovers back. Cards work in bigger hotels, haggling is normal in souks, and rounding up is a fine tip.

Phone data

Ooredoo, Orange and Tunisie Telecom sell SIMs at their shops with your passport, and coverage is good in towns with gaps in the deep desert.

Heads up

Harissa is in a lot of dishes, so ask for less if you don't like it spicy.

Tunisia travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Tunisia cost on a budget?

About $31 a day for one person, from $28 in Kairouan to $48 in Ksar Ghilane. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $217 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Tunisia?

March to May and October to November is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Tunisia?

Kairouan, at about $28 a day. The Great Mosque, one of North Africa's oldest, and a walled medina of carpet shops and honey-soaked pastry stalls.

How long do you need in Tunisia?

Tunis and Carthage, El Jem, the Saharan south around Tozeur, then Djerba. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in Tunisia?

One US dollar is about 2.97 Tunisian dinar (TND), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 59.33 TND, and a typical budget day of $31 comes to about 92.00 TND.

How do you get around Tunisia cheaply?

Louages, shared minibuses that leave when full, are the fastest cheap way between towns, and SNCFT trains run south from Tunis toward Sousse, El Jem and Sfax. In cities, yellow taxis use meters, so ask the driver to switch it on. Ferries and short flights reach Djerba, and there is also a road causeway.

What food should you try in Tunisia?
