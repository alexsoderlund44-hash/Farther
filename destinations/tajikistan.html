Mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. Cash machines and exchange desks pay a little less.

Getting around

Shared taxis run between Dushanbe and other towns, leaving when full. For the Pamir Highway, most travelers share a 4x4 with a driver, often found through guesthouses in Dushanbe or Khorog. Arrange the jeep a few days ahead to find others to split costs.

Where to sleep

Homestays are the norm along the Pamir Highway and in the Fann Mountains, with simple guesthouses in Dushanbe and Khorog.

What to eat

  • Qurutob: flatbread soaked in yogurt sauce with onion
  • Osh: rice pilaf with meat and carrots
  • Sambusa: baked pastry filled with meat
  • Green tea: served everywhere, often with bread and sweets

Money

Carry plenty of cash, since ATMs are scarce outside Dushanbe and Khorog and cards rarely work elsewhere. Tipping isn't expected, and haggling is normal for taxis and at bazaars.

Phone data

Tcell and Megafon SIMs need a passport, with coverage in towns but long gaps on the Pamir Highway.

Heads up

Passes on the Pamir Highway rise above 4,000 meters, so acclimatize and watch for altitude sickness.

Tajikistan travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Tajikistan cost on a budget?

About $33 a day for one person, from $23 in Istaravshan to $47 in Pamir Highway. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $231 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Tajikistan?

June to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Tajikistan?

Istaravshan, at about $23 a day. Wander old town lanes, watch knife-makers at work and browse a big covered bazaar in this ancient town on the Silk Road.

How long do you need in Tajikistan?

Dushanbe, the Fann Mountains, then the Pamir Highway east toward Kyrgyzstan. 2 to 3 weeks.

How much is $1 in Tajikistan?

One US dollar is about 9.22 Tajik somoni (TJS), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 184 TJS, and a typical budget day of $33 comes to about 300 TJS.

How do you get around Tajikistan cheaply?

Shared taxis run between Dushanbe and other towns, leaving when full. For the Pamir Highway, most travelers share a 4x4 with a driver, often found through guesthouses in Dushanbe or Khorog. Arrange the jeep a few days ahead to find others to split costs.

What food should you try in Tajikistan?

Qurutob, flatbread soaked in yogurt sauce with onion. Osh, rice pilaf with meat and carrots. Sambusa, baked pastry filled with meat. Green tea, served everywhere, often with bread and sweets.
