Ghana on a budget

Friendly, English-speaking and an easy start to West Africa, Ghana mixes Accra's markets and beach bars, the Cape Coast castles and the waterfalls and hills of the Volta Region.

Mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. Cash machines and exchange desks pay a little less.

Getting around

Tro-tros, shared minibuses, go almost everywhere cheaply, and STC and VIP coaches link the big cities. Uber, Bolt and Yango work in Accra and Kumasi. Shared taxis follow set routes in towns.

Where to sleep

Accra has hostels and guesthouses, and the coast around Cape Coast has beach lodges with cheap rooms and camping. The Volta region has simple guesthouses.

What to eat

  • Jollof rice: spicy tomato rice, a source of friendly rivalry
  • Waakye: rice and beans cooked together, with many toppings
  • Kelewele: spicy fried plantain cubes, a street snack
  • Banku: fermented corn dough, served with grilled tilapia

Money

Cash is used daily, ATMs are common in cities, and mobile money is very popular. Haggling is normal at markets, and tips are not required but appreciated.

Phone data

MTN, Telecel and AT SIMs need passport registration, and coverage is good in towns and along main roads.

Heads up

Use your right hand to eat, give and receive, as the left is seen as impolite.

Ghana travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Ghana cost on a budget?

About $30 a day for one person, from $24 in Larabanga to $43 in Mole National Park. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $210 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Ghana?

November to March is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Ghana?

Larabanga, at about $24 a day. Village with one of West Africa's oldest Sudanese-style mud mosques, whitewashed with wooden spikes, at the edge of Mole National Park.

How long do you need in Ghana?

Accra, Cape Coast, Kakum and the Volta region. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in Ghana?

One US dollar is about 11.62 Ghanaian cedi (GHS), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 232 GHS, and a typical budget day of $30 comes to about 350 GHS.

How do you get around Ghana cheaply?

Tro-tros, shared minibuses, go almost everywhere cheaply, and STC and VIP coaches link the big cities. Uber, Bolt and Yango work in Accra and Kumasi. Shared taxis follow set routes in towns.

What food should you try in Ghana?

Jollof rice, spicy tomato rice, a source of friendly rivalry. Waakye, rice and beans cooked together, with many toppings. Kelewele, spicy fried plantain cubes, a street snack. Banku, fermented corn dough, served with grilled tilapia.
