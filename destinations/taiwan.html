Getting around

The high-speed rail runs down the west coast, regular TRA trains and buses cover the rest, and an EasyCard works on transport and in convenience stores. Uber operates in the main cities. Book TRA trains on the east coast ahead for weekends and holidays.

Where to sleep

Hostels are cheap and well run in Taipei, Tainan and Kaohsiung, and family-run guesthouses called minsu are common in the countryside.

What to eat

  • Beef noodle soup: braised beef in rich broth with wheat noodles
  • Xiaolongbao: soup dumplings with pork filling
  • Gua bao: steamed bun with braised pork belly
  • Bubble tea: milk tea with chewy tapioca pearls

Money

Cards work in larger shops, but night markets and small eateries are cash only, and convenience store ATMs accept foreign cards. Tipping isn't expected, and haggling is rare.

Phone data

Chunghwa Telecom and other carriers sell tourist SIMs at the airport with your passport, coverage is excellent, and eSIMs are easy too.

Heads up

Eating and drinking are banned in metro stations and trains, and staff do enforce fines.

Taiwan travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Taiwan cost on a budget?

About $39 a day for one person, from $32 in Tainan to $46 in Green Island. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $273 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Taiwan?

March to May and October to November is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Taiwan?

Tainan, at about $32 a day. Taiwan's oldest city, with hundreds of incense-filled temples, Dutch-era forts and some of the island's most famous street snacks.

How long do you need in Taiwan?

Taipei, the east coast to Taroko, then down to Alishan and Tainan. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in Taiwan?

One US dollar is about 31.87 New Taiwan dollar (TWD), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 637 TWD, and a typical budget day of $39 comes to about 1,200 TWD.

How do you get around Taiwan cheaply?

The high-speed rail runs down the west coast, regular TRA trains and buses cover the rest, and an EasyCard works on transport and in convenience stores. Uber operates in the main cities. Book TRA trains on the east coast ahead for weekends and holidays.

What food should you try in Taiwan?

Beef noodle soup, braised beef in rich broth with wheat noodles. Xiaolongbao, soup dumplings with pork filling. Gua bao, steamed bun with braised pork belly. Bubble tea, milk tea with chewy tapioca pearls.
