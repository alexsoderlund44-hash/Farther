Getting around

Viazul tourist buses link Havana, Viñales, Trinidad and Cienfuegos, but shared colectivo taxis often cost about the same and are faster, and your casa host can book one. Fuel shortages can cancel buses, so confirm departures a day ahead. There is no international ride-hailing app, so agree taxi fares before getting in.

Where to sleep

Casas particulares (licensed rooms in family homes) are the standard budget bed, and hosts often serve big breakfasts and dinners for a fee.

What to eat

  • Ropa vieja: shredded beef stewed with peppers and tomato
  • Moros y cristianos: black beans and rice cooked together
  • Tostones: flattened, twice-fried green plantain
  • Café cubano: short, strong espresso sweetened while brewing

Money

Bring euros or US dollars in clean, undamaged notes, as cards issued by US banks don't work and ATMs are unreliable. The informal exchange rate is usually much better than the official one, and tipping is widely expected in a tight economy.

Phone data

ETECSA, the state carrier, sells tourist SIMs, but data is slow and power cuts can affect service, and many international eSIMs don't cover Cuba, so check before you go.

Heads up

Power and water cuts are common, so carry a power bank, a flashlight and any medicines you need, as pharmacies are often short.

Cuba travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Cuba cost on a budget?

About $49 a day for one person, from $40 in Remedios to $56 in Topes de Collantes. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $343 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Cuba?

November to April is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Cuba?

Remedios, at about $40 a day. Quiet colonial town with a gilded cathedral altar, famous for its noisy Parrandas festival of floats and fireworks each December.

How long do you need in Cuba?

Havana, Viñales, Trinidad and Cienfuegos. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in Cuba?

One US dollar is about 24.00 Cuban peso (CUP), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 480 CUP, and a typical budget day of $49 comes to about 1,200 CUP.

Fuel shortages can cancel buses, so confirm departures a day ahead. There is no international ride-hailing app, so agree taxi fares before getting in.
