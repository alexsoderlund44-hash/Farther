Brazil

Brazil Coast and Falls

Rio's beaches and viewpoints, the island trails of Ilha Grande, colonial Paraty, then the thundering Iguacu Falls.

Length16 days
Total, flights included$1,942
All-in per day$121
Stops5

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 5 · Brazil

    Rio de Janeiro 5 nights · $210

    Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, cable car views from Sugarloaf and the dawn hike up Morro Dois Irmãos above the city.

    Budget tip: Por quilo buffets (pay by weight) are the cheap way to eat. Monday night samba at Pedra do Sal is free.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 6 to 8 · Brazil

    Ilha Grande 3 nights · $132

    A car-free island where jungle trails lead to Lopes Mendes, one of Brazil's finest beaches, and cheap pousadas line Abraão.

    Budget tip: Ferries from Conceição de Jacareí are the cheapest. Walk the trails instead of paying for water taxis.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 9 to 11 · Brazil

    Paraty 3 nights · $120

    Whitewashed houses and colorful doors line cobbled streets that flood at high tide, with the jungle beaches of Trindade nearby.

    Budget tip: Take the local bus to Trindade instead of a boat tour. Cachaça tastings at the nearby distilleries are cheap.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 12 to 13 · Brazil

    São Paulo 2 nights · $82

    A giant city of food from everywhere, world-class museums, street art in Beco do Batman and the Japanese district of Liberdade.

    Budget tip: MASP is free on Tuesdays. Eat at por-quilo buffets, where you pay by the weight of your plate.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 14 to 16 · Brazil

    Iguaçu Falls 3 nights · $153

    Hundreds of waterfalls along a 3 km horseshoe, with walkways that end in the roaring spray of the Devil's Throat.

    Budget tip: Visit both the Brazilian and Argentine sides. City buses run to the park entrance for much less than a tour.

    Getting here: about $90 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,100.
