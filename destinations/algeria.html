Algeria on a budget

Africa's biggest country pairs Roman ruins like Timgad with the Sahara's wildest scenery, from Algiers' old casbah to the rock art and dunes of Tassili n'Ajjer, all with very few tourists.

Getting around

Algeria has long-distance buses and SNTF trains between northern cities, plus shared taxis that leave when full. Air Algerie flies to the far south, including Djanet. In Algiers and other big cities, the Algerian app Yassir works for rides.

Where to sleep

Mid-range and simple hotels are the main option, as hostels are rare. In the desert around Djanet, trips are usually run by local agencies, with nights in tents or under the stars.

What to eat

  • Couscous: steamed semolina with vegetables and meat, served on Fridays
  • Chorba frik: soup of cracked green wheat with lamb
  • Mhadjeb: thin folded flatbread stuffed with spicy tomato and onion
  • Garantita: baked chickpea flan sold as street food

Money

Algeria is mostly a cash economy, foreign cards rarely work, and many travelers bring euros to change. Haggling is light outside markets, and rounding up is enough for a tip.

Phone data

Mobilis, Djezzy and Ooredoo sell SIMs with your passport at official shops, and coverage is good in the north but thin in the Sahara.

Heads up

Trips into the deep south are usually arranged through a licensed local agency, so sort that out early in your planning.

Algeria travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Algeria cost on a budget?

About $55 a day for one person, from $39 in Constantine to $85 in Tamanrasset. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $385 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Algeria?

October to April is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Algeria?

Constantine, at about $39 a day. A dramatic city of suspension bridges spanning the deep Rhumel gorge, with cliffside walkways and an old medina.

How long do you need in Algeria?

Algiers, Constantine, Timgad, then fly south to Djanet. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in Algeria?

One US dollar is about 134 Algerian dinar (DZD), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 2,684 DZD, and a typical budget day of $55 comes to about 7,400 DZD.

How do you get around Algeria cheaply?

Algeria has long-distance buses and SNTF trains between northern cities, plus shared taxis that leave when full. Air Algerie flies to the far south, including Djanet. In Algiers and other big cities, the Algerian app Yassir works for rides.
