Where to go in October

Cheap places to travel in October

55 countries with good weather in October, cheapest first. Daily budgets are for one person in US dollars.

JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec

  1. India $23/day

    Overwhelming, endlessly varied and very cheap, from Rajasthan's forts to Himalayan valleys, with sleeper trains, street food and budget hostels linking it all.

    Cheapest stops: Amritsar ($14) · Pushkar ($17) · Varanasi ($18)

  2. Bolivia $25/day

    The cheapest country in South America, with high-altitude cities, the Uyuni salt flat, Amazon jungle and $2 market lunches.

    Cheapest stops: Copacabana ($20) · Sucre ($21) · Cochabamba ($21)

  3. Malawi $25/day

    The 'warm heart of Africa', with a freshwater lake as big as a sea, cheap lakeside lodges and few other travelers.

    Cheapest stops: Zomba Plateau ($21) · Nkhata Bay ($23) · Livingstonia ($23)

  4. Nepal $27/day

    The cheapest big-mountain trekking in the world, walking teahouse to teahouse under Himalayan peaks, with dal bhat refills and cheap guesthouses in Kathmandu and Pokhara.

    Cheapest stops: Lumbini ($19) · Tansen ($19) · Kathmandu ($23)

  5. Vietnam $29/day

    Steaming pho on every corner, sleeper buses running the length of the country and some of the cheapest hostels and homestays in Asia. Vietnam on a budget is easy.

    Cheapest stops: Quy Nhơn ($24) · Châu Đốc ($24) · Đà Lạt ($25)

  6. Uzbekistan $29/day

    Silk Road cities of blue-tiled domes and madrasas, connected by fast trains and fueled by plov, with family guesthouses and big breakfasts at low prices.

    Cheapest stops: Rishtan ($23) · Margilan ($25) · Kokand ($25)

  7. Armenia $29/day

    Ancient monasteries on cliff edges, wine caves and a café-filled capital with views of Mount Ararat, all at low prices and with very few crowds.

    Cheapest stops: Gyumri ($24) · Etchmiadzin ($25) · Dilijan ($26)

  8. Egypt $30/day

    Pharaonic temples, Red Sea reefs and remote desert oases, with cheap koshari, sleeper trains and some of the lowest prices around the Mediterranean.

    Cheapest stops: Nuweiba ($25) · Rosetta ($26) · Alexandria ($27)

  9. Morocco $31/day

    Labyrinthine medinas, Atlas mountain hikes and Saharan dunes within a few cheap bus rides of each other, with riad hostels and mint tea at every stop.

    Cheapest stops: Sidi Ifni ($27) · Chefchaouen ($28) · Tafraoute ($28)

  10. Tunisia $31/day

    Roman ruins, Saharan oases and blue-and-white villages, with cheap couscous, shared louages and far fewer tourists than Morocco.

    Cheapest stops: Kairouan ($28) · Mahdia ($28) · Sousse ($29)

  11. Madagascar $35/day

    Lemurs, baobabs and wildlife found nowhere else on Earth, reached by long, bumpy taxi-brousse rides and rewarded with cheap guesthouses and rice dishes.

    Cheapest stops: Antsirabe ($21) · Fianarantsoa ($22) · Ambositra ($22)

  12. Albania $36/day

    Europe's cheapest coastline and some of its wildest mountains, with Ottoman towns, byrek bakeries and family guesthouses that make Albania easy on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Korçë ($31) · Shkodër ($32) · Pogradec ($32)

  13. Azerbaijan $36/day

    Flame-shaped towers on the Caspian, bubbling mud volcanoes and Silk Road villages in the Caucasus, with cheap guesthouses and homestays.

    Cheapest stops: Ganja ($27) · Lankaran ($27) · Quba ($28)

  14. Lesotho $36/day

    Lesotho is a mountain kingdom entirely above 1,000 m, with pony treks, village homestays, the Sani Pass and huge waterfalls, all surprisingly cheap to explore.

    Cheapest stops: Thaba-Bosiu ($31) · Morija ($31) · Maseru ($34)

  15. Timor-Leste $37/day

    One of Asia's youngest and least-visited countries, with world-class coral reefs, mountain villages and quiet beaches free of crowds.

    Cheapest stops: Maubisse ($32) · Mount Ramelau ($34) · Baucau ($34)

  16. Kenya $37/day

    Classic safari country on a budget, with cheap camping safaris to the Maasai Mara, cycling among zebras at Hell's Gate and a laid-back Swahili coast in Lamu and Diani.

    Cheapest stops: Iten ($25) · Mombasa ($30) · Kakamega Forest ($31)

  17. Taiwan $39/day

    Night markets, marble gorges and mountain tea villages, all linked by spotless trains, with cheap street food and hostels that make Taiwan easy on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Tainan ($32) · Kaohsiung ($32) · Hualien ($33)

  18. Mexico $41/day

    World-class food at street prices, ancient pyramids, colonial towns and comfortable night buses everywhere, making Mexico one of the easiest countries to travel on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Cuetzalan ($34) · Mazunte ($35) · Campeche ($35)

  19. China $42/day

    The Great Wall, karst rivers, spicy street food and huge modern cities, linked by the world's biggest high-speed rail network and cheap hostels.

    Cheapest stops: Xiahe ($34) · Kashgar ($34) · Yangshuo ($35)

  20. Moldova $42/day

    Europe's least-visited country, with enormous underground wine cellars, cliffside cave monasteries and very low prices.

    Cheapest stops: Chișinău ($28) · Soroca ($29) · Bender ($29)

  21. Eritrea $43/day

    Eritrea preserves Asmara's 1930s Italian modernist architecture, frozen in time, with espresso cafés, art deco cinemas and a dramatic mountain road down to the Red Sea.

    Cheapest stops: Keren ($35) · Debre Bizen ($37) · Senafe ($38)

  22. Jordan $49/day

    Rose-red Petra, Mars-like Wadi Rum and a long-distance hiking trail that crosses the whole country, with falafel stands and Bedouin camps along the way.

    Cheapest stops: As-Salt ($33) · Little Petra (Siq al-Barid) ($36) · Karak ($37)

  23. Hungary $50/day

    Thermal baths, ruin bars and wine country, with cheap set lunches and some of the best-value cities in Central Europe.

    Cheapest stops: Pécs ($41) · Debrecen ($42) · Szeged ($42)

  24. Mauritius $50/day

    Mauritius is cheaper than its resort image, with turquoise lagoons, the Le Morne hike, rainforest trails in Black River Gorges and Port Louis street food like dholl puri.

    Cheapest stops: Curepipe ($40) · Mahébourg ($41) · Pamplemousses ($44)

  25. Montenegro $51/day

    A fjord-like bay, wild mountain canyons and Balkan prices just inland from the coast, with cheap private rooms, campsites and hearty mountain food.

    Cheapest stops: Ostrog Monastery ($33) · Stari Bar ($38) · Ulcinj ($39)

  26. South Africa $52/day

    Hike Table Mountain in Cape Town, drive the Garden Route, climb into the Drakensberg and self-drive safari in Kruger. South Africa has great hostels and is easy on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Coffee Bay ($41) · Port St Johns ($41) · Durban ($42)

  27. Samoa $52/day

    Open-sided beach fale huts with meals included, freshwater swimming holes and traditional Polynesian village life on two green islands.

    Cheapest stops: Apia ($46) · Manono Island ($48) · Savai'i ($50)

  28. Malta $53/day

    A tiny island nation of golden stone cities, prehistoric temples and clear-water swimming spots, easy to explore by bus.

    Cheapest stops: Marsaxlokk ($49) · Mellieħa ($49) · Birgu ($50)

  29. Turkey $54/day

    Bazaars and ferries in Istanbul, Cappadocia's fairy chimneys, a turquoise coast and Mesopotamian towns, with cheap street food and pensions throughout.

    Cheapest stops: Konya ($34) · Şanlıurfa ($35) · Lycian Way ($35)

  30. South Korea $54/day

    Royal palaces, mountain temples and sizzling street food, linked by fast KTX trains and great-value guesthouses that make South Korea easy on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Daegu ($38) · Suwon ($41) · Jeonju ($42)

  31. Mozambique $54/day

    Mozambique has long Indian Ocean beaches, whale sharks off Tofo, dhow trips to the Bazaruto islands and the historic coral-stone town of Ilha de Moçambique.

    Cheapest stops: Inhambane ($29) · Gurúè ($31) · Xai-Xai ($31)

  32. Turkmenistan $55/day

    A closed, eccentric country of white marble boulevards, Silk Road ruins and the Darvaza gas crater that has burned for decades.

    Cheapest stops: Kow Ata Underground Lake ($48) · Merv ($51) · Köneürgench ($51)

  33. Algeria $55/day

    Africa's biggest country pairs Roman ruins like Timgad with the Sahara's wildest scenery, from Algiers' old casbah to the rock art and dunes of Tassili n'Ajjer, all with very few tourists.

    Cheapest stops: Constantine ($39) · Tlemcen ($39) · Béjaïa ($39)

  34. Cyprus $57/day

    Beaches, Roman mosaics and painted mountain churches on a divided island with cheap buses and good-value food.

    Cheapest stops: Famagusta ($52) · Nicosia ($53) · Larnaca ($53)

  35. Greece $61/day

    Ancient ruins, slow island ferries and gyros for a few euros, with cheap guesthouses and quiet beaches once you leave the famous islands.

    Cheapest stops: Thessaloniki ($51) · Mount Olympus ($51) · Meteora ($56)

  36. Portugal $62/day

    Western Europe's best value on a budget: tiled Lisbon and Porto, Atlantic surf beaches, cheap wine and custard tarts, and friendly hostels in every town.

    Cheapest stops: Braga ($47) · Aveiro ($48) · Coimbra ($50)

  37. Zimbabwe $64/day

    Zimbabwe has the best views of Victoria Falls, the medieval stone city of Great Zimbabwe and some of Africa's finest walking safaris along the Zambezi at Mana Pools.

    Cheapest stops: Bvumba Mountains ($34) · Chinhoyi Caves ($34) · Bulawayo ($35)

  38. Croatia $65/day

    Island-hopping, old stone towns and the waterfall lakes of Plitvice, much cheaper outside July and August.

    Cheapest stops: Zagreb ($55) · Šibenik ($56) · Zadar ($58)

  39. Suriname $65/day

    A wooden Dutch colonial capital, Javanese warungs and some of the world's most intact rainforest along wide jungle rivers.

    Cheapest stops: Paramaribo ($41) · Commewijne ($45) · Jodensavanne ($50)

  40. Fiji $65/day

    Budget island resorts with meals included, kava ceremonies, friendly villages and coral reefs, especially out in the Yasawa Islands.

    Cheapest stops: Nadi ($38) · Suva ($41) · Levuka ($42)

  41. Japan $66/day

    Pricier than its neighbours, but convenience-store meals, capsule hostels and rail passes make Japan workable on a budget, from Tokyo to Kyoto's temples.

    Cheapest stops: Naha, Okinawa ($52) · Fukuoka ($53) · Nara ($57)

  42. Spain $68/day

    Cheap tapas, Moorish palaces and long walking routes like the Camino, with good-value hostels in every city from Barcelona to Seville.

    Cheapest stops: Camino de Santiago ($42) · Santiago de Compostela ($54) · Cuenca ($54)

  43. Namibia $70/day

    Red dunes at Sossusvlei, rhinos at Etosha's waterholes and long empty roads. Namibia is best seen on a budget by splitting a rental car and camping under huge desert skies.

    Cheapest stops: Windhoek ($38) · Swakopmund ($46) · Walvis Bay ($51)

  44. Vanuatu $72/day

    An active volcano you can stand on, swimming blue holes and WWII wrecks, spread across friendly Melanesian islands.

    Cheapest stops: Port Vila ($55) · Champagne Beach ($60) · Espiritu Santo ($63)

  45. France $73/day

    Expensive in Paris, much cheaper in the regions, with bakeries, cheese markets and set lunches that feed you well on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Cévennes ($61) · Toulouse ($63) · Marseille ($64)

  46. Chile $75/day

    From the Atacama desert's salt flats to Patagonia's granite towers. Pricier than its neighbors, but hostels and comfortable long-distance buses help.

    Cheapest stops: Putre ($39) · Arica ($39) · La Serena ($42)

  47. Australia $77/day

    Expensive, but hostels, campervans and working holiday visas make long trips possible, from Sydney's beaches to the Outback and the Reef.

    Cheapest stops: Blue Mountains ($57) · Adelaide ($59) · Melbourne ($61)

  48. Botswana $82/day

    Botswana is high-end safari country, but budget mokoro trips into the Okavango Delta, self-drive camping in Chobe and tours to the salt pans keep it within reach.

    Cheapest stops: Maun ($37) · Kasane ($41) · Gaborone ($41)

  49. Zambia $85/day

    Zambia offers wild walking safaris in South Luangwa and the other side of Victoria Falls at Livingstone, with rafting, Devil's Pool swims and cheap camps by the parks.

    Cheapest stops: Lusaka ($35) · Siavonga ($35) · Mpulungu ($38)

  50. Italy $87/day

    Art, ruins and food everywhere, more affordable in the south and outside peak summer, with hostels, trains and pizza that keep costs low.

    Cheapest stops: Naples ($58) · Assisi ($58) · Lecce ($59)

  51. United States $95/day

    Pricey cities balanced by world-class national parks. Road trips with campsites, motels and hostels make the US doable on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Las Vegas ($74) · Great Smoky Mountains ($76) · Big Bend ($77)

  52. Guyana $100/day

    English-speaking, little-visited and covered in rainforest, with Kaieteur Falls, savanna ranches and community-run eco-lodges.

    Cheapest stops: Georgetown ($54) · Bartica ($72) · Surama ($77)

  53. Seychelles $106/day

    The Seychelles has granite boulder beaches on remote islands. It is pricey, but self-catering guesthouses, local buses and ferries make Mahé, Praslin and La Digue doable on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Victoria ($75) · Mahé ($94) · La Digue ($101)

  54. Tanzania $190/day

    Climb Kilimanjaro, watch the Serengeti's great herds and wander Zanzibar's Stone Town. Park fees are the main cost, so shared camping safaris from Arusha keep Tanzania affordable.

    Cheapest stops: Arusha ($25) · Moshi ($26) · Bagamoyo ($27)

  55. Bhutan $230/day

    A Himalayan kingdom of cliffside monasteries and fortress dzongs, where a daily tourist fee makes short trips the budget option.

    Cheapest stops: Paro ($230) · Punakha ($230) · Tiger's Nest ($230)
