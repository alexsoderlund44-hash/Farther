Getting around

Trenitalia and Italo run fast trains between cities, while regional trains are cheaper and don't need booking. FlixBus and Itabus offer cheap long routes, and ride-hailing is limited, though FreeNow works with taxis. Book high-speed trains early for the best prices.

Where to sleep

Hostels are common in big cities, and agriturismi offer farm stays in the countryside. Book ahead in summer in Cinque Terre and Venice.

What to eat

  • Pizza napoletana: soft wood-fired pizza from Naples
  • Cacio e pepe: pasta with pecorino and black pepper
  • Arancini: fried rice balls stuffed with ragù or cheese
  • Gelato: dense Italian ice cream

Money

Cards are widely accepted, but carry some cash for small bars and markets. A cover charge (coperto) is common, and tipping is optional.

Phone data

TIM, Vodafone, WindTre and Iliad SIMs need passport registration, while EU visitors can roam at home rates; coverage is good across the country.

Heads up

Validate paper regional train tickets in the machines before boarding, or you can be fined.

Italy travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Italy cost on a budget?

About $87 a day for one person, from $58 in Naples to $105 in Capri. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $609 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Italy?

April to June and September to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Italy?

Naples, at about $58 a day. Pizza was born on these chaotic, washing-line streets, home to the treasure-packed National Archaeological Museum.

How long do you need in Italy?

Rome, Florence, Cinque Terre and Venice, or Naples and Sicily for less money. 2 to 3 weeks.

How much is $1 in Italy?

One US dollar is about 0.889 Euro (EUR), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 17.78 EUR, and a typical budget day of $87 comes to about 77.00 EUR.

How do you get around Italy cheaply?

Trenitalia and Italo run fast trains between cities, while regional trains are cheaper and don't need booking. FlixBus and Itabus offer cheap long routes, and ride-hailing is limited, though FreeNow works with taxis. Book high-speed trains early for the best prices.

What food should you try in Italy?

Pizza napoletana, soft wood-fired pizza from Naples. Cacio e pepe, pasta with pecorino and black pepper. Arancini, fried rice balls stuffed with ragù or cheese. Gelato, dense Italian ice cream.
