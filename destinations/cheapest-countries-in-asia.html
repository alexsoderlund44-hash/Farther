Ranked by daily cost

Cheapest countries in Asia

What a typical day costs one person on a budget in 2026, in US dollars: a dorm bed or simple room, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees. Countries under a travel warning are left out.

Whole worldAsiaEuropeAfricaAmericasMiddle EastOceania

  1. Bangladesh $22/day

    Very few tourists, river life everywhere, tea hills and the Sundarbans, the world's largest mangrove forest, all at rock-bottom prices.

    Best time: November to February. Cheapest stops: Puthia ($19) · Dhaka ($20) · Srimangal ($20)

  2. India $23/day

    Overwhelming, endlessly varied and very cheap, from Rajasthan's forts to Himalayan valleys, with sleeper trains, street food and budget hostels linking it all.

    Best time: October to March. Cheapest stops: Amritsar ($14) · Pushkar ($17) · Varanasi ($18)

  3. Kyrgyzstan $24/day

    Wild alpine lakes, nomad yurts, horse treks and free camping almost anywhere, with cheap guesthouses and some of the best hiking in Central Asia.

    Best time: June to September. Cheapest stops: Osh ($19) · Ala-Kul ($22) · Bishkek ($23)

  4. Malaysia $26/day

    Street-food capitals, cool tea highlands, one of the world's oldest rainforests and easy-going islands, all linked by cheap buses and hawker centres.

    Best time: March to September. Cheapest stops: Ipoh ($21) · Melaka ($23) · George Town, Penang ($26)

  5. Nepal $27/day

    The cheapest big-mountain trekking in the world, walking teahouse to teahouse under Himalayan peaks, with dal bhat refills and cheap guesthouses in Kathmandu and Pokhara.

    Best time: March to April and October to November. Cheapest stops: Lumbini ($19) · Tansen ($19) · Kathmandu ($23)

  6. Vietnam $29/day

    Steaming pho on every corner, sleeper buses running the length of the country and some of the cheapest hostels and homestays in Asia. Vietnam on a budget is easy.

    Best time: October to April. Cheapest stops: Quy Nhơn ($24) · Châu Đốc ($24) · Đà Lạt ($25)

  7. Laos $29/day

    Slow river journeys, quiet mountain towns, cheap guesthouses and the most laid-back pace in Southeast Asia. Laos rewards travelers who are in no hurry.

    Best time: November to March. Cheapest stops: Muang Ngoi Neua ($20) · Pakse ($23) · Nong Khiaw ($24)

  8. Cambodia $29/day

    Angkor's temples, a sobering modern history, riverside capitals and a sleepy south coast, all with cheap guesthouses and simple street food.

    Best time: November to March. Cheapest stops: Kampong Cham ($21) · Battambang ($22) · Kratie ($23)

  9. Uzbekistan $29/day

    Silk Road cities of blue-tiled domes and madrasas, connected by fast trains and fueled by plov, with family guesthouses and big breakfasts at low prices.

    Best time: April to May and September to October. Cheapest stops: Rishtan ($23) · Margilan ($25) · Kokand ($25)

  10. Sri Lanka $31/day

    Tea country train rides, ancient cities, surf beaches and wildlife on a compact island where cheap guesthouses and rice and curry are never far away.

    Best time: December to April. Cheapest stops: Jaffna ($26) · Ella ($27) · Adam's Peak ($27)

  11. Tajikistan $33/day

    The Pamir Highway, turquoise mountain lakes and homestay hospitality in one of the least-visited countries in Asia, where travel is cheap but rugged.

    Best time: June to September. Cheapest stops: Istaravshan ($23) · Dushanbe ($25) · Khujand ($25)

  12. Philippines $36/day

    Over 7,000 islands of limestone lagoons, surf breaks, white sand beaches and mountain rice terraces, with cheap island-hopping tours and homestays.

    Best time: December to May. Cheapest stops: Batad Rice Terraces ($27) · Siquijor ($27) · Vigan ($27)

  13. Indonesia $37/day

    Volcano hikes, ancient temples, rice terraces and island-hopping across thousands of islands, with cheap warungs and homestays almost everywhere you go.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Lake Toba ($22) · Bukittinggi ($23) · Yogyakarta ($25)

  14. Kazakhstan $37/day

    Modern Almaty backed by the snowy Tian Shan, with canyons, alpine lakes and cheap homestays a short drive away.

    Best time: May to September. Cheapest stops: Turkistan ($28) · Shymkent ($28) · Big Almaty Lake ($30)

  15. Timor-Leste $37/day

    One of Asia's youngest and least-visited countries, with world-class coral reefs, mountain villages and quiet beaches free of crowds.

    Best time: May to October. Cheapest stops: Maubisse ($32) · Mount Ramelau ($34) · Baucau ($34)

  16. Taiwan $39/day

    Night markets, marble gorges and mountain tea villages, all linked by spotless trains, with cheap street food and hostels that make Taiwan easy on a budget.

    Best time: March to May and October to November. Cheapest stops: Tainan ($32) · Kaohsiung ($32) · Hualien ($33)

  17. Thailand $40/day

    The classic first big trip on a budget: easy transport, cheap guesthouses and hostels, legendary street food, tropical islands and misty northern mountains.

    Best time: November to February. Cheapest stops: Lopburi ($28) · Chiang Rai ($29) · Phimai ($29)

  18. China $42/day

    The Great Wall, karst rivers, spicy street food and huge modern cities, linked by the world's biggest high-speed rail network and cheap hostels.

    Best time: April to May and September to October. Cheapest stops: Xiahe ($34) · Kashgar ($34) · Yangshuo ($35)

  19. Mongolia $43/day

    Endless steppe, nomad gers, horse treks and the Gobi Desert, best seen on a shared multi-day jeep tour that splits the cost of driver, food and fuel.

    Best time: June to September. Cheapest stops: Ulaanbaatar ($26) · Terelj National Park ($33) · Kharkhorin ($33)

  20. Singapore $53/day

    A pricey city-state where hawker centres, free gardens and a cheap MRT keep a short stopover on a budget surprisingly affordable.

    Best time: February to August. Cheapest stops: Little India, Singapore ($49) · Kampong Glam ($49) · MacRitchie Reservoir ($50)

  21. South Korea $54/day

    Royal palaces, mountain temples and sizzling street food, linked by fast KTX trains and great-value guesthouses that make South Korea easy on a budget.

    Best time: April to May and September to November. Cheapest stops: Daegu ($38) · Suwon ($41) · Jeonju ($42)

  22. Turkmenistan $55/day

    A closed, eccentric country of white marble boulevards, Silk Road ruins and the Darvaza gas crater that has burned for decades.

    Best time: April to May and September to October. Cheapest stops: Kow Ata Underground Lake ($48) · Merv ($51) · Köneürgench ($51)

  23. Japan $66/day

    Pricier than its neighbours, but convenience-store meals, capsule hostels and rail passes make Japan workable on a budget, from Tokyo to Kyoto's temples.

    Best time: March to May and October to November. Cheapest stops: Naha, Okinawa ($52) · Fukuoka ($53) · Nara ($57)

  24. Maldives $93/day

    Not just luxury resorts: local islands have cheap guesthouses, public ferries and the same turquoise water, making the Maldives possible on a budget.

    Best time: December to April. Cheapest stops: Malé ($55) · Thoddoo ($78) · Maafushi ($85)

  25. Bhutan $230/day

    A Himalayan kingdom of cliffside monasteries and fortress dzongs, where a daily tourist fee makes short trips the budget option.

    Best time: March to May and October to November. Cheapest stops: Paro ($230) · Punakha ($230) · Tiger's Nest ($230)
