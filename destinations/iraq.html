Mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. Cash machines and exchange desks pay a little less.

Getting around

Shared taxis leave from city garages when full and are the usual way between cities, and minibuses cover shorter routes. Careem has operated in Baghdad and Erbil, and regular taxis are cheap if you agree the fare first. Expect frequent checkpoints, especially between the Kurdistan Region and the rest of Iraq.

Where to sleep

Budget hotels in city centers are the usual option, as hostels are rare outside Erbil. In the Kurdish mountains, simple guesthouses and camping are possible in some valleys.

What to eat

  • Masgouf: butterflied carp slow-grilled beside an open fire
  • Kubba: fried or boiled bulgur shells stuffed with spiced meat
  • Dolma: vine leaves and vegetables stuffed with rice and meat
  • Kahi with geymar: flaky pastry with clotted cream and date syrup

Money

Iraq is largely a cash economy, and US dollars are widely accepted alongside dinars; ATMs exist in Erbil and Baghdad but don't always take foreign cards, so bring enough cash. Tipping is small and informal, and light haggling is normal in markets.

Phone data

Local SIMs from Asiacell, Zain or Korek (in Kurdistan) need your passport and are cheap, with good coverage in cities and weaker signal in remote areas.

Heads up

Carry your passport at all times for checkpoints, and don't photograph security forces or checkpoints.

Iraq travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Iraq cost on a budget?

About $42 a day for one person, from $25 in Najaf to $51 in Mesopotamian Marshes. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $294 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Iraq?

March to April and October to November is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Iraq?

Najaf, at about $25 a day. Shia pilgrimage city around the gold-domed Imam Ali Shrine, beside Wadi al-Salam, one of the largest cemeteries in the world.
