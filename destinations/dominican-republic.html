Getting around

Caribe Tours and Metro Tours run comfortable buses between major towns, and cheap guaguas (minibuses) cover shorter routes. Santo Domingo has a Metro, and Uber and inDrive work in the capital and Santiago. Motoconchos (motorbike taxis) are common in Las Terrenas, but agree the fare first.

Where to sleep

Santo Domingo's Zona Colonial has hostels and guesthouses, and Jarabacoa and Las Terrenas have small family hotels and cabañas.

What to eat

  • La bandera: rice, red beans and stewed meat, the lunch staple
  • Mangú: mashed green plantain with pickled onions, eaten at breakfast
  • Sancocho: thick stew of several meats and root vegetables
  • Morir soñando: orange juice mixed with milk and sugar

Money

Cards work in cities and resorts, but carry pesos for guaguas, colmados and small eateries. Restaurant bills include a 10% legal service charge, and an extra 10% for good service is customary.

Phone data

Claro and Altice SIMs are easy to buy with a passport, and coverage is good in towns and along the main roads.

Heads up

Don't drink tap water, and stick to bottled or filtered water, including for ice in small places.

Dominican Republic travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Dominican Republic cost on a budget?

About $45 a day for one person, from $36 in Constanza to $85 in Pico Duarte. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $315 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Dominican Republic?

December to April is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Dominican Republic?

Constanza, at about $36 a day. A high valley of strawberry farms and pine forest, the coolest spot in the Caribbean, with the tall Aguas Blancas waterfall.

How long do you need in Dominican Republic?

Santo Domingo, Jarabacoa, Samaná and Las Terrenas. 10 days.

How much is $1 in Dominican Republic?

One US dollar is about 60.16 Dominican peso (DOP), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 1,203 DOP, and a typical budget day of $45 comes to about 2,700 DOP.
