Mexico

Oaxaca and Chiapas

Markets and mezcal in Oaxaca, surf towns on the Pacific, highland San Cristobal and the jungle ruins of Palenque.

Length17 days
Total, flights included$1,369
All-in per day$81
Stops7

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Mexico

    Mexico City 3 nights · $129

    Graze at taco stands, spend a day in the National Anthropology Museum and climb the pyramids at Teotihuacán on a day trip.

    Budget tip: The metro costs 5 pesos a ride. Street tacos are far cheaper than sit-down restaurants and often better.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 4 · Mexico

    Puebla 1 night · $37

    Colonial center of Talavera tiled facades, home of mole poblano and chiles en nogada, with the giant pyramid of Cholula next door.

    Budget tip: Eat cemitas and chalupas at local markets like Mercado del Carmen; colectivos to Cholula cost little and the pyramid tunnels are cheap.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 5 to 8 · Mexico

    Oaxaca 4 nights · $164

    Mole, mezcal and buzzing markets in the city, plus day trips to the petrified waterfalls of Hierve el Agua and Monte Albán.

    Budget tip: Eat in the Mercado 20 de Noviembre grill hall. ADO night buses save a hostel night between cities.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 9 to 9 · Mexico

    Hierve el Agua 1 night · $40

    Mineral springs flowing over cliffs that look like frozen waterfalls, with infinity pools facing the mountains of the Oaxaca valley.

    Budget tip: Shared colectivos from Mitla are far cheaper than a tour; check the site is open before going, as local disputes sometimes close it.

    Getting here: about $6 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 10 to 12 · Mexico

    Mazunte 3 nights · $105

    A laid-back beach village with a sea turtle center, yoga shalas and sunsets watched from the rocky Punta Cometa headland.

    Budget tip: Walk the coast path to Zipolite and San Agustinillo. Simple cabañas with a hammock often cost less than hostel beds.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 13 to 15 · Mexico

    San Cristóbal de las Casas 3 nights · $108

    A cool highland town of colorful colonial streets, with indigenous Tzotzil villages and the cliffs of Sumidero Canyon nearby.

    Budget tip: Colectivos to nearby villages cost about $1. Bring layers, since nights get cold at this altitude.

    Getting here: about $35 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 16 to 17 · Mexico

    Palenque 2 nights · $82

    Maya temples rising from the jungle to the roar of howler monkeys, with the Misol-Ha and Agua Azul waterfalls a short ride away.

    Budget tip: Sleep in the jungle cabañas at El Panchán near the ruins rather than in town. Colectivos shuttle back and forth cheaply.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $600.
