Argentina

Argentina North and Wine

Tango nights in Buenos Aires, Iguazu Falls, the coloured hills of Salta and Humahuaca, wineries in Cafayate and Mendoza.

Length17 days
Total, flights included$2,287
All-in per day$135
Stops6

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 4 · Argentina

    Buenos Aires 4 nights · $200

    Browse the San Telmo Sunday market, watch tango in the street and eat late-night steak at neighborhood parrillas.

    Budget tip: Prices change fast with inflation. Check current exchange options, such as cards and transfer services, before you go.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 5 to 6 · Argentina

    Puerto Iguazú 2 nights · $146

    The Argentine side of the Iguazú falls, where catwalks lead right to the roaring edge of the Devil's Throat.

    Budget tip: Get your ticket stamped on the way out for half-price entry the next day. Buses from town to the park are cheap.

    Getting here: about $70 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 7 to 9 · Argentina

    Salta 3 nights · $126

    A colonial city with a museum of Inca child mummies found high in the Andes, and the best empanadas in the country.

    Budget tip: Share a rental car to loop through the Calchaquí valleys. Local peñas serve live folk music for the price of a drink.

    Getting here: about $90 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 10 to 11 · Argentina

    Quebrada de Humahuaca 2 nights · $80

    See the Hill of Seven Colours at Purmamarca and the striped peaks of the 14-coloured Hornocal range in the far north.

    Budget tip: Use local buses between Purmamarca, Tilcara and Humahuaca, and stay in cheap hostels in Tilcara.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 12 to 13 · Argentina

    Cafayate 2 nights · $86

    A small wine town of torrontés vineyards, with red rock gorges and amphitheaters in the Quebrada de las Conchas.

    Budget tip: Walk or bike to the wineries near town. Take the Salta bus and ask to be dropped in the gorge for a hike.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 14 to 17 · Argentina

    Mendoza 4 nights · $204

    Cycle between Malbec wineries in the sun with the snowy Andes rising behind the vineyards.

    Budget tip: Rent bikes in Maipú and visit 3 or 4 wineries in a day instead of paying for a tour.

    Getting here: about $60 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,200.
