Where to go in April

Cheap places to travel in April

56 countries with good weather in April, cheapest first. Daily budgets are for one person in US dollars.

JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec

  1. Bolivia $25/day

    The cheapest country in South America, with high-altitude cities, the Uyuni salt flat, Amazon jungle and $2 market lunches.

    Cheapest stops: Copacabana ($20) · Sucre ($21) · Cochabamba ($21)

  2. Malaysia $26/day

    Street-food capitals, cool tea highlands, one of the world's oldest rainforests and easy-going islands, all linked by cheap buses and hawker centres.

    Cheapest stops: Ipoh ($21) · Melaka ($23) · George Town, Penang ($26)

  3. Nepal $27/day

    The cheapest big-mountain trekking in the world, walking teahouse to teahouse under Himalayan peaks, with dal bhat refills and cheap guesthouses in Kathmandu and Pokhara.

    Cheapest stops: Lumbini ($19) · Tansen ($19) · Kathmandu ($23)

  4. Nicaragua $28/day

    Colonial cities, volcano adventures, a lake island and Caribbean beaches at Central America's lowest prices, with cheap hostels and street food.

    Cheapest stops: Matagalpa ($25) · Estelí ($25) · Granada ($26)

  5. Vietnam $29/day

    Steaming pho on every corner, sleeper buses running the length of the country and some of the cheapest hostels and homestays in Asia. Vietnam on a budget is easy.

    Cheapest stops: Quy Nhơn ($24) · Châu Đốc ($24) · Đà Lạt ($25)

  6. Uzbekistan $29/day

    Silk Road cities of blue-tiled domes and madrasas, connected by fast trains and fueled by plov, with family guesthouses and big breakfasts at low prices.

    Cheapest stops: Rishtan ($23) · Margilan ($25) · Kokand ($25)

  7. Egypt $30/day

    Pharaonic temples, Red Sea reefs and remote desert oases, with cheap koshari, sleeper trains and some of the lowest prices around the Mediterranean.

    Cheapest stops: Nuweiba ($25) · Rosetta ($26) · Alexandria ($27)

  8. Sri Lanka $31/day

    Tea country train rides, ancient cities, surf beaches and wildlife on a compact island where cheap guesthouses and rice and curry are never far away.

    Cheapest stops: Jaffna ($26) · Ella ($27) · Adam's Peak ($27)

  9. Morocco $31/day

    Labyrinthine medinas, Atlas mountain hikes and Saharan dunes within a few cheap bus rides of each other, with riad hostels and mint tea at every stop.

    Cheapest stops: Sidi Ifni ($27) · Chefchaouen ($28) · Tafraoute ($28)

  10. Tunisia $31/day

    Roman ruins, Saharan oases and blue-and-white villages, with cheap couscous, shared louages and far fewer tourists than Morocco.

    Cheapest stops: Kairouan ($28) · Mahdia ($28) · Sousse ($29)

  11. El Salvador $32/day

    Surf beaches, volcano hikes and pupusas on every corner, in a small, cheap country that is now much safer than its reputation.

    Cheapest stops: Perquín ($27) · Suchitoto ($29) · Ruta de las Flores ($30)

  12. Paraguay $32/day

    South America's least-visited country, with Jesuit mission ruins, tereré on every street corner and the wild Chaco, all very cheap.

    Cheapest stops: Asunción ($27) · Encarnación ($27) · Areguá ($27)

  13. Guatemala $33/day

    Smoking volcanoes, Mayan ruins and lake villages linked by colorful chicken buses, with cheap Spanish schools and hostels all along the way.

    Cheapest stops: Todos Santos Cuchumatán ($25) · Nebaj ($25) · Quetzaltenango (Xela) ($26)

  14. The Gambia $33/day

    The Gambia is a narrow river country of rich birdlife, Atlantic beaches and easygoing villages, with chimps and hippos upriver near Janjanbureh.

    Cheapest stops: Tanji ($28) · Kartong ($29) · Banjul ($32)

  15. Madagascar $35/day

    Lemurs, baobabs and wildlife found nowhere else on Earth, reached by long, bumpy taxi-brousse rides and rewarded with cheap guesthouses and rice dishes.

    Cheapest stops: Antsirabe ($21) · Fianarantsoa ($22) · Ambositra ($22)

  16. Eswatini $35/day

    Eswatini is a small kingdom of game reserves, mountain hikes and cultural festivals, easy to reach from South Africa and cheap to explore on foot or by bike.

    Cheapest stops: Manzini ($28) · Sibebe Rock ($31) · Ezulwini Valley ($33)

  17. Philippines $36/day

    Over 7,000 islands of limestone lagoons, surf breaks, white sand beaches and mountain rice terraces, with cheap island-hopping tours and homestays.

    Cheapest stops: Batad Rice Terraces ($27) · Siquijor ($27) · Vigan ($27)

  18. Azerbaijan $36/day

    Flame-shaped towers on the Caspian, bubbling mud volcanoes and Silk Road villages in the Caucasus, with cheap guesthouses and homestays.

    Cheapest stops: Ganja ($27) · Lankaran ($27) · Quba ($28)

  19. Senegal $36/day

    Senegal blends mbalax music, Atlantic surf, the island of Gorée and the colonial charm of Saint-Louis, with mangrove channels in the Sine-Saloum Delta to slow things down.

    Cheapest stops: Touba ($27) · Ziguinchor ($29) · Joal-Fadiouth ($30)

  20. Lesotho $36/day

    Lesotho is a mountain kingdom entirely above 1,000 m, with pony treks, village homestays, the Sani Pass and huge waterfalls, all surprisingly cheap to explore.

    Cheapest stops: Thaba-Bosiu ($31) · Morija ($31) · Maseru ($34)

  21. Taiwan $39/day

    Night markets, marble gorges and mountain tea villages, all linked by spotless trains, with cheap street food and hostels that make Taiwan easy on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Tainan ($32) · Kaohsiung ($32) · Hualien ($33)

  22. Sierra Leone $40/day

    Sierra Leone has empty white beaches, the Tacugama chimp sanctuary and quiet islands like Banana Islands, a destination rebuilding steadily and still refreshingly uncrowded.

    Cheapest stops: River Number Two ($35) · Freetown Peninsula ($37) · Banana Islands ($40)

  23. China $42/day

    The Great Wall, karst rivers, spicy street food and huge modern cities, linked by the world's biggest high-speed rail network and cheap hostels.

    Cheapest stops: Xiahe ($34) · Kashgar ($34) · Yangshuo ($35)

  24. Brazil $42/day

    Beaches, rainforest and waterfalls on a huge scale, with good-value hostels, pay-by-weight buffet restaurants and long-distance buses.

    Cheapest stops: Belém ($32) · São Luís ($32) · Olinda ($33)

  25. Cape Verde $45/day

    Cape Verde's volcanic islands offer mountain hikes on Santo Antão, a crater village on Fogo, soulful morna music in Mindelo and quiet Atlantic beaches.

    Cheapest stops: Tarrafal ($37) · Brava ($37) · Praia ($41)

  26. Dominican Republic $45/day

    Beyond the all-inclusive resorts, there's a colonial capital, cool mountain towns for hiking and rafting, and bays where humpback whales gather in winter.

    Cheapest stops: Constanza ($36) · Jarabacoa ($37) · Santo Domingo ($39)

  27. Panama $46/day

    Watch ships squeeze through the Panama Canal, drink highland coffee in Boquete and sail between the tiny islands of San Blas.

    Cheapest stops: Boquete ($37) · El Valle de Antón ($38) · Cerro Punta ($38)

  28. Jordan $49/day

    Rose-red Petra, Mars-like Wadi Rum and a long-distance hiking trail that crosses the whole country, with falafel stands and Bedouin camps along the way.

    Cheapest stops: As-Salt ($33) · Little Petra (Siq al-Barid) ($36) · Karak ($37)

  29. Honduras $49/day

    Cheap Caribbean diving and Mayan ruins, best kept to the well-traveled route between Copán and the Bay Islands. Check current travel advice before going.

    Cheapest stops: Comayagua ($26) · Gracias ($27) · Lago de Yojoa ($28)

  30. Cuba $49/day

    Classic cars, salsa and family-run casas particulares, in a country going through a hard economic time with frequent blackouts and shortages.

    Cheapest stops: Remedios ($40) · Gibara ($40) · Camagüey ($41)

  31. Hungary $50/day

    Thermal baths, ruin bars and wine country, with cheap set lunches and some of the best-value cities in Central Europe.

    Cheapest stops: Pécs ($41) · Debrecen ($42) · Szeged ($42)

  32. South Africa $52/day

    Hike Table Mountain in Cape Town, drive the Garden Route, climb into the Drakensberg and self-drive safari in Kruger. South Africa has great hostels and is easy on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Coffee Bay ($41) · Port St Johns ($41) · Durban ($42)

  33. Singapore $53/day

    A pricey city-state where hawker centres, free gardens and a cheap MRT keep a short stopover on a budget surprisingly affordable.

    Cheapest stops: Little India, Singapore ($49) · Kampong Glam ($49) · MacRitchie Reservoir ($50)

  34. Malta $53/day

    A tiny island nation of golden stone cities, prehistoric temples and clear-water swimming spots, easy to explore by bus.

    Cheapest stops: Marsaxlokk ($49) · Mellieħa ($49) · Birgu ($50)

  35. Turkey $54/day

    Bazaars and ferries in Istanbul, Cappadocia's fairy chimneys, a turquoise coast and Mesopotamian towns, with cheap street food and pensions throughout.

    Cheapest stops: Konya ($34) · Şanlıurfa ($35) · Lycian Way ($35)

  36. South Korea $54/day

    Royal palaces, mountain temples and sizzling street food, linked by fast KTX trains and great-value guesthouses that make South Korea easy on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Daegu ($38) · Suwon ($41) · Jeonju ($42)

  37. Turkmenistan $55/day

    A closed, eccentric country of white marble boulevards, Silk Road ruins and the Darvaza gas crater that has burned for decades.

    Cheapest stops: Kow Ata Underground Lake ($48) · Merv ($51) · Köneürgench ($51)

  38. Algeria $55/day

    Africa's biggest country pairs Roman ruins like Timgad with the Sahara's wildest scenery, from Algiers' old casbah to the rock art and dunes of Tassili n'Ajjer, all with very few tourists.

    Cheapest stops: Constantine ($39) · Tlemcen ($39) · Béjaïa ($39)

  39. Trinidad and Tobago $55/day

    Trinidad's Carnival and doubles street food, then Tobago's quiet reefs, rainforest and beaches, a Caribbean trip with more local life than resorts.

    Cheapest stops: Port of Spain ($50) · Maracas Bay ($51) · Pitch Lake ($54)

  40. Cyprus $57/day

    Beaches, Roman mosaics and painted mountain churches on a divided island with cheap buses and good-value food.

    Cheapest stops: Famagusta ($52) · Nicosia ($53) · Larnaca ($53)

  41. Jamaica $59/day

    Reggae, smoky jerk chicken, rainforest waterfalls and the coffee-growing Blue Mountains, with cheaper guesthouses away from the resort strips.

    Cheapest stops: Treasure Beach ($53) · Port Antonio ($54) · Cockpit Country ($54)

  42. Portugal $62/day

    Western Europe's best value on a budget: tiled Lisbon and Porto, Atlantic surf beaches, cheap wine and custard tarts, and friendly hostels in every town.

    Cheapest stops: Braga ($47) · Aveiro ($48) · Coimbra ($50)

  43. Belize $64/day

    Snorkel the reef off Caribbean cayes, see the Great Blue Hole and wade into Mayan caves. Pricier than its neighbors, but guesthouses help.

    Cheapest stops: Cockscomb Basin ($40) · Punta Gorda ($42) · Hopkins ($43)

  44. Costa Rica $64/day

    Sloths, toucans and rainforest everywhere, plus volcanoes and two coasts. It's the priciest country in Central America, so hostels and local sodas matter.

    Cheapest stops: Cahuita ($41) · San José ($43) · Puerto Viejo ($45)

  45. Belgium $65/day

    Medieval squares, frites, waffles and Trappist beer, all a short and cheap train ride apart.

    Cheapest stops: Liège ($55) · Namur ($58) · Leuven ($60)

  46. Japan $66/day

    Pricier than its neighbours, but convenience-store meals, capsule hostels and rail passes make Japan workable on a budget, from Tokyo to Kyoto's temples.

    Cheapest stops: Naha, Okinawa ($52) · Fukuoka ($53) · Nara ($57)

  47. Spain $68/day

    Cheap tapas, Moorish palaces and long walking routes like the Camino, with good-value hostels in every city from Barcelona to Seville.

    Cheapest stops: Camino de Santiago ($42) · Santiago de Compostela ($54) · Cuenca ($54)

  48. Chile $75/day

    From the Atacama desert's salt flats to Patagonia's granite towers. Pricier than its neighbors, but hostels and comfortable long-distance buses help.

    Cheapest stops: Putre ($39) · Arica ($39) · La Serena ($42)

  49. New Zealand $76/day

    Fjords, volcanoes and Great Walks, best seen by campervan with hostels and cheap Department of Conservation campsites in between.

    Cheapest stops: Napier ($56) · Christchurch ($58) · Dunedin ($58)

  50. Australia $77/day

    Expensive, but hostels, campervans and working holiday visas make long trips possible, from Sydney's beaches to the Outback and the Reef.

    Cheapest stops: Blue Mountains ($57) · Adelaide ($59) · Melbourne ($61)

  51. Netherlands $78/day

    Canals, bikes and windmills in a compact, flat country, easy to cover by train with a cheaper city base than Amsterdam.

    Cheapest stops: Groningen ($66) · Texel ($72) · Leiden ($72)

  52. Italy $87/day

    Art, ruins and food everywhere, more affordable in the south and outside peak summer, with hostels, trains and pizza that keep costs low.

    Cheapest stops: Naples ($58) · Assisi ($58) · Lecce ($59)

  53. Maldives $93/day

    Not just luxury resorts: local islands have cheap guesthouses, public ferries and the same turquoise water, making the Maldives possible on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Malé ($55) · Thoddoo ($78) · Maafushi ($85)

  54. United States $95/day

    Pricey cities balanced by world-class national parks. Road trips with campsites, motels and hostels make the US doable on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Las Vegas ($74) · Great Smoky Mountains ($76) · Big Bend ($77)

  55. Seychelles $106/day

    The Seychelles has granite boulder beaches on remote islands. It is pricey, but self-catering guesthouses, local buses and ferries make Mahé, Praslin and La Digue doable on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Victoria ($75) · Mahé ($94) · La Digue ($101)

  56. Bhutan $230/day

    A Himalayan kingdom of cliffside monasteries and fortress dzongs, where a daily tourist fee makes short trips the budget option.

    Cheapest stops: Paro ($230) · Punakha ($230) · Tiger's Nest ($230)
