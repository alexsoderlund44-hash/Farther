Getting around

KTX high-speed trains and express buses link the main cities, and a T-money card covers city buses and subways. Kakao T is the main taxi app, and Uber also works in Seoul. Book KTX seats ahead around the Chuseok and Lunar New Year holidays.

Where to sleep

Guesthouses and hostels are cheap in Seoul and Busan, and a jjimjilbang (bathhouse) with sleeping mats is a fun budget night.

What to eat

  • Bibimbap: rice bowl with vegetables, egg and chili paste
  • Tteokbokki: chewy rice cakes in spicy sauce
  • Kimchi jjigae: spicy stew of kimchi, tofu and pork
  • Korean fried chicken: double-fried, crispy, often glazed

Money

Cards are accepted almost everywhere, even for small amounts, but markets may prefer cash. Tipping isn't expected, and haggling is rare outside markets.

Phone data

Prepaid SIMs and eSIMs are sold at Incheon Airport with your passport, and coverage is excellent.

South Korea travel: quick answers

How much does a day in South Korea cost on a budget?

About $54 a day for one person, from $38 in Daegu to $60 in Ulleungdo. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $378 before flights.

When is the best time to visit South Korea?

April to May and September to November is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in South Korea?

Daegu, at about $38 a day. Inland city with Seomun market's night stalls, Donghwasa temple on Palgongsan, the Kim Gwang-seok mural street and grilled makchang.

How long do you need in South Korea?

Seoul, Gyeongju, Busan, then fly to Jeju. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in South Korea?

One US dollar is about 1,345 South Korean won (KRW), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 26,892 KRW, and a typical budget day of $54 comes to about 73,000 KRW.

How do you get around South Korea cheaply?

KTX high-speed trains and express buses link the main cities, and a T-money card covers city buses and subways. Kakao T is the main taxi app, and Uber also works in Seoul. Book KTX seats ahead around the Chuseok and Lunar New Year holidays.

What food should you try in South Korea?

Bibimbap, rice bowl with vegetables, egg and chili paste. Tteokbokki, chewy rice cakes in spicy sauce. Kimchi jjigae, spicy stew of kimchi, tofu and pork. Korean fried chicken, double-fried, crispy, often glazed.
