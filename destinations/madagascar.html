Getting around

Taxi-brousses, shared minibuses, cover the whole island but are slow and crowded, while private shuttle services like Cotisse cost more but are more comfortable. Roads in the south are rough, so plan long travel days. Domestic flights save time but are expensive and often rescheduled.

Where to sleep

Simple hotels and bungalows are the usual cheap beds, often family-run, and some national parks have campsites. Places near Andasibe and Isalo can fill up in the July to August high season.

What to eat

  • Romazava: beef and greens stew, often called the national dish
  • Ravitoto: pounded cassava leaves cooked with pork
  • Mofo gasy: small rice cakes sold hot at breakfast
  • Koba: sweet banana, peanut and rice paste wrapped in leaves

Money

Cash in ariary is needed almost everywhere, ATMs are found in larger towns, and euros are the easiest foreign currency to change. Cards rarely work outside a few hotels, light haggling is normal in markets, and guides expect a tip.

Phone data

Telma, Orange and Airtel SIMs are cheap and need your passport, with decent coverage in towns and along main roads but long dead zones in between.

Heads up

Hiring a local guide is required in most national parks, and malaria is a risk across much of the island, so ask a doctor about prevention.

Madagascar travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Madagascar cost on a budget?

About $35 a day for one person, from $21 in Antsirabe to $68 in Masoala National Park. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $245 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Madagascar?

April to May and September to November is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Madagascar?

Antsirabe, at about $21 a day. A highland spa town of colorful pousse-pousse rickshaws, gem workshops and volcanic crater lakes like Tritriva nearby.

How long do you need in Madagascar?

Antananarivo, east to Andasibe, then the long road south through Ranomafana and Isalo. 3 weeks.

How much is $1 in Madagascar?

One US dollar is about 4,459 Malagasy ariary (MGA), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 89,170 MGA, and a typical budget day of $35 comes to about 160,000 MGA.

How do you get around Madagascar cheaply?

Taxi-brousses, shared minibuses, cover the whole island but are slow and crowded, while private shuttle services like Cotisse cost more but are more comfortable. Roads in the south are rough, so plan long travel days. Domestic flights save time but are expensive and often rescheduled.

What food should you try in Madagascar?

Romazava, beef and greens stew, often called the national dish. Ravitoto, pounded cassava leaves cooked with pork. Mofo gasy, small rice cakes sold hot at breakfast. Koba, sweet banana, peanut and rice paste wrapped in leaves.
