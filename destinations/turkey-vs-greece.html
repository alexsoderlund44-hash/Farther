Turkey vs Greece: which is cheaper?

Turkey is the cheaper of the two, at about $54 a day against $61 in Greece, which is roughly 11% less each day. Two weeks costs about $756 in Turkey and $854 in Greece, before flights. Both are at their best in May to June and September to October, so you could combine them on one trip.
