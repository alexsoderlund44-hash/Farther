Getting around

Buses and minibuses link the main towns, including Maun and Kasane, but don't reach the parks. Most budget visitors join a mokoro trip into the Okavango Delta from Maun and a boat or game drive in Chobe from Kasane. Self-drive 4x4 trips need serious planning and campsite bookings well ahead.

Where to sleep

Camping is the cheapest way to stay, and lodges in Maun and Kasane often have campsites and simple rooms. Campsites inside parks are limited and book out months ahead.

What to eat

  • Seswaa: pounded slow-cooked beef, often served at celebrations
  • Bogobe: stiff sorghum or maize porridge
  • Magwinya: fried dough balls, a common breakfast
  • Morogo: wild spinach cooked with onion and tomato

Money

Cards work in Maun and Kasane, and ATMs are found in towns, but carry cash for small shops and tips. Tipping safari guides and polers is expected.

Phone data

Mascom, Orange and BTC SIMs need passport registration, and coverage is fine in towns but very limited in the Delta and parks.

Heads up

Many camps are unfenced and wildlife walks through at night, so stay near your tent after dark and listen to staff.

Botswana travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Botswana cost on a budget?

About $82 a day for one person, from $37 in Maun to $122 in Moremi Game Reserve. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $574 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Botswana?

May to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Botswana?

Maun, at about $37 a day. The dusty gateway town to the Okavango Delta, where mokoro canoe trips and scenic flights begin.

How long do you need in Botswana?

Maun and the Okavango Delta, then Chobe. 10 days.

How much is $1 in Botswana?

One US dollar is about 13.99 Botswana pula (BWP), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 280 BWP, and a typical budget day of $82 comes to about 1,100 BWP.

How do you get around Botswana cheaply?

Buses and minibuses link the main towns, including Maun and Kasane, but don't reach the parks. Most budget visitors join a mokoro trip into the Okavango Delta from Maun and a boat or game drive in Chobe from Kasane. Self-drive 4x4 trips need serious planning and campsite bookings well ahead.

What food should you try in Botswana?

Seswaa, pounded slow-cooked beef, often served at celebrations. Bogobe, stiff sorghum or maize porridge. Magwinya, fried dough balls, a common breakfast. Morogo, wild spinach cooked with onion and tomato.
