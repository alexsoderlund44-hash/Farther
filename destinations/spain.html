Getting around

High-speed trains from Renfe, Ouigo and Iryo link the big cities, and Alsa buses cover smaller towns cheaply. Uber, Cabify and Bolt work in the major cities, and BlaBlaCar is popular for rides. Book trains early for the cheapest fares, especially on weekends.

Where to sleep

Hostels are common in big cities and many offer private rooms, and pensiones are cheap small hotels. Book ahead in summer and during festivals.

What to eat

  • Tortilla española: thick potato and egg omelet
  • Patatas bravas: fried potatoes with spicy sauce
  • Churros con chocolate: fried dough sticks dipped in thick chocolate
  • Gazpacho: cold tomato and pepper soup, popular in summer

Money

Cards and contactless are accepted almost everywhere. Use bank ATMs and decline conversion offers; tipping is optional, with small coins or rounding up common.

Phone data

Movistar, Vodafone and Orange SIMs need passport registration, while EU visitors can roam at home rates; coverage is good across the country.

Heads up

Pickpocketing is common in Barcelona and Madrid tourist areas, so keep valuables in a front pocket.

Spain travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Spain cost on a budget?

About $68 a day for one person, from $42 in Camino de Santiago to $83 in San Sebastián. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $476 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Spain?

April to June and September to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Spain?

Camino de Santiago, at about $42 a day. Walk the Camino de Santiago, a 30-day pilgrimage across northern Spain, or just the last 100 km into Santiago de Compostela.

How long do you need in Spain?

Barcelona, Madrid, then Andalucía: Seville, Granada and Córdoba. 2 to 3 weeks.

How much is $1 in Spain?

One US dollar is about 0.889 Euro (EUR), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 17.78 EUR, and a typical budget day of $68 comes to about 60.00 EUR.

High-speed trains from Renfe, Ouigo and Iryo link the big cities, and Alsa buses cover smaller towns cheaply. Uber, Cabify and Bolt work in the major cities, and BlaBlaCar is popular for rides. Book trains early for the cheapest fares, especially on weekends.
