Getting around

Chile's long-distance buses are some of the best in the region, with cama and semi-cama seats on overnight runs. Budget airlines JetSMART and Sky are often the cheapest way to San Pedro de Atacama (via Calama) and Patagonia, and Uber, DiDi and Cabify work in the cities. Book Torres del Paine refugios or campsites months ahead for the W Trek in summer.

Where to sleep

Hostel dorms are common in Santiago, Valparaíso and San Pedro, and Torres del Paine has refugios and campsites that must be booked in advance.

What to eat

  • Empanada de pino: baked pastry with beef, onion, egg and olive
  • Completo: hot dog piled with avocado, tomato and mayonnaise
  • Pastel de choclo: baked corn pie over a meat filling
  • Mote con huesillo: cold peach nectar with wheat and dried peaches

Money

Cards are accepted almost everywhere, and many ATMs charge a fixed fee per withdrawal. Restaurants usually suggest a 10% tip on the bill, which is voluntary.

Phone data

Entel, Movistar and WOM SIMs are easy to buy, and coverage is good in towns but thin in the Atacama desert and much of Torres del Paine.

Heads up

Phone snatching is common in central Santiago and Valparaíso, so keep your phone away when walking.

Chile travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Chile cost on a budget?

About $75 a day for one person, from $39 in Putre to $115 in Rapa Nui. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $525 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Chile?

October to April is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Chile?

Putre, at about $39 a day. An Aymara village at 3,500 m near Lauca National Park, with flamingos, vicuñas and Lake Chungará beneath Parinacota volcano.

How long do you need in Chile?

Santiago, Valparaíso, San Pedro de Atacama, then fly to Torres del Paine. 3 weeks.
