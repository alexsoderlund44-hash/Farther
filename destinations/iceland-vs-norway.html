Iceland vs Norway: which is cheaper?

Norway is the cheaper of the two, at about $94 a day against $115 in Iceland, which is roughly 18% less each day. Two weeks costs about $1,316 in Norway and $1,610 in Iceland, before flights. Both are at their best in June to August, so you could combine them on one trip.
