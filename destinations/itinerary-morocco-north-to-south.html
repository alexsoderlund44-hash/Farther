Morocco

Morocco Cities and Sahara

Blue mountain towns, the Fes medina, a night in the dunes and the Atlantic breeze in Essaouira, an easy first taste of Africa.

Length19 days
Total, flights included$1,329
All-in per day$70
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 2 · Morocco

    Tangier 2 nights · $64

    Mint tea in old literary cafés, a hillside kasbah and clear views across the Strait of Gibraltar to Spain from this storied port.

    Budget tip: Book the Al Boraq high-speed train to Casablanca early for cheap fares. Ferries from Tarifa take an hour, an easy way in from Spain.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 3 to 5 · Morocco

    Chefchaouen 3 nights · $84

    Wander the blue-washed lanes of a Rif mountain town, then day hike to the pools of the Akchour waterfalls.

    Budget tip: CTM buses are reliable and cheap. Book a day ahead in high season, since seats sell out.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 6 to 8 · Morocco

    Fes 3 nights · $93

    Get lost in the world's largest car-free medina, watching dyers at the Chouara tanneries and slipping into tiled medieval madrasas like Bou Inania.

    Budget tip: Pay a licensed guide for half a day to learn the medina's layout, then explore alone. A bowl of bissara soup costs under $1 at breakfast stalls.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 9 to 10 · Morocco

    Merzouga 2 nights · $118

    Ride a camel into the towering Erg Chebbi dunes at sunset and spend a night in a Saharan desert camp under the stars.

    Budget tip: Book the camp directly in Merzouga rather than a tour from Marrakech, which adds long drive days and markup.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 11 to 12 · Morocco

    Todra Gorge 2 nights · $58

    Hike between 300-metre canyon walls, try rock climbing and visit Berber villages near the Dadès valley.

    Budget tip: Guesthouses in the gorge include dinner. Walk the loop over to the nomad plateau for free views.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 13 to 13 · Morocco

    Aït Benhaddou 1 night · $34

    A fortified earth ksar climbing a hillside above a riverbed, famous from Gladiator and Game of Thrones and glowing gold at dusk.

    Budget tip: Stay overnight in a village guesthouse to see it at dawn before tour groups arrive. Ouarzazate is 30 km away by cheap grand taxi.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 14 to 16 · Morocco

    Marrakech 3 nights · $99

    Sleep in a riad hostel inside the medina, then eat grilled meats and snail soup at the night food stalls on Jemaa el-Fnaa.

    Budget tip: Agree prices before you sit down at a food stall. A bowl of harira soup is about $0.50.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 17 to 19 · Morocco

    Essaouira 3 nights · $90

    A windswept Atlantic port with blue fishing boats, a relaxed medina and grilled sardines straight from the harbor.

    Budget tip: Buy fish at the port and have it grilled at the stalls for a few dollars, cheaper than any restaurant.

    Getting here: about $9 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $600.
