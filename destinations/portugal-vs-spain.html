Portugal vs Spain: which is cheaper?

Portugal is the cheaper of the two, at about $62 a day against $68 in Spain, which is roughly 9% less each day. Two weeks costs about $868 in Portugal and $952 in Spain, before flights. Both are at their best in April to June and September to October, so you could combine them on one trip.
