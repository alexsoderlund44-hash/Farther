A typical day here (about $22) is roughly 2,700 BDT.

Mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. Cash machines and exchange desks pay a little less.

Getting around

Buses and trains link the main cities, and river launches are a slow, cheap way to travel in the south. Uber and Pathao work in Dhaka. Buy train tickets ahead through the official railway app, since seats go fast.

Where to sleep

Budget hotels and guesthouses are the norm, with few hostels; Srimangal has simple tea-country resorts and the Sundarbans are visited on boat trips that include beds.

What to eat

  • Kacchi biryani: mutton and rice slow-cooked together
  • Fuchka: crisp shells filled with spiced tamarind water
  • Ilish curry: hilsa fish in mustard sauce
  • Bhorta: mashed vegetables or fish with mustard oil and chili

Money

Bangladesh is mostly cash, ATMs are common in cities, and cards work only in bigger hotels. Agree on rickshaw and CNG fares before you get in, and small tips are welcome.

Phone data

Grameenphone, Robi and Banglalink SIMs need biometric registration with your passport, and coverage is good in most areas.

Heads up

Dress modestly, especially outside Dhaka, and expect a lot of friendly attention.

Bangladesh travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Bangladesh cost on a budget?

About $22 a day for one person, from $19 in Puthia to $54 in Sundarbans. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $154 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Bangladesh?

November to February is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Bangladesh?

Puthia, at about $19 a day. Village near Rajshahi crowded with terracotta-covered Hindu temples, a pond-side Shiva temple and the faded palace of the Puthia rajas.

How long do you need in Bangladesh?

Dhaka, Srimangal's tea hills, then a boat trip in the Sundarbans. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in Bangladesh?

One US dollar is about 123 Bangladeshi taka (BDT), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 2,463 BDT, and a typical budget day of $22 comes to about 2,700 BDT.

How do you get around Bangladesh cheaply?

Buses and trains link the main cities, and river launches are a slow, cheap way to travel in the south. Uber and Pathao work in Dhaka. Buy train tickets ahead through the official railway app, since seats go fast.

What food should you try in Bangladesh?

Kacchi biryani, mutton and rice slow-cooked together. Fuchka, crisp shells filled with spiced tamarind water. Ilish curry, hilsa fish in mustard sauce. Bhorta, mashed vegetables or fish with mustard oil and chili.
