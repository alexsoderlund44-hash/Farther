Getting around

CFR trains are slow but cheap and cover most of the country, and maxi-taxis (minibuses) fill the gaps. Bolt and Uber work in Bucharest and other big cities. Book sleeper trains to Maramureș ahead, and in Bucharest use apps rather than street taxis.

Where to sleep

Hostels are common in Bucharest, Brașov and Sibiu, and guesthouses (pensiune) are the cheap option in the countryside. Rooms are easy to find most of the year, but book ahead around Bran in summer.

What to eat

  • Sarmale: cabbage rolls stuffed with pork and rice
  • Mici: grilled skinless sausages with mustard
  • Ciorbă de burtă: sour tripe soup with sour cream and garlic
  • Papanași: fried cheese doughnuts with sour cream and jam

Money

Cards and contactless are widely used in cities, but bring lei in cash for villages and small guesthouses. Avoid Euronet ATMs and choose to be charged in lei; tipping 10 percent in restaurants is normal.

Phone data

Orange, Vodafone and Digi SIMs are cheap and easy to buy, and coverage is good across the country.

Heads up

Stray and shepherd dogs are common in rural areas and on mountain trails, so keep calm, keep your distance and don't run.

Romania travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Romania cost on a budget?

About $37 a day for one person, from $34 in Timișoara to $45 in Bran Castle. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $259 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Romania?

May to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Romania?

Timișoara, at about $34 a day. Square-lined baroque centre where the 1989 revolution began, an Orthodox cathedral with patterned roof tiles and lively Union Square cafés.

How long do you need in Romania?

Bucharest, Brașov and Bran, Sibiu, then north to Maramureș. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in Romania?

One US dollar is about 4.75 Romanian leu (RON), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 95.09 RON, and a typical budget day of $37 comes to about 180 RON.

How do you get around Romania cheaply?

CFR trains are slow but cheap and cover most of the country, and maxi-taxis (minibuses) fill the gaps. Bolt and Uber work in Bucharest and other big cities. Book sleeper trains to Maramureș ahead, and in Bucharest use apps rather than street taxis.

What food should you try in Romania?

Sarmale, cabbage rolls stuffed with pork and rice. Mici, grilled skinless sausages with mustard. Ciorbă de burtă, sour tripe soup with sour cream and garlic. Papanași, fried cheese doughnuts with sour cream and jam.
