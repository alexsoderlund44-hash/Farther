Tanzania vs South Africa: which is cheaper?

South Africa is the cheaper of the two, at about $52 a day against $190 in Tanzania, which is roughly 73% less each day. Two weeks costs about $728 in South Africa and $2,660 in Tanzania, before flights. Both are at their best in January to February and October, so you could combine them on one trip.
