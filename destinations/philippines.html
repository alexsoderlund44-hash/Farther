Getting around

Domestic flights on Cebu Pacific and Philippine Airlines are the quickest way between islands, with ferries and outrigger boats for shorter crossings and jeepneys and buses on land. Grab works in Manila, Cebu and other cities. Book flights and popular ferries ahead in high season and keep plans flexible in typhoon season.

Where to sleep

Hostels and guesthouses are easy to find in Palawan, Siargao and Cebu; book ahead on small islands in the dry season.

What to eat

  • Adobo: meat braised in vinegar, soy sauce and garlic
  • Sinigang: sour tamarind soup with pork or fish
  • Lechon: whole roast pig with crackling skin
  • Halo-halo: shaved ice dessert with beans, fruit and milk

Money

Carry cash, especially on smaller islands like Siquijor where ATMs are few and can run dry. Tipping is optional, and haggling is fine at markets and with tricycle drivers.

Phone data

Globe and Smart SIMs must be registered with your passport, and coverage is good in cities but weak on remote islands.

Heads up

Typhoons between roughly June and November can cancel ferries and flights, so leave spare days in your plans.

Philippines travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Philippines cost on a budget?

About $36 a day for one person, from $27 in Batad Rice Terraces to $51 in Malapascua. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $252 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Philippines?

December to May is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Philippines?

Batad Rice Terraces, at about $27 a day. A 2,000-year-old amphitheatre of rice terraces carved into the mountains and reached only on foot.

How long do you need in Philippines?

Fly into Manila, north to Batad, then island-hop Palawan, Siquijor and Siargao. 3 to 4 weeks.

How much is $1 in Philippines?

One US dollar is about 62.63 Philippine peso (PHP), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 1,253 PHP, and a typical budget day of $36 comes to about 2,300 PHP.
