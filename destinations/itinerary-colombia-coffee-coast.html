Colombia

Colombia Coffee and Coast

Andean colonial towns, Medellin, the coffee hills and the Caribbean coast, a friendly first trip to South America at fair prices.

Length22 days
Total, flights included$1,582
All-in per day$72
Stops9

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Colombia

    Bogotá 3 nights · $96

    Wander La Candelaria's colonial streets and graffiti walls, gaze at the treasures of the Gold Museum and ride up Monserrate for city views.

    Budget tip: The Gold Museum is free on Sundays. Free walking tours leave daily from La Candelaria, where most of the cheap hostels are.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 5 · Colombia

    Villa de Leyva 2 nights · $64

    Whitewashed houses and wooden balconies around one of the largest cobbled plazas in South America, with fossils in the surrounding desert hills.

    Budget tip: Rent a bike to reach the fossil museum and the Pozos Azules. Weekdays are quieter and cheaper than weekends.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 6 to 9 · Colombia

    Medellín 4 nights · $140

    Ride the Metrocable over hillside barrios and join a guided Comuna 13 graffiti walk to hear how the city transformed.

    Budget tip: One cheap ticket covers metro and metrocable. A bandeja paisa fills you up for the whole day.

    Getting here: about $35 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 10 to 10 · Colombia

    Guatapé 1 night · $34

    Climb 740 steps up El Peñol rock for a view over a maze of blue lakes, then wander the brightly painted town below.

    Budget tip: Buses leave Medellín's north terminal every half hour, so it is an easy and cheap day trip from Medellín.

    Getting here: about $5 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 11 to 12 · Colombia

    Jardín 2 nights · $62

    Painted balconies, coffee farms and a homemade cable car, with a hike to the Cueva del Esplendor waterfall pouring through a cave roof.

    Budget tip: Hike to the cave waterfall instead of paying for horses. Buses come from Medellín in about three hours.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 13 to 15 · Colombia

    Salento 3 nights · $96

    Hike among towering wax palms in the Cocora valley and tour coffee farms in the green hills of the coffee region.

    Budget tip: Willys jeeps to Cocora leave from the main square and cost about $2 each way.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 16 to 18 · Colombia

    Cartagena 3 nights · $126

    Wander the walled old city's colorful balconies, find street art in Getsemaní and take boat day trips to the Rosario islands.

    Budget tip: Stay in Getsemaní for cheaper hostels. Book island boats directly at the dock instead of through street touts.

    Getting here: about $70 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 19 to 20 · Colombia

    Minca 2 nights · $60

    Hammock hostels above the clouds, coffee farms and jungle waterfalls in the Sierra Nevada foothills near Santa Marta.

    Budget tip: Moto-taxis run from Santa Marta for a cheap ride up. Bring cash, as there are no reliable ATMs.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  9. Days 21 to 22 · Colombia

    Palomino 2 nights · $66

    Relaxed Caribbean village where you float down a jungle river on an inner tube to the sea, with Sierra Nevada peaks behind.

    Budget tip: Rent a tube for the river trip from locals and walk the trail up yourself instead of booking a guided package.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $650.
