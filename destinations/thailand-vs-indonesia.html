Thailand vs Indonesia: which is cheaper?

Thailand and Indonesia cost about the same: roughly $40 a day in Thailand and $37 in Indonesia. Pick on season and what you want to see. Their best seasons don't overlap: Thailand is best in November to February and Indonesia in May to September.
