Germany · Czechia · Austria · Slovakia · Hungary · Poland

Central Europe by Train

Berlin to Krakow on comfortable trains, with castle towns, coffee houses and thermal baths, a smooth trip for first visits to Europe.

Length21 days
Total, flights included$2,082
All-in per day$99
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Germany

    Berlin 3 nights · $186

    Trace the Berlin Wall at the East Side Gallery, spend a day on Museum Island and dive into a nightlife scene that runs until Monday.

    Budget tip: Döner kebabs and Späti corner shops keep food cheap. Many Wall memorials and free walking tours cost nothing.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 5 · Germany

    Dresden 2 nights · $112

    Baroque landmarks rebuilt after WWII, including the domed Frauenkirche, line the Elbe alongside superb art galleries.

    Budget tip: Cheaper than Munich. Combine it with hiking in Saxon Switzerland, an easy day trip by train.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 6 to 9 · Czechia

    Prague 4 nights · $208

    Cross Charles Bridge at dawn before the crowds, climb to Prague Castle and share long tables in old beer halls.

    Budget tip: Eat a lunch menu (polední menu) and avoid Old Town Square restaurants. A pint of Czech lager is often cheaper than water.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 10 to 11 · Czechia

    Český Krumlov 2 nights · $102

    A storybook castle town wrapped around a bend of the Vltava, with painted towers, cobbled lanes and riverside pubs.

    Budget tip: Float the Vltava by canoe or raft. Come as an overnight stay rather than a day trip, when the lanes empty out.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 12 to 14 · Austria

    Vienna 3 nights · $201

    Imperial palaces like Schönbrunn, grand coffee houses serving Sachertorte and cheap standing tickets at the State Opera.

    Budget tip: Opera standing places cost about €15 and go on sale online the day before. Schönbrunn's gardens are free to walk.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 15 to 15 · Slovakia

    Bratislava 1 night · $45

    Pastel squares, quirky bronze statues and a white castle overlooking the Danube fill this compact, walkable old town.

    Budget tip: An easy cheap stop between Vienna and Budapest, with trains and buses linking all three for little money.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 16 to 18 · Hungary

    Budapest 3 nights · $159

    Soak in the Széchenyi thermal bath, gaze across the Danube at the Parliament and drink in the ruin bars of the Jewish Quarter.

    Budget tip: Lunch menus (napi menü) are the cheap way to eat. Walk up to Fisherman's Bastion and Gellért Hill for free views.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 19 to 21 · Poland

    Kraków 3 nights · $144

    Stroll the grand old town square, eat pierogi in the Jewish quarter of Kazimierz and day trip to the Wieliczka salt mine.

    Budget tip: Milk bars serve hot lunches for under $6. Free walking tours run daily from the main square.

    Getting here: about $45 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $750.
