Getting around

There are no trains, but cheap intercity buses link Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca and Paphos. Buses to the Troodos mountains are limited, so many people rent a car for a few days.

Where to sleep

Paphos and Larnaca have a few hostels and cheap apartments, and Troodos villages have agrotourism guesthouses in restored stone houses.

What to eat

  • Halloumi: squeaky grilled cheese, often from goat and sheep milk
  • Souvlaki: grilled pork skewers in pita with salad
  • Kleftiko: lamb slow-baked in a sealed clay oven
  • Koupepia: vine leaves stuffed with rice and minced meat

Money

Cards work widely in the south, but carry cash for village tavernas. The north uses the Turkish lira, and leaving about 10 percent is a normal tip.

Phone data

Prepaid SIMs from Cyta, Epic or PrimeTel are easy to buy and EU SIMs roam free in the south, but northern Cyprus uses Turkish networks where EU roaming does not apply.

Heads up

Crossing between the south and the north is easy at official checkpoints in Nicosia, but EU roaming and car rental insurance usually stop at the line.

Cyprus travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Cyprus cost on a budget?

About $57 a day for one person, from $52 in Famagusta to $60 in Ayia Napa. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $399 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Cyprus?

April to June and September to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Cyprus?

Famagusta, at about $52 a day. Walled Venetian city in the north with a Gothic cathedral turned mosque, Othello's Tower and the eerie fenced ghost resort of Varosha.

How long do you need in Cyprus?

Nicosia, the Troodos mountains and Paphos. 1 week.

How much is $1 in Cyprus?

One US dollar is about 0.889 Euro (EUR), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 17.78 EUR, and a typical budget day of $57 comes to about 51.00 EUR.

How do you get around Cyprus cheaply?

There are no trains, but cheap intercity buses link Nicosia, Limassol, Larnaca and Paphos. Buses to the Troodos mountains are limited, so many people rent a car for a few days.

What food should you try in Cyprus?

Halloumi, squeaky grilled cheese, often from goat and sheep milk. Souvlaki, grilled pork skewers in pita with salad. Kleftiko, lamb slow-baked in a sealed clay oven. Koupepia, vine leaves stuffed with rice and minced meat.
