India

Golden Triangle and Rajasthan

Delhi, the Taj Mahal and the desert cities of Rajasthan, ending on the Ganges in Varanasi, for curious travelers comfortable with crowds.

Length21 days
Total, flights included$1,591
All-in per day$76
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · India

    Delhi 3 nights · $69

    Mughal grandeur at Humayun's Tomb and the Red Fort, Qutub Minar's sandstone tower, and parathas fried to order in Old Delhi's lanes.

    Budget tip: Ride the Delhi Metro instead of autos; it is cheap, air-conditioned and reaches most sights. Many monuments sell discounted tickets online, skipping queues.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 5 · India

    Agra 2 nights · $66

    See the Taj Mahal glow at sunrise, explore red sandstone Agra Fort and day-trip to the abandoned Mughal city of Fatehpur Sikri.

    Budget tip: Watch the Taj at sunset from Mehtab Bagh across the river for a fraction of the price.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  3. Days 6 to 8 · India

    Jaipur 3 nights · $69

    Climb to Amber Fort, gaze up at the honeycomb Hawa Mahal and bargain for textiles in the bazaars of the Pink City.

    Budget tip: The composite ticket covers several forts and monuments for less. Use Uber autos to avoid haggling with rickshaw drivers.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  4. Days 9 to 10 · India

    Pushkar 2 nights · $34

    Holy lake ringed by 52 bathing ghats, the rare Brahma Temple, desert sunrise from Savitri Mata hill and November's huge camel fair.

    Budget tip: Politely decline anyone handing out flowers at the ghats; it leads to donation demands. Prices triple during the camel fair, so book ahead or visit outside it.

    Getting here: about $4 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  5. Days 11 to 12 · India

    Jodhpur 2 nights · $46

    Blue-washed houses tumbling below the cliff-top Mehrangarh Fort, the spice stalls of Sardar Market and makhaniya lassi by the clock tower.

    Budget tip: Stay in the old blue city near the fort for rooftop views and walk everywhere; the fort audio guide is worth paying for.

    Getting here: about $6 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  6. Days 13 to 15 · India

    Jaisalmer 3 nights · $69

    A golden sandstone fort that people still live in rises from the Thar Desert, with camel treks and nights under the stars nearby.

    Budget tip: Choose a small camel camp away from Sam dunes for quieter nights. Haveli guesthouses in the fort are cheap.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  7. Days 16 to 18 · India

    Udaipur 3 nights · $66

    Lake palaces, rooftop sunsets over Lake Pichola and a day trip to the vast walls of Kumbhalgarh fort.

    Budget tip: Rooftop hostels on the lake cost the same as ones without the view, so hold out for a lakeside bed.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  8. Days 19 to 21 · India

    Varanasi 3 nights · $54

    Drift past the ghats on a dawn boat ride, then join the crowds for the fire-lit evening Ganga aarti ceremony.

    Budget tip: Agree boat prices before you board. Lassi shops near the ghats make a cheap, filling lunch.

    Getting here: about $70 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,000.
