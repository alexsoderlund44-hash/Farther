Mexico vs Guatemala: which is cheaper?

Guatemala is the cheaper of the two, at about $33 a day against $41 in Mexico, which is roughly 20% less each day. Two weeks costs about $462 in Guatemala and $574 in Mexico, before flights. Both are at their best in November to March, so you could combine them on one trip.
