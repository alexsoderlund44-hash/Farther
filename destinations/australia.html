Getting around

Greyhound buses cover the east coast with hop-on hop-off passes, and campervan relocation deals can be very cheap if your dates are flexible. Domestic flights on Jetstar and Virgin Australia save days on long jumps like Cairns to Uluru. Uber and DiDi operate in the cities, and Uluru tours and Whitsunday sailing trips are worth booking ahead.

Where to sleep

Hostel dorms are easy to find along the east coast, and campgrounds and free rest areas suit people with a campervan. Book ahead during school holidays and around Christmas.

What to eat

  • Meat pie: hand-sized pastry filled with minced beef and gravy
  • Vegemite toast: buttered toast with a thin layer of yeast spread
  • Flat white: espresso with velvety steamed milk
  • Barramundi: mild white fish, often grilled or battered

Money

Contactless cards work almost everywhere, even at markets, and ATMs from the big four banks are the cheapest to use. Tipping isn't expected, and haggling isn't done.

Phone data

Prepaid SIMs from Telstra, Optus or Vodafone are sold everywhere and need ID verification with your passport; Telstra has the best coverage in the outback, but expect long stretches with no signal.

Heads up

The sun is strong, so wear sunscreen and a hat, and only swim between the red and yellow flags on patrolled beaches.

Australia travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Australia cost on a budget?

About $77 a day for one person, from $57 in Blue Mountains to $120 in Whitsunday Islands. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $539 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Australia?

March to May and September to November is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Australia?

Blue Mountains, at about $57 a day. Sandstone cliffs and eucalyptus valleys glowing blue in the haze, with the Three Sisters above Katoomba and an easy day trip from Sydney.
