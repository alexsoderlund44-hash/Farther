Albania vs Montenegro: which is cheaper?

Albania is the cheaper of the two, at about $36 a day against $51 in Montenegro, which is roughly 29% less each day. Two weeks costs about $504 in Albania and $714 in Montenegro, before flights. Both are at their best in May to June and September to October, so you could combine them on one trip.
