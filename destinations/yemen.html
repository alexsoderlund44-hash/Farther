Getting around

Visitors to Socotra travel only on organized tours, which include the flight from the mainland or a nearby country, a driver and a 4x4. There is no public transport worth relying on, and roads to remote areas are rough. Book well ahead, as charters are infrequent and can change.

Where to sleep

Most tours camp on beaches and plateaus with basic equipment provided, plus a few simple guesthouses or hotels in Hadibo.

What to eat

  • Saltah: bubbling meat stew topped with fenugreek froth
  • Mandi: slow-cooked rice and meat with smoky spices
  • Bint al-sahn: layered honey bread with black seeds
  • Fresh fish: grilled catch of the day, often eaten on the beach

Money

Bring all your money in US dollars cash, as card payments and ATMs can't be relied on. Most costs are paid to your tour operator in advance, and small tips for drivers and guides are customary.

Phone data

Don't count on data on Socotra, as coverage is limited to the main town and some roads, so tell people at home you'll be out of contact.

Heads up

Socotra's plants and animals are protected, so don't take seeds, plants or dragon's blood resin off the island.

Yemen travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Yemen cost on a budget?

About $60 a day for one person, from $32 in Sana'a to $100 in Socotra. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $420 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Yemen?

October to March is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Yemen?

Sana'a, at about $32 a day. A UNESCO old city of gingerbread tower houses, decorated with white gypsum around stained-glass windows.

How long do you need in Yemen?

Socotra only, on a 1 week organised tour.

How much is $1 in Yemen?

One US dollar is about 237 Yemeni rial (YER), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 4,738 YER, and a typical budget day of $60 comes to about 14,000 YER.

How do you get around Yemen cheaply?

Visitors to Socotra travel only on organized tours, which include the flight from the mainland or a nearby country, a driver and a 4x4. There is no public transport worth relying on, and roads to remote areas are rough. Book well ahead, as charters are infrequent and can change.

What food should you try in Yemen?

Saltah, bubbling meat stew topped with fenugreek froth. Mandi, slow-cooked rice and meat with smoky spices. Bint al-sahn, layered honey bread with black seeds. Fresh fish, grilled catch of the day, often eaten on the beach.

Is it safe to travel to Yemen?
