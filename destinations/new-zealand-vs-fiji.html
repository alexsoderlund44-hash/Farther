New Zealand vs Fiji: which is cheaper?

Fiji is the cheaper of the two, at about $65 a day against $76 in New Zealand, which is roughly 14% less each day. Two weeks costs about $910 in Fiji and $1,064 in New Zealand, before flights. Their best seasons don't overlap: New Zealand is best in November to April and Fiji in May to October.
