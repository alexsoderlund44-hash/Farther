Japan vs South Korea: which is cheaper?

South Korea is the cheaper of the two, at about $54 a day against $66 in Japan, which is roughly 18% less each day. Two weeks costs about $756 in South Korea and $924 in Japan, before flights. Both are at their best in April to May and October to November, so you could combine them on one trip.
