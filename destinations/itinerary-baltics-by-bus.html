Lithuania · Latvia · Estonia

Baltic Capitals by Bus

Cheap buses link Vilnius, Riga and Tallinn's old towns with castles, dunes and forests between, a good first look at northern Europe without Nordic prices.

Length16 days
Total, flights included$1,569
All-in per day$98
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Lithuania

    Vilnius 3 nights · $129

    Church spires and baroque courtyards fill the old town, and across the river lies Užupis, the self-declared artists' republic.

    Budget tip: Try cepelinai, potato dumplings, in local canteens. Free walking tours are a good first-day introduction.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 4 · Lithuania

    Trakai 1 night · $46

    A red-brick Gothic castle on a lake island, reached by wooden footbridges, with Karaim kibinai pastries to eat by the water.

    Budget tip: Easy day trip by bus from Vilnius. Walk around the lakeshore for free views of the castle.

    Getting here: about $3 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 5 to 5 · Lithuania

    Kaunas 1 night · $39

    Interwar modernist buildings, a red-brick castle and bold street art fill Lithuania's second city.

    Budget tip: Cheaper than Vilnius for beds and food, and the trains between the two cities are cheap and quick.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 6 to 7 · Lithuania

    Curonian Spit 2 nights · $112

    Huge drifting sand dunes, pine forests and empty Baltic beaches on a thin strip of land between lagoon and sea.

    Budget tip: Rent a bike in Nida and ride the forest cycle paths. Stay in Nida guesthouses outside July and August.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 8 to 10 · Latvia

    Riga 3 nights · $129

    Ornate art nouveau facades and a huge central market housed in old Zeppelin hangars, selling smoked fish and rye bread.

    Budget tip: Eat at Lido buffets for cheap Latvian food. The Central Market sells picnic supplies for much less.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 11 to 11 · Latvia

    Gauja National Park 1 night · $46

    Sandstone cliffs, medieval castles at Sigulda and Turaida, and lazy canoe trips down the Gauja river.

    Budget tip: Trains from Riga take about an hour. Hiking trails between the castles are free to walk.

    Getting here: about $4 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 12 to 13 · Estonia

    Tartu 2 nights · $88

    Estonia's university town mixes cosy student bars and riverside parks with the striking Estonian National Museum.

    Budget tip: Cheaper than Tallinn. The Estonian National Museum is worth the ticket price.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 14 to 16 · Estonia

    Tallinn 3 nights · $156

    One of Europe's best-preserved medieval old towns, with city walls, towers and rooftop views from Toompea hill.

    Budget tip: Public transport is free for residents and cheap for visitors. Walk the Old Town; it's compact and free to explore.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $750.
