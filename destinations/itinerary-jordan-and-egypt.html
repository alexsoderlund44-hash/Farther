Jordan · Egypt

Petra to the Pyramids

Petra, Wadi Rum and the Red Sea, then Cairo and the Nile temples, a history rich trip with real desert nights and reef snorkeling.

Length21 days
Total, flights included$1,960
All-in per day$93
Stops9

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 2 · Jordan

    Amman 2 nights · $76

    Climb to the Citadel for city views, sit in the Roman theatre and eat falafel at Hashem, a downtown institution.

    Budget tip: Buy the Jordan Pass before you arrive. It covers your visa and Petra entry, which saves a lot.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 3 to 3 · Jordan

    Madaba 1 night · $40

    A friendly town of Byzantine mosaics, including the oldest surviving map of the Holy Land on the floor of St George's Church.

    Budget tip: Madaba is a quieter, cheaper base than Amman, with good guesthouses and only 30 minutes from the airport.

    Getting here: about $5 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 4 to 5 · Jordan

    Dana Biosphere Reserve 2 nights · $84

    A stone village perched on a canyon rim, and a full-day hike down Wadi Dana through changing landscapes toward Feynan.

    Budget tip: Stay at the Dana guesthouse in the village. The Jordan Trail passes through here on its way to Petra.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 6 to 8 · Jordan

    Petra 3 nights · $177

    Walk the Siq to the Treasury, climb the 800 steps to the Monastery and hike the quieter back trail from Little Petra.

    Budget tip: Get a 2 or 3 day Petra ticket through the Jordan Pass. Arrive at 6 a.m. for an empty Siq.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 9 to 10 · Jordan

    Wadi Rum 2 nights · $96

    Explore sandstone canyons and red dunes by jeep or on foot, then sleep in a Bedouin camp under a sky full of stars.

    Budget tip: Camp prices often include dinner and breakfast. Book a jeep tour directly with a local Bedouin camp to avoid agency markups.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 11 to 13 · Egypt

    Dahab 3 nights · $84

    A laid-back Red Sea town where you snorkel coral reefs straight from the shore and climb Mount Sinai for sunrise.

    Budget tip: Snorkel the Lighthouse and Eel Garden reefs for free. Dive courses here are among the cheapest anywhere in the world.

    Getting here: about $115 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 14 to 16 · Egypt

    Cairo 3 nights · $90

    Treasures in the Egyptian Museum, minarets of Islamic Cairo, haggling in Khan el-Khalili and cheap bowls of koshari in Downtown.

    Budget tip: Use Uber instead of street taxis to avoid haggling. The metro costs under $0.50 and skips the city's notorious traffic.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 17 to 19 · Egypt

    Luxor 3 nights · $108

    Walk among Karnak's giant columns, descend into painted tombs in the Valley of the Kings and drift over the West Bank by dawn balloon.

    Budget tip: Cross the Nile on the public ferry for a few cents. Entry tickets add up quickly, so pick your temples.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  9. Days 20 to 21 · Egypt

    Aswan 2 nights · $60

    Sail the Nile by felucca at sunset and wander the colorfully painted Nubian villages on Elephantine Island.

    Budget tip: Sleep in a Nubian guesthouse on the west bank. Abu Simbel is a long but worthwhile day trip from here.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $900.
