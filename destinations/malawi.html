Getting around

Minibuses and larger buses run between towns, and shared taxis or pickups cover the last stretch to places like Cape Maclear. Expect long waits until vehicles fill. The Ilala ferry runs up and down the lake when in service, but its schedule is unreliable, so check locally.

Where to sleep

Lakeside lodges at Cape Maclear and Nkhata Bay offer dorms, cheap chalets and camping, and many have their own restaurants. Zomba Plateau has campsites and simple lodges.

What to eat

  • Nsima: stiff maize porridge, eaten by hand with relish
  • Chambo: tilapia-like fish from Lake Malawi, usually grilled or fried
  • Usipa: tiny dried lake fish, cooked with tomato and onion
  • Mandasi: sweet fried dough, sold at roadside stalls

Money

Carry kwacha in cash, since cards work mostly in Lilongwe and a few lodges, and ATMs are found mainly in cities and larger towns. Haggling is normal at craft stalls, and tips for guides and staff are appreciated.

Phone data

Airtel and TNM SIMs are easy to get with your passport, and coverage is fair along the main roads and lakeshore.

Heads up

Bilharzia is present in parts of Lake Malawi, and malaria is a risk countrywide, so ask a doctor about both before swimming.

Malawi travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Malawi cost on a budget?

About $25 a day for one person, from $21 in Zomba Plateau to $80 in Majete Wildlife Reserve. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $175 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Malawi?

May to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Malawi?

Zomba Plateau, at about $21 a day. Walk through pine forests to waterfalls and viewpoints high over the southern plains, a cool escape from the lowland heat.

How long do you need in Malawi?

Lilongwe, Cape Maclear, up the lake to Nkhata Bay, with Zomba Plateau on the way. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in Malawi?

One US dollar is about 1,745 Malawian kwacha (MWK), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 34,905 MWK, and a typical budget day of $25 comes to about 44,000 MWK.

How do you get around Malawi cheaply?

Minibuses and larger buses run between towns, and shared taxis or pickups cover the last stretch to places like Cape Maclear. Expect long waits until vehicles fill. The Ilala ferry runs up and down the lake when in service, but its schedule is unreliable, so check locally.

What food should you try in Malawi?
