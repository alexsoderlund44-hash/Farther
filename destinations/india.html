India on a budget

Overwhelming, endlessly varied and very cheap, from Rajasthan's forts to Himalayan valleys, with sleeper trains, street food and budget hostels linking it all.

Getting around

Trains are the best value for long distances, and overnight sleeper buses and cheap domestic flights fill the gaps. Uber, Ola and Rapido work in most cities and avoid fare haggling. Book train tickets weeks ahead if you can, because popular routes sell out.

Where to sleep

Hostel chains like Zostel and simple guesthouses are cheap almost everywhere, and Rajasthan has heritage havelis at fair prices.

What to eat

  • Masala dosa: crispy rice crepe filled with spiced potato
  • Thali: a platter of curries, dal, rice and bread
  • Chole bhature: spicy chickpeas with puffy fried bread
  • Masala chai: milky spiced tea sold on every corner

Money

Cards work in cities, but carry cash for small shops, rickshaws and rural areas, and expect ATM fees on foreign cards. Haggling is normal at markets and with auto-rickshaws, and small tips are appreciated in restaurants.

Phone data

Airtel and Jio SIMs need your passport, visa and a photo, and activation can take a day, so an eSIM is simpler; coverage is good in most areas.

Heads up

Drink bottled or filtered water, and be wary of touts at stations who say your booked hotel or train is cancelled.

India travel: quick answers

How much does a day in India cost on a budget?

About $23 a day for one person, from $14 in Amritsar to $33 in Agra. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $161 before flights.

When is the best time to visit India?

October to March is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in India?

Amritsar, at about $14 a day. The Golden Temple shimmers on its sacred pool, and its community kitchen, the langar, feeds tens of thousands of visitors every day.

How long do you need in India?

Jaipur and Udaipur in Rajasthan, then Varanasi and up to Rishikesh. 3 to 4 weeks.

How much is $1 in India?

One US dollar is about 96.40 Indian rupee (INR), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 1,928 INR, and a typical budget day of $23 comes to about 2,200 INR.

How do you get around India cheaply?

Trains are the best value for long distances, and overnight sleeper buses and cheap domestic flights fill the gaps. Uber, Ola and Rapido work in most cities and avoid fare haggling. Book train tickets weeks ahead if you can, because popular routes sell out.

What food should you try in India?

Masala dosa, crispy rice crepe filled with spiced potato. Thali, a platter of curries, dal, rice and bread. Chole bhature, spicy chickpeas with puffy fried bread. Masala chai, milky spiced tea sold on every corner.
