Getting around

Malta Public Transport buses reach almost everywhere, and a Tallinja card cuts the fares. The Gozo Channel ferry and the fast ferry from Valletta link to Gozo, and Bolt, Uber and eCabs are widely used.

Where to sleep

Hostels cluster in St Julian's and Sliema, and Gozo has farmhouse rooms and guesthouses. Summer prices rise sharply, so book early.

What to eat

  • Pastizzi: flaky pastries filled with ricotta or mushy peas
  • Ftira: Maltese bread with tuna, tomato, capers and olives
  • Stuffat tal-fenek: rabbit stewed in wine and garlic
  • Kinnie: bittersweet orange and herb soft drink

Money

Cards are common, but carry cash for pastizzi shops, buses and small bars. Leave 5 to 10 percent in restaurants if service is not included.

Phone data

Prepaid SIMs from Epic, GO or Melita need ID, EU SIMs roam free, and coverage is excellent on Malta and Gozo.

Heads up

Traffic drives on the left, so look right first when crossing the road.

Malta travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Malta cost on a budget?

About $53 a day for one person, from $49 in Marsaxlokk to $61 in Comino. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $371 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Malta?

April to June and September to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Malta?

Marsaxlokk, at about $49 a day. Colourful luzzu fishing boats bob in the harbour of this village, famous for its Sunday fish market.

How long do you need in Malta?

Valletta, Mdina and a few days on Gozo. 5 days.

How much is $1 in Malta?

One US dollar is about 0.889 Euro (EUR), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 17.78 EUR, and a typical budget day of $53 comes to about 47.00 EUR.

How do you get around Malta cheaply?

Malta Public Transport buses reach almost everywhere, and a Tallinja card cuts the fares. The Gozo Channel ferry and the fast ferry from Valletta link to Gozo, and Bolt, Uber and eCabs are widely used.

What food should you try in Malta?

Pastizzi, flaky pastries filled with ricotta or mushy peas. Ftira, Maltese bread with tuna, tomato, capers and olives. Stuffat tal-fenek, rabbit stewed in wine and garlic. Kinnie, bittersweet orange and herb soft drink.
