Getting around

Trains connect Cairo, Luxor and Aswan, and the sleeper train on that route is worth booking ahead. Go Bus and other coach companies cover Dahab and the rest of Sinai, while Uber, Careem and inDrive work well in Cairo and Alexandria. Microbuses are the cheapest way around towns once you know the routes.

Where to sleep

Downtown Cairo has many cheap hostels and budget hotels, Aswan has simple Nubian guesthouses, and Dahab has seaside camps and dive hostels. Siwa has small eco-lodges and guesthouses.

What to eat

  • Koshari: rice, lentils and pasta with tomato sauce and fried onions
  • Ful medames: slow-cooked fava beans with oil, cumin and bread
  • Taameya: Egyptian falafel made from fava beans
  • Karkade: chilled or hot hibiscus tea, deep red and tart

Money

Cash is king, ATMs are common in cities, and cards work in bigger hotels and chain stores. Tipping (baksheesh) is expected for almost any small service, so carry plenty of small notes, and haggle at markets and with drivers.

Phone data

SIM cards from Vodafone, Orange, Etisalat (e&) or WE need your passport and are best bought at an official store, and coverage is good along the Nile and in Sinai towns.

Heads up

At the pyramids and other big sites, camel and horse rides often get pricier mid-ride, so agree the full price and duration before you get on.

Egypt travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Egypt cost on a budget?

About $30 a day for one person, from $25 in Nuweiba to $51 in Sharm el-Sheikh. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $210 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Egypt?

October to April is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Egypt?

Nuweiba, at about $25 a day. Relaxed Gulf of Aqaba coast lined with simple beach camps of palm huts, Bedouin food and views across to Saudi mountains.

How long do you need in Egypt?

Cairo, sleeper train to Luxor and Aswan, back north to Dahab, then a long bus west to Siwa. 3 weeks.

How much is $1 in Egypt?

One US dollar is about 52.25 Egyptian pound (EGP), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 1,045 EGP, and a typical budget day of $30 comes to about 1,600 EGP.
