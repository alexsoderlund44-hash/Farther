Getting around

CTM and Supratours buses link most towns, and ONCF trains run the north, including the fast Al Boraq line between Tangier and Casablanca. Shared grand taxis fill up and leave between nearby towns, and petit taxis work inside cities. Buy bus tickets a day ahead for popular routes like Marrakech to Merzouga.

Where to sleep

Riads and small hostels inside the old medinas are the classic cheap stay, and Merzouga has simple desert camps and guesthouses. Rooms near Toubkal in Imlil are basic gites.

What to eat

  • Tagine: slow-cooked stew of meat or vegetables in a clay pot
  • Harira: tomato, lentil and chickpea soup, big during Ramadan
  • Msemen: flaky square flatbread, eaten with honey or cheese
  • Mint tea: sweet green tea with fresh mint, poured from height

Money

Morocco runs on cash, ATMs are easy to find in towns, and cards mostly work only in bigger hotels and shops. Haggling is normal in souks, and small tips for guides, porters and cafe service are expected.

Phone data

A local SIM from Maroc Telecom, Orange or inwi is cheap and quick to set up with your passport, and coverage is good in towns but patchy in the mountains and desert.

Heads up

In the medinas, people may say a street is closed or offer to guide you, then lead you to a shop or ask for money, so a polite no and an offline map help.

Morocco travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Morocco cost on a budget?

About $31 a day for one person, from $27 in Sidi Ifni to $62 in M'Hamid & Erg Chigaga. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $217 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Morocco?

March to May and October to November is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Morocco?

Sidi Ifni, at about $27 a day. Faded Spanish-era Art Deco streets on the Atlantic, a short coastal walk from the red rock arches and wild sands of Legzira beach.

How long do you need in Morocco?

Marrakech, up to Imlil for Toubkal, across to Merzouga, then north to Chefchaouen. 2 to 3 weeks.

How much is $1 in Morocco?

One US dollar is about 9.92 Moroccan dirham (MAD), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 198 MAD, and a typical budget day of $31 comes to about 310 MAD.

CTM and Supratours buses link most towns, and ONCF trains run the north, including the fast Al Boraq line between Tangier and Casablanca. Shared grand taxis fill up and leave between nearby towns, and petit taxis work inside cities. Buy bus tickets a day ahead for popular routes like Marrakech to Merzouga.
