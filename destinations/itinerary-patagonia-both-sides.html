Argentina · Chile

Patagonia on Both Sides

Lakes around Bariloche and Puerto Varas, Chiloe's wooden churches, then Torres del Paine, glaciers and El Chalten, for hikers willing to pay southern prices.

Length23 days
Total, flights included$3,077
All-in per day$134
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Argentina

    Bariloche 3 nights · $156

    Blue lakes, refugio-to-refugio hikes through Nahuel Huapi National Park and some of Patagonia's best chocolate shops.

    Budget tip: Refugios offer cheap bunks on the trail; bring your own sleeping bag and some food to save money.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 5 · Argentina

    El Bolsón 2 nights · $84

    A hippie town in a valley of hops and berry farms, with mountain refugios, craft beer and the Cajón del Azul gorge.

    Budget tip: Sleep in refugios along the trails rather than in town. The craft market runs several days a week.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 6 to 8 · Chile

    Puerto Varas 3 nights · $156

    A lakeside town of German-era wooden houses facing the snowy cone of Osorno volcano, with the Petrohué falls nearby.

    Budget tip: Local minibuses run to Petrohué and Ensenada for a few dollars. Try kuchen, a German-style fruit cake, in the town's bakeries.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 9 to 11 · Chile

    Chiloé 3 nights · $144

    A misty island of wooden UNESCO-listed churches, palafito stilt houses in Castro and curanto feasts cooked in earth pits.

    Budget tip: Buses and ferries run from Puerto Montt. Try curanto at Castro's market stalls, where it costs less than in restaurants.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 12 to 12 · Chile

    Punta Arenas 1 night · $61

    Windswept city on the Strait of Magellan, with ornate wool-baron mansions and boat trips to the Magdalena Island penguin colony.

    Budget tip: Duty-free shopping in the Zona Franca is good for gear; the penguin trip is pricey, so compare Isla Magdalena with the Otway colony.

    Getting here: about $110 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 13 to 16 · Chile

    Torres del Paine 4 nights · $352

    Hike the W trek past glaciers, Lago Grey and the French Valley to the base of Patagonia's famous granite towers.

    Budget tip: Campsites must be booked months ahead. Carry your own tent and food to skip pricey refugio beds and meals.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 17 to 19 · Argentina

    El Calafate 3 nights · $261

    The gateway to the Perito Moreno glacier, where towers of blue ice crack and crash into the lake.

    Budget tip: Take the public bus to the glacier instead of a tour. The park fee is high, so go once and stay all day.

    Getting here: about $45 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 20 to 23 · Argentina

    El Chaltén 4 nights · $312

    Trails start straight from town and climb to Laguna de los Tres, the glacial lake beneath the granite spires of Fitz Roy.

    Budget tip: Since late 2024 the park charges foreigners about $40 a day for trails, with multi-day passes cheaper. Carry food from El Calafate; town shops are pricey.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,300.
