Getting around

Where travel is possible, long-distance buses and domestic flights link the main cities, and trains are slow; many roads and regions are affected by the conflict. Check current conditions for every leg before booking anything.

Where to sleep

Foreigners may stay only in licensed hotels and guesthouses, which limits the cheapest options.

What to eat

  • Mohinga: fish and rice noodle soup, the national breakfast
  • Laphet thoke: fermented tea leaf salad with nuts and beans
  • Shan noodles: rice noodles with chicken or pork in a light sauce
  • Lahpet yay: sweet, strong tea with condensed milk

Money

The country runs mostly on cash kyat, and foreign cards often don't work in ATMs, so plan funds carefully. Tipping is small and optional, and haggling is normal at markets.

Phone data

MPT and other operators sell SIMs with passport registration, but internet shutdowns and blocked sites are common.

Heads up

Don't photograph military or police sites, convoys or checkpoints.

Myanmar travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Myanmar cost on a budget?

About $33 a day for one person, from $28 in Yangon to $44 in Bagan. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $231 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Myanmar?

November to February is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Myanmar?

Yangon, at about $28 a day. The gilded Shwedagon Pagoda glowing at dusk, crumbling colonial blocks downtown and tea shops serving mohinga, the fish noodle soup locals eat for breakfast.

How long do you need in Myanmar?

What food should you try in Myanmar?

Is it safe to travel to Myanmar?
