Australia

Sydney to the Daintree

Harbor beaches, the Blue Mountains, Byron's surf, K'gari's sand tracks, Whitsunday sailing and Great Barrier Reef snorkeling, the classic coastal run north.

Length21 days
Total, flights included$3,529
All-in per day$168
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 4 · Australia

    Sydney 4 nights · $268

    The Opera House, harbor ferries to Manly and the clifftop Bondi to Coogee walk past ocean pools and sandy coves.

    Budget tip: Opal card fares are capped on Sundays. The Manly ferry doubles as a cheap harbor cruise.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 5 to 6 · Australia

    Blue Mountains 2 nights · $114

    Sandstone cliffs and eucalyptus valleys glowing blue in the haze, with the Three Sisters above Katoomba and an easy day trip from Sydney.

    Budget tip: The train from Sydney is cheap with an Opal card, and most walking tracks, including the Prince Henry Cliff Walk, start a short stroll from Katoomba station.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 7 to 9 · Australia

    Byron Bay 3 nights · $198

    Surf beaches, barefoot cafés and the lighthouse walk to mainland Australia's most easterly point, where dolphins often play below the cliffs.

    Budget tip: The Cape Byron walking track is free and best at sunrise; in winter you may spot humpback whales migrating past the headland.

    Getting here: about $90 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 10 to 11 · Australia

    Brisbane 2 nights · $122

    Subtropical riverside city with free CityHopper ferries, the South Bank swimming lagoon, Kangaroo Point cliffs and nearby koala sanctuaries.

    Budget tip: Ride the free CityHopper ferry along the river; Queensland public transport fares are a flat 50 cents, so buses and trains cost very little.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 12 to 13 · Australia

    K'gari (Fraser Island) 2 nights · $184

    The world's largest sand island, where you drive the beach highway, swim in clear freshwater lakes like Lake McKenzie and spot wild dingoes.

    Budget tip: Split a 4WD tag-along tour from Hervey Bay or Rainbow Beach with others. Driving yourself means hiring a 4WD and buying a vehicle permit first.

    Getting here: about $50 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 14 to 16 · Australia

    Whitsunday Islands 3 nights · $360

    Sail among 74 islands, sink your toes into the silica sand of Whitehaven Beach and climb to Hill Inlet as the tide swirls below.

    Budget tip: Wait until you reach Airlie Beach and grab a last-minute deal on a two-night sailing trip; walk-in prices often beat booking months ahead.

    Getting here: about $80 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 17 to 19 · Australia

    Cairns 3 nights · $291

    The launchpad for Great Barrier Reef snorkel and dive day trips, with a free swimming lagoon on the Esplanade and cheap hostels near the night markets.

    Budget tip: Pay the extra for an outer-reef day trip rather than a cheaper inner-reef boat; the coral and visibility are far better. Swim in the Esplanade Lagoon for free.

    Getting here: about $60 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 20 to 21 · Australia

    Daintree Rainforest 2 nights · $154

    Ancient rainforest tumbles straight down to the reef at Cape Tribulation, where cassowaries stroll across the road and boardwalks wind through mangroves.

    Budget tip: Take the cable ferry across the Daintree River and spend a night or two in a Cape Tribulation hostel instead of a rushed day trip from Cairns.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,500.
