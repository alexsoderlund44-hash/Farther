Nepal vs Bhutan: which is cheaper?

Nepal is the cheaper of the two, at about $27 a day against $230 in Bhutan, which is roughly 88% less each day. Two weeks costs about $378 in Nepal and $3,220 in Bhutan, before flights. Both are at their best in March to April and October to November, so you could combine them on one trip.
