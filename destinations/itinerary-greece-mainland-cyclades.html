Greece

Greek Mainland and Cyclades

Monasteries on Meteora's rocks, ancient Delphi and Nafplio, then ferries to Paros, Naxos and Amorgos, a mix of ruins and quiet island villages.

Length21 days
Total, flights included$2,252
All-in per day$107
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 2 · Greece

    Thessaloniki 2 nights · $102

    A lively waterfront promenade, Byzantine churches and the best street food in Greece fill this easygoing northern city.

    Budget tip: Strolling the waterfront costs nothing, and flaky bougatsa makes a cheap breakfast.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 3 to 4 · Greece

    Meteora 2 nights · $112

    Byzantine monasteries perched on top of giant sandstone pillars above Kalambaka, with old footpaths winding between them.

    Budget tip: Walk the old footpaths between monasteries instead of taking a tour. Each monastery has its own closing day, so check before you go.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 5 to 6 · Greece

    Delphi 2 nights · $120

    The ancient sanctuary of Apollo spills down the slopes of Mount Parnassus, over an olive-filled valley.

    Budget tip: Make a day trip by KTEL bus from Athens, or stay overnight to enter at opening time.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 7 to 8 · Greece

    Nafplio 2 nights · $112

    A Venetian harbour town of bougainvillea lanes, and the handiest base for ancient Mycenae and Epidaurus.

    Budget tip: Climb the 999 steps up to Palamidi fortress early. Take local buses to Mycenae.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 9 to 11 · Greece

    Athens 3 nights · $183

    Climb to the Parthenon on the Acropolis, wander the old lanes of Plaka and toast the floodlit ruins from a rooftop bar.

    Budget tip: Gyros cost about €4. Many sites are free on some winter Sundays, so plan museum days around them if you can.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 12 to 14 · Greece

    Paros 3 nights · $195

    Relaxed Cyclades hub with Naoussa's fishing harbour, the marble Byzantine Path, the Panagia Ekatontapiliani church and quick boats to Antiparos.

    Budget tip: Ferries from Piraeus or Rafina cost far less than flights; use the island buses or a rented scooter to reach the beaches.

    Getting here: about $45 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 15 to 18 · Greece

    Naxos 4 nights · $248

    Long sandy beaches like Plaka and Agios Prokopios, marble mountain villages and local cheese, all cheaper than Mykonos or Santorini.

    Budget tip: Book slow ferries rather than fast ones to save money. Local buses reach the beaches and mountain villages cheaply.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 19 to 21 · Greece

    Amorgos 3 nights · $180

    Long, rugged Cycladic island of the cliff-hugging Hozoviotissa Monastery, the shipwreck from The Big Blue and ancient paths between villages.

    Budget tip: Walk the old mule paths between Chora, Lagada and Aegiali instead of taking taxis, and check ferry days carefully as connections are limited.

    Getting here: about $40 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $800.
