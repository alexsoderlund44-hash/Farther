Thailand · Laos · Cambodia · Vietnam

Mekong Loop Grand Tour

Six weeks through Thailand, Laos, Cambodia and southern Vietnam, with night trains, river boats and temples for travelers with time to wander.

Length40 days
Total, flights included$2,389
All-in per day$60
Stops9

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 4 · Thailand

    Bangkok 4 nights · $140

    Hop between riverside temples by ferry, graze on street food in Chinatown at night and lose a morning in the weekend Chatuchak market.

    Budget tip: Orange-flag river ferries cost under $1 and beat the traffic. Politely skip tuk-tuk drivers offering 'gem tours', which are a well-known scam.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  2. Days 5 to 10 · Thailand

    Chiang Mai 6 nights · $198

    Wander golden temples in the moated old city, eat khao soi at the Sunday walking street and ride a songthaew up to Doi Suthep.

    Budget tip: Shared red songthaews cost about 30 baht around town. Avoid burning season in March and April, when smoke haze is heavy.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  3. Days 11 to 15 · Laos

    Luang Prabang 5 nights · $155

    Watch the dawn alms procession of saffron-robed monks, swim below the Kuang Si waterfalls and catch sunset from the top of Mount Phousi.

    Budget tip: The night market vegetarian buffet is about $2 a plate. Bike to Kuang Si if you are fit, or share a tuk-tuk.

    Getting here: about $45 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  4. Days 16 to 19 · Laos

    Vang Vieng 4 nights · $116

    Swim in the Blue Lagoons, explore caves and float down the Nam Song on a tube beneath towering limestone cliffs.

    Budget tip: The new railway gets you here from Luang Prabang in about an hour, far quicker and more comfortable than the winding mountain road.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  5. Days 20 to 21 · Laos

    Vientiane 2 nights · $54

    A laid-back Mekong capital with the golden stupa of Pha That Luang, the moving COPE visitor centre and riverside night markets.

    Budget tip: The new railway makes Vientiane an easy stop between Vang Vieng and the Thai border. COPE is free to visit, though donations help.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  6. Days 22 to 26 · Cambodia

    Siem Reap 5 nights · $155

    See Angkor Wat at sunrise and the jungle-wrapped roots of Ta Prohm, best explored at your own pace by bicycle.

    Budget tip: The 3-day Angkor pass is $62 and works on any 3 days within 10 days, so you can rest between temple runs.

    Getting here: about $120 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  7. Days 27 to 29 · Cambodia

    Phnom Penh 3 nights · $75

    Tour the Royal Palace, browse riverside markets and learn the country's painful history at the Tuol Sleng and Choeung Ek memorials.

    Budget tip: Use the PassApp ride app for fixed tuk-tuk fares. A bowl of street noodle soup is about $1.50 for breakfast.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  8. Days 30 to 35 · Cambodia

    Kampot 6 nights · $144

    Lazy river sunsets, peppercorn farms in the countryside and a scooter ride up to the misty plateau of Bokor mountain.

    Budget tip: Day trip to Kep for a crab market lunch instead of staying there, since Kampot has cheaper guesthouses and more choice.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  9. Days 36 to 40 · Vietnam

    Ho Chi Minh City 5 nights · $145

    Dodge a river of motorbikes, spend a sobering hour at the War Remnants Museum and reach the Mekong Delta by cheap local bus.

    Budget tip: District 3 and Binh Thanh have cheaper street food and banh mi than touristy District 1, and they feel more local too.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $950.
