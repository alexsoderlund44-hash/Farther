Getting around

In and around Goma, moto-taxis and shared minibuses are the usual transport, and boats cross Lake Kivu to Bukavu. Across the wider country, distances are huge and roads poor, so domestic flights are often the only practical link. The east, including Goma and Virunga, is covered by the advisory, so check current advice before any planning.

Where to sleep

Cheap rooms are mostly guesthouses and church-run hostels in the cities. Virunga's treks to Nyiragongo have used summit shelters run by the park, booked as part of the permit.

What to eat

  • Fufu: cassava or maize dough eaten with stews
  • Pondu: stewed cassava leaves with palm oil and fish
  • Moambe chicken: chicken in palm nut sauce
  • Liboke: fish steamed in banana leaves with spices

Money

US dollars are widely used alongside francs, but notes must be recent and undamaged or they will be refused. Cards work only in some upscale places, so carry cash; haggling is normal.

Phone data

Vodacom, Airtel, Orange and Africell sell SIMs that need passport registration, and coverage is fair in cities but patchy elsewhere.

Heads up

Taking photos in public, especially of officials or infrastructure, can lead to trouble, so ask first or keep the camera away.
