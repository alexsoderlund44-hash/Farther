Mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. Cash machines and exchange desks pay a little less.

Getting around

Long-distance buses run from Ulaanbaatar to provincial towns, and most travelers explore the countryside on shared jeep tours in Russian vans arranged through guesthouses. Domestic flights help with the far west. Join a shared tour a few days ahead to split the cost with others.

Where to sleep

Guesthouses in Ulaanbaatar are cheap and arrange tours, while out in the countryside you sleep in ger camps, with nomad families or in a tent.

What to eat

  • Buuz: steamed dumplings filled with mutton
  • Khuushuur: fried flat pastries stuffed with meat
  • Tsuivan: stir-fried noodles with meat and vegetables
  • Airag: fermented mare's milk, a summer drink

Money

Cards work in Ulaanbaatar, but carry cash for the countryside, where ATMs are only in provincial centers. Tipping your driver and guide after a tour is customary.

Phone data

Unitel and Mobicom SIMs need your passport and work in towns and along main routes, but much of the countryside has no signal.

Heads up

Pickpocketing is common in Ulaanbaatar, especially on buses and at the Narantuul market.

Mongolia travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Mongolia cost on a budget?

About $43 a day for one person, from $26 in Ulaanbaatar to $55 in Gobi Desert. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $301 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Mongolia?

June to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Mongolia?

Ulaanbaatar, at about $26 a day. Visit Gandan Monastery, catch a throat singing show and stock up for the overland trips that all start here.

How long do you need in Mongolia?

Ulaanbaatar, a week-long jeep trip to the Gobi, then north to Khövsgöl Lake. 3 weeks.

One US dollar is about 3,599 Mongolian tögrög (MNT), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 71,984 MNT, and a typical budget day of $43 comes to about 150,000 MNT.
