Getting around

Marshrutkas and shared taxis link Yerevan with most towns, leaving from a few different bus stations depending on the direction. Yandex Go and GG are the ride-hailing apps that work well and are cheap for longer day trips. In summer the overnight train between Yerevan and Tbilisi is worth booking ahead.

Where to sleep

Yerevan has many cheap hostels, and family guesthouses and B&Bs are the norm in Dilijan, Goris and around Tatev. Rooms are easy to find most of the year.

What to eat

  • Khorovats: Armenian barbecue of pork, lamb or vegetables
  • Lavash: thin flatbread baked in a clay tonir oven
  • Tolma: grape leaves stuffed with spiced meat and rice
  • Ghapama: pumpkin stuffed with rice and dried fruit, a festive dish

Money

Cards work in Yerevan cafes and shops, but bring dram in cash for marshrutkas, guesthouses and villages. Restaurants often add a service charge, and tipping beyond that is optional.

Phone data

Team, Viva or Ucom SIMs are cheap and easy to buy with your passport, and coverage is good in towns and along the main highways.

Heads up

Avoid travel near the border with Azerbaijan without checking current advice, as some roads pass close to it.

Armenia travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Armenia cost on a budget?

About $29 a day for one person, from $24 in Gyumri to $36 in Tatev Monastery. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $203 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Armenia?

May to June and September to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Armenia?

Gyumri, at about $24 a day. Black-tuff 19th-century streets, workshops of potters and woodcarvers, and a proud artistic streak define Armenia's second city.

How long do you need in Armenia?

Yerevan, north to Dilijan, then south through Areni to Tatev. 10 days, easy to pair with Georgia.

How much is $1 in Armenia?

One US dollar is about 363 Armenian dram (AMD), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 7,251 AMD, and a typical budget day of $29 comes to about 11,000 AMD.

How do you get around Armenia cheaply?

Marshrutkas and shared taxis link Yerevan with most towns, leaving from a few different bus stations depending on the direction. Yandex Go and GG are the ride-hailing apps that work well and are cheap for longer day trips. In summer the overnight train between Yerevan and Tbilisi is worth booking ahead.

What food should you try in Armenia?

Khorovats, Armenian barbecue of pork, lamb or vegetables. Lavash, thin flatbread baked in a clay tonir oven. Tolma, grape leaves stuffed with spiced meat and rice. Ghapama, pumpkin stuffed with rice and dried fruit, a festive dish.
