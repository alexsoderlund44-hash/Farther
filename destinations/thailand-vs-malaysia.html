Thailand vs Malaysia: which is cheaper?

Malaysia is the cheaper of the two, at about $26 a day against $40 in Thailand, which is roughly 35% less each day. Two weeks costs about $364 in Malaysia and $560 in Thailand, before flights. Their best seasons don't overlap: Thailand is best in November to February and Malaysia in March to September.
