Mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. Cash machines and exchange desks pay a little less.

Getting around

Buses link Skopje with Ohrid, Bitola and most towns, and minibuses cover smaller routes. Taxis in Skopje are cheap and metered, though ride-hailing apps are not common. Buses to Ohrid fill up on summer weekends, so buy a seat a day ahead.

Where to sleep

Hostels in Skopje and Ohrid are inexpensive, and private rooms and guesthouses are easy to find around Lake Ohrid. Book ahead in Ohrid during July and August.

What to eat

  • Tavče gravče: baked white beans in a clay pot
  • Ajvar: roasted red pepper spread, often homemade
  • Shopska salad: tomatoes, cucumber and onion under grated white cheese
  • Pastrmajlija: oval bread topped with diced meat and sometimes egg

Money

Carry denar in cash for buses, bakeries and guesthouses, though cards work in many city restaurants. Rounding up the bill is customary.

Phone data

An A1 or Telekom SIM is cheap and easy to buy with your passport, and coverage is good in towns.

Heads up

As in Albania, a nod can mean no and a head shake yes, so confirm with words.

North Macedonia travel: quick answers

How much does a day in North Macedonia cost on a budget?

About $32 a day for one person, from $28 in Tetovo to $34 in Galičica National Park. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $224 before flights.

When is the best time to visit North Macedonia?

May to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in North Macedonia?

Tetovo, at about $28 a day. Ornate Painted Mosque with floral designs, the Arabati Baba Tekke dervish complex and access to Popova Šapka on the Šar mountains.

How long do you need in North Macedonia?

Skopje, Matka Canyon, then west via Mavrovo to Lake Ohrid. 1 week.

How much is $1 in North Macedonia?

One US dollar is about 54.28 Macedonian denar (MKD), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 1,086 MKD, and a typical budget day of $32 comes to about 1,700 MKD.

How do you get around North Macedonia cheaply?

Buses link Skopje with Ohrid, Bitola and most towns, and minibuses cover smaller routes. Taxis in Skopje are cheap and metered, though ride-hailing apps are not common. Buses to Ohrid fill up on summer weekends, so buy a seat a day ahead.

What food should you try in North Macedonia?

Tavče gravče, baked white beans in a clay pot. Ajvar, roasted red pepper spread, often homemade. Shopska salad, tomatoes, cucumber and onion under grated white cheese. Pastrmajlija, oval bread topped with diced meat and sometimes egg.
