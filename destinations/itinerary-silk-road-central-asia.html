Kazakhstan · Kyrgyzstan · Uzbekistan

Silk Road Central Asia

Mountain lakes and yurt stays in Kyrgyzstan, then the tiled cities of Uzbekistan by train, for travelers who like fewer tourists.

Length20 days
Total, flights included$1,835
All-in per day$92
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Kazakhstan

    Almaty 3 nights · $96

    Leafy avenues, the Green Bazaar and day hikes from Medeu or up to the turquoise Big Almaty Lake in the mountains.

    Budget tip: Use Yandex Go for cheap taxis. Bus 12 runs from the center to the mountains at Medeu.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 6 · Kyrgyzstan

    Karakol 3 nights · $72

    A wooden Russian cathedral, a pagoda-style Dungan mosque and trailheads in every direction make this the hiking capital of Kyrgyzstan.

    Budget tip: The Sunday animal market is worth an early start. Ashlan-fu noodles cost about $1.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 7 to 8 · Kyrgyzstan

    Song-Kul Lake 2 nights · $68

    Sleep in a family yurt beside a high alpine lake, ride horses across summer pastures and drink fresh kumis with herders.

    Budget tip: Yurt stays include dinner and breakfast, so your food costs drop to almost nothing while you are here.

    Getting here: about $40 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 9 to 10 · Kyrgyzstan

    Bishkek 2 nights · $46

    Haggle in Osh Bazaar, admire Soviet-era architecture and take an easy day trip to hike in Ala Archa national park.

    Budget tip: Plov and lagman cost about $3 at local cafes. Marshrutka minibuses go everywhere in the city for 20 som.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 11 to 12 · Uzbekistan

    Tashkent 2 nights · $56

    Haggle in the domed Chorsu Bazaar, ride ornate Soviet metro stations and admire the tiled Hast Imam complex in Uzbekistan's capital.

    Budget tip: Metro rides cost a few cents. Eat plov at the Central Asian Plov Centre around lunchtime, when it is freshest.

    Getting here: about $35 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 13 to 15 · Uzbekistan

    Samarkand 3 nights · $87

    Stand in the Registan square at dusk, walk the avenue of tiled tombs at Shah-i-Zinda and visit the vast Bibi-Khanym mosque.

    Budget tip: Book Afrosiyob fast trains early on the railway website, as they sell out days ahead in high season.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 16 to 18 · Uzbekistan

    Bukhara 3 nights · $84

    A walkable old town of madrasas, covered trading domes and the Kalyan minaret, with tea houses around the Lyabi-Hauz pool.

    Budget tip: Family guesthouses in the old town include big breakfasts, so you can skip lunch on a budget.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 19 to 20 · Uzbekistan

    Khiva 2 nights · $64

    A complete walled city of mud-brick minarets and madrasas that glows golden at sunset from the ramparts.

    Budget tip: One ticket covers most sites inside Itchan Kala for 2 days, so you can explore slowly.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,100.
