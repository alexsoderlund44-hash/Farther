Portugal

Portugal Coast to Coast

Port cellars and the Douro Valley, university town Coimbra, Lisbon's hills and Sintra's palaces, then the Algarve cliffs.

Length19 days
Total, flights included$1,957
All-in per day$103
Stops7

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 4 · Portugal

    Porto 4 nights · $224

    Stroll Ribeira's riverside quay, cross the double-decker Dom Luís I bridge and sip tawny in the port cellars of Vila Nova de Gaia.

    Budget tip: Many port lodges in Gaia offer cheap tastings. Fill up on a francesinha sandwich or a bifana from a neighborhood café.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 5 to 6 · Portugal

    Douro Valley 2 nights · $138

    Terraced port vineyards stack up the slopes along the river, and family quintas pour tastings with valley views.

    Budget tip: Take the scenic train from Porto to Pinhão instead of paying for a tour.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 7 to 8 · Portugal

    Coimbra 2 nights · $100

    Students in black capes sing fado in the lanes of this old university town, home to the gilded baroque Joanina Library.

    Budget tip: University ticket slots sell out, so book your Joanina Library time ahead.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 9 to 12 · Portugal

    Lisbon 4 nights · $248

    Rattle up steep hills on Tram 28, watch the sun set from a miradouro viewpoint and eat warm pastéis de nata in the lanes of Alfama.

    Budget tip: Eat the prato do dia (dish of the day) at lunch for the best value, and walk or ride trams instead of taking taxis.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 13 to 13 · Portugal

    Sintra 1 night · $69

    Fairy-tale palaces and mossy gardens crown the forested hills here, including colourful Pena and the mystical Quinta da Regaleira, an easy day trip from Lisbon.

    Budget tip: Take the train from Lisbon and walk between the palaces instead of paying for tuk-tuks.

    Getting here: about $3 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 14 to 15 · Portugal

    Évora 2 nights · $104

    A Roman temple, a chapel lined with human bones and whitewashed lanes sit inside the walls of this Alentejo town.

    Budget tip: Alentejo lunch menus are cheap and huge. Cycle out to the nearby megaliths instead of taking a tour.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 16 to 19 · Portugal

    Lagos 4 nights · $244

    Sea caves, golden sandstone cliffs and the clifftop walk to Ponta da Piedade make this the western Algarve's liveliest beach town.

    Budget tip: Visit in May, June or September for half-price beds. Kayaking into the sea caves beats the boat tours.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $750.
