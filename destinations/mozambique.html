Getting around

Chapas, shared minibuses, run between towns, and long-distance buses often leave before dawn. The trip from Maputo to Tofo and Vilanculos is long, so break it into stages. Dhow and boat trips to the Bazaruto islands are booked locally in Vilanculos.

Where to sleep

Tofo and Vilanculos have beach hostels and lodges with dorms and camping. Book ahead around Christmas and Easter, when South African visitors arrive.

What to eat

  • Matapa: cassava leaves stewed with peanuts and coconut milk
  • Frango piri-piri: grilled chicken with hot chili sauce
  • Prawns: grilled garlic prawns, a coastal specialty
  • Pao: small crusty bread rolls sold fresh each morning

Money

Carry meticais in cash, as cards work mainly in bigger lodges, and ATMs are in towns but can run out. Haggling is normal at markets and for boat trips.

Phone data

Vodacom, Movitel and Tmcel SIMs need your passport, and coverage is good in towns and along the coast.

Heads up

Police checks are common, so always carry your passport or a certified copy.

Mozambique travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Mozambique cost on a budget?

About $54 a day for one person, from $29 in Inhambane to $87 in Gorongosa. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $378 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Mozambique?

May to November is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Mozambique?

Inhambane, at about $29 a day. A sleepy colonial town on a calm bay, with an old cathedral, dhows at the jetty and a slow, friendly pace.

How long do you need in Mozambique?

Maputo, Tofo, Vilanculos and the Bazaruto islands. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in Mozambique?

One US dollar is about 63.84 Mozambican metical (MZN), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 1,277 MZN, and a typical budget day of $54 comes to about 3,400 MZN.

How do you get around Mozambique cheaply?

Chapas, shared minibuses, run between towns, and long-distance buses often leave before dawn. The trip from Maputo to Tofo and Vilanculos is long, so break it into stages. Dhow and boat trips to the Bazaruto islands are booked locally in Vilanculos.

What food should you try in Mozambique?

Matapa, cassava leaves stewed with peanuts and coconut milk. Frango piri-piri, grilled chicken with hot chili sauce. Prawns, grilled garlic prawns, a coastal specialty. Pao, small crusty bread rolls sold fresh each morning.
