Mexico

Yucatan in Two Weeks

Colonial Merida, cenote swims, a car free island and the lagoon at Bacalar, a relaxed first trip to Mexico with short bus rides.

Length14 days
Total, flights included$1,291
All-in per day$92
Stops5

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Mexico

    Mérida 3 nights · $114

    The Yucatán's colonial capital fills its plazas with free music and dance most nights, with cenotes and Maya ruins an easy day trip away.

    Budget tip: Cheap colectivos run to the Cuzamá cenotes and Uxmal. Eat cochinita pibil tacos from market stalls for breakfast, the way locals do.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 5 · Mexico

    Valladolid 2 nights · $78

    Pastel streets, a swimmable cenote right in town and quick access to Chichén Itzá and the climbable pyramid at Ek Balam.

    Budget tip: Catch the first colectivo to Chichén Itzá so you arrive at opening, ahead of the tour buses from Cancún. Hostels and guesthouses here are cheap.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 6 to 8 · Mexico

    Holbox 3 nights · $156

    Sand streets and golf carts instead of cars, flamingos in the lagoon, whale sharks in summer and glowing bioluminescent plankton after dark.

    Budget tip: Bring plenty of cash, as the island's ATMs often run dry. The ferry leaves from Chiquilá, reached by bus from Cancún.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 9 to 11 · Mexico

    Tulum 3 nights · $168

    Clifftop Maya ruins above turquoise Caribbean water, plus cenotes like Gran Cenote and Dos Ojos for swimming among stalactites.

    Budget tip: Stay in town rather than the beach strip, rent a bicycle to reach the ruins and beaches, and arrive at the site right at opening.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 12 to 14 · Mexico

    Bacalar 3 nights · $120

    Paddleboard across the 'lagoon of seven colours' and swim in cenotes, a calmer, cheaper alternative to the Caribbean coast.

    Budget tip: It's cheaper than Tulum. Swim at the public balnearios for a small fee instead of paying for a boat tour.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $600.
