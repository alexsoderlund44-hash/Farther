Mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. Cash machines and exchange desks pay a little less.

Getting around

Trains from PKP Intercity and Polregio cover the country well, and FlixBus fills the gaps cheaply. Bolt, Uber and FreeNow all work in the cities, and the Jakdojade app handles local transit tickets. Book Intercity trains early for the best fares, especially on Friday and Sunday.

Where to sleep

Hostels in Kraków, Warsaw and Gdańsk are plentiful and good value, and private rooms (pokoje) are common in Zakopane and on the coast. Book ahead for the Baltic coast in July and August.

What to eat

  • Pierogi: dumplings filled with potato and cheese, meat or fruit
  • Żurek: sour rye soup with sausage and egg
  • Zapiekanka: open baguette with mushrooms and cheese, a cheap street snack
  • Oscypek: smoked sheep cheese from the Tatra mountains, often grilled

Money

Cards and contactless are accepted almost everywhere, even for small amounts. Avoid Euronet ATMs, which charge high fees, and always choose to be charged in złoty; tip about 10 percent in sit-down restaurants.

Phone data

Prepaid SIMs from Orange, Play, Plus or T-Mobile need registration with your passport in store, so an eSIM is often simpler, and coverage is good across the country.

Heads up

Many milk bars (bar mleczny) serve very cheap home-style meals, so look for them to save money at lunch.

Poland travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Poland cost on a budget?

About $48 a day for one person, from $37 in Lublin to $67 in Wieliczka Salt Mine. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $336 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Poland?

May to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Poland?

Lublin, at about $37 a day. Jewish heritage, the Byzantine-style frescoes of the Holy Trinity chapel and the sobering Majdanek memorial shape this eastern old town.

How long do you need in Poland?

Kraków, a few days hiking from Zakopane, then north to Gdańsk by train. 10 days.

One US dollar is about 3.90 Polish złoty (PLN), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 77.91 PLN, and a typical budget day of $48 comes to about 190 PLN.

Trains from PKP Intercity and Polregio cover the country well, and FlixBus fills the gaps cheaply. Bolt, Uber and FreeNow all work in the cities, and the Jakdojade app handles local transit tickets. Book Intercity trains early for the best fares, especially on Friday and Sunday.
