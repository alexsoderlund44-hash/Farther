Getting around

Minibus taxis (kombis) link Mbabane, Manzini and the main towns cheaply and leave when full. Reaching Mlilwane and Hlane is easier with a rental car from South Africa, since public transport stops at the main road. Check that your rental agreement allows crossing the border.

Where to sleep

Hostel-style lodges, camping and simple huts are the cheap options, and Mlilwane's rest camp has dorms, huts and campsites. Weekends and South African holidays fill up, so book ahead then.

What to eat

  • Sishwala: stiff maize porridge served with meat or stew
  • Sidvudvu: pumpkin and maize porridge
  • Emasi: thick soured milk, often eaten with maize
  • Umcombotsi: home-brewed sorghum beer

Money

The lilangeni is pegged to the South African rand, and rand is accepted everywhere, so you don't need to change money. Cards work in towns and ATMs are common; tipping around 10 percent is normal in restaurants.

Phone data

MTN and Eswatini Mobile SIMs need passport registration, and coverage is good across most of this small country.

Heads up

Don't spend lilangeni in South Africa, since it isn't accepted there, so use it up before leaving.

Eswatini travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Eswatini cost on a budget?

About $35 a day for one person, from $28 in Manzini to $81 in Mkhaya Game Reserve. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $245 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Eswatini?

April to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Eswatini?

Manzini, at about $28 a day. Commercial hub with a busy market selling Swazi crafts, woven baskets and fresh produce, and a lively bus rank linking the whole country.

How long do you need in Eswatini?

Mbabane, Mlilwane and Hlane. 4 days, easy from South Africa.

How much is $1 in Eswatini?

One US dollar is about 16.65 Swazi lilangeni (SZL), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 333 SZL, and a typical budget day of $35 comes to about 580 SZL.

How do you get around Eswatini cheaply?

Minibus taxis (kombis) link Mbabane, Manzini and the main towns cheaply and leave when full. Reaching Mlilwane and Hlane is easier with a rental car from South Africa, since public transport stops at the main road. Check that your rental agreement allows crossing the border.

What food should you try in Eswatini?
