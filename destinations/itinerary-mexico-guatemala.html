Mexico · Guatemala

Mexico to Guatemala

Mexico City markets, Oaxacan food, Maya ruins and volcano lakes, a long overland trip for travelers who want culture and slow days.

Length31 days
Total, flights included$2,038
All-in per day$66
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 4 · Mexico

    Mexico City 4 nights · $172

    Graze at taco stands, spend a day in the National Anthropology Museum and climb the pyramids at Teotihuacán on a day trip.

    Budget tip: The metro costs 5 pesos a ride. Street tacos are far cheaper than sit-down restaurants and often better.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 5 to 9 · Mexico

    Oaxaca 5 nights · $205

    Mole, mezcal and buzzing markets in the city, plus day trips to the petrified waterfalls of Hierve el Agua and Monte Albán.

    Budget tip: Eat in the Mercado 20 de Noviembre grill hall. ADO night buses save a hostel night between cities.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 10 to 13 · Mexico

    San Cristóbal de las Casas 4 nights · $144

    A cool highland town of colorful colonial streets, with indigenous Tzotzil villages and the cliffs of Sumidero Canyon nearby.

    Budget tip: Colectivos to nearby villages cost about $1. Bring layers, since nights get cold at this altitude.

    Getting here: about $45 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 14 to 15 · Mexico

    Palenque 2 nights · $82

    Maya temples rising from the jungle to the roar of howler monkeys, with the Misol-Ha and Agua Azul waterfalls a short ride away.

    Budget tip: Sleep in the jungle cabañas at El Panchán near the ruins rather than in town. Colectivos shuttle back and forth cheaply.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 16 to 18 · Guatemala

    Flores 3 nights · $93

    A tiny island town of brightly painted houses on Lake Petén Itzá, with lakeside sunsets and cheap hostels near Tikal.

    Budget tip: Use it as your base for Tikal. Swim off the docks for free and take a boat to San Miguel for a short viewpoint walk.

    Getting here: about $40 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 19 to 21 · Guatemala

    Semuc Champey 3 nights · $102

    Swim in stepped turquoise pools over a natural limestone bridge, then tube and wade through caves by candlelight.

    Budget tip: Stay in Lanquín or near the park in jungle lodges. The shuttle road is rough, so travel light.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 22 to 26 · Guatemala

    Antigua 5 nights · $165

    Wander cobbled streets of colonial ruins, then do the overnight Acatenango hike to watch Volcán de Fuego erupt beside you.

    Budget tip: Acatenango tours run about $60 with gear. Rent warm clothes in town, since it freezes at the top.

    Getting here: about $40 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 27 to 31 · Guatemala

    Lake Atitlán 5 nights · $155

    Hop between San Pedro, San Marcos and San Juan by lancha across a volcano-ringed lake, each village with its own character.

    Budget tip: Pay local prices on boats by asking another passenger what they paid first, then hand over exact change.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $700.
