Getting around

First-class buses like ADO are comfortable and easy to book on their apps, and overnight routes save a night's lodging. Budget airlines such as Volaris and Viva Aerobus are often cheap if booked early, and Uber, DiDi and inDrive operate in most cities. The Mexico City Metro is one of the cheapest ways to cross a big city anywhere.

Where to sleep

Hostel dorms are easy to find in Mexico City, Oaxaca and San Cristóbal, and small family-run posadas offer good-value private rooms.

What to eat

  • Tacos al pastor: spit-roasted pork tacos with pineapple and salsa
  • Tlayuda: large crisp Oaxacan tortilla with beans, cheese and meat
  • Mole negro: dark, complex sauce of chilies, spices and chocolate
  • Tamales: steamed corn dough parcels with savory or sweet fillings

Money

Cards work in cities, but you'll need pesos for markets, street food and colectivos, so use bank ATMs and decline the machine's own currency conversion. Tip 10 to 15% in restaurants, and haggle gently only at craft markets.

Phone data

Telcel has the widest coverage and SIMs are sold in convenience stores, though new lines now need ID registration, so an eSIM can save time.

Heads up

Drink bottled or filtered water, and use official taxis or apps rather than hailing cabs on the street at night.

Mexico travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Mexico cost on a budget?

About $41 a day for one person, from $34 in Cuetzalan to $66 in Chichén Itzá. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $287 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Mexico?

October to March is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Mexico?

Cuetzalan, at about $34 a day. Cloud-wrapped streets in the Sierra Norte de Puebla, with Nahua markets, jungle waterfalls and voladores spinning down from a tall pole.

How long do you need in Mexico?

Mexico City, Oaxaca, then San Cristóbal de las Casas. 2 to 3 weeks.

How much is $1 in Mexico?

One US dollar is about 18.19 Mexican peso (MXN), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 364 MXN, and a typical budget day of $41 comes to about 750 MXN.

How do you get around Mexico cheaply?

First-class buses like ADO are comfortable and easy to book on their apps, and overnight routes save a night's lodging. Budget airlines such as Volaris and Viva Aerobus are often cheap if booked early, and Uber, DiDi and inDrive operate in most cities. The Mexico City Metro is one of the cheapest ways to cross a big city anywhere.
