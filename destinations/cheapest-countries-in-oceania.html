Cheapest countries in Oceania

What a typical day costs one person on a budget in 2026, in US dollars: a dorm bed or simple room, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees. Countries under a travel warning are left out.

Whole worldAsiaEuropeAfricaAmericasMiddle EastOceania

  1. Samoa $52/day

    Open-sided beach fale huts with meals included, freshwater swimming holes and traditional Polynesian village life on two green islands.

    Best time: May to October. Cheapest stops: Apia ($46) · Manono Island ($48) · Savai'i ($50)

  2. Fiji $65/day

    Budget island resorts with meals included, kava ceremonies, friendly villages and coral reefs, especially out in the Yasawa Islands.

    Best time: May to October. Cheapest stops: Nadi ($38) · Suva ($41) · Levuka ($42)

  3. Vanuatu $72/day

    An active volcano you can stand on, swimming blue holes and WWII wrecks, spread across friendly Melanesian islands.

    Best time: May to October. Cheapest stops: Port Vila ($55) · Champagne Beach ($60) · Espiritu Santo ($63)

  4. New Zealand $76/day

    Fjords, volcanoes and Great Walks, best seen by campervan with hostels and cheap Department of Conservation campsites in between.

    Best time: November to April. Cheapest stops: Napier ($56) · Christchurch ($58) · Dunedin ($58)

  5. Australia $77/day

    Expensive, but hostels, campervans and working holiday visas make long trips possible, from Sydney's beaches to the Outback and the Reef.

    Best time: March to May and September to November. Cheapest stops: Blue Mountains ($57) · Adelaide ($59) · Melbourne ($61)
