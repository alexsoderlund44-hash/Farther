Peru · Bolivia

Peru and Bolivia Andes

Canyons, Inca sites, Lake Titicaca and the Uyuni salt flats on a high altitude loop for travelers ready for long bus rides.

Length22 days
Total, flights included$2,028
All-in per day$92
Stops9

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 2 · Peru

    Lima 2 nights · $68

    Tangy ceviche, bohemian bars in Barranco and clifftop walks above the Pacific in Miraflores, in South America's food capital.

    Budget tip: Set lunch menus cost about $4. The Metropolitano bus is cheaper than taxis for crossing the city.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 3 to 5 · Peru

    Arequipa 3 nights · $90

    Explore the white volcanic stone of the old city and Santa Catalina monastery, then trek deep into Colca Canyon to spot condors.

    Budget tip: Do Colca Canyon on your own by public bus to Cabanaconde, and stay in cheap guesthouses in the canyon.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 6 to 7 · Peru

    Colca Canyon 2 nights · $70

    Andean condors soar over one of the world's deepest canyons, and a trek drops down to the palm-shaded Sangalle oasis.

    Budget tip: Buy the tourist ticket at the entrance. The trek is easy to do without a guide.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 8 to 11 · Peru

    Cusco 4 nights · $140

    Run your hands over Inca stonework, explore the Sacred Valley and take a day trip to the striped slopes of Rainbow Mountain.

    Budget tip: Menu del día lunches are about $3. Spend 2 days acclimatising before trekking to avoid altitude sickness.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 12 to 12 · Peru

    Machu Picchu 1 night · $100

    The Inca citadel on a ridge high above the Urubamba valley, wrapped in cloud forest and morning mist.

    Budget tip: Tickets sell out, so book early. Walk the tracks from Hidroeléctrica to Aguas Calientes instead of taking the expensive train.

    Getting here: about $90 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 13 to 14 · Peru

    Puno 2 nights · $66

    Lake Titicaca's floating Uros reed islands, knitting men on Taquile and nights with island families in homestays on Amantaní.

    Budget tip: Book homestays through local cooperatives rather than agencies, so more of your money reaches the families.

    Getting here: about $95 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 15 to 16 · Bolivia

    Copacabana 2 nights · $40

    A lakeside pilgrimage town on Lake Titicaca, with sunset views from Cerro Calvario and boats out to Isla del Sol.

    Budget tip: Eat fresh trout at the beach stalls for a few dollars, far cheaper than the restaurants on the main street.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 17 to 19 · Bolivia

    La Paz 3 nights · $75

    Glide over the city by cable car, browse the witches' market for dried llama fetuses and cycle Death Road down to the jungle.

    Budget tip: Mi Teleférico cable cars cost about $0.50 a ride. Market lunches are $2 for soup and a main course.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  9. Days 20 to 22 · Bolivia

    Uyuni Salt Flat 3 nights · $171

    The 3-day jeep tour crosses the blinding white salt flat to red and green flamingo lagoons near the Chilean border.

    Budget tip: Compare agencies in Uyuni town before booking. 3-day tours run $150 to $200 all-in, including meals and basic lodging.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $900.
