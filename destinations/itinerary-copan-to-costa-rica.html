Honduras · El Salvador · Nicaragua · Costa Rica

Copan to Costa Rica

Maya ruins at Copan, Salvadoran surf towns, Nicaragua's colonial cities and lake volcano, then Costa Rican cloud forest, a steady overland trip through five countries.

Length24 days
Total, flights included$1,862
All-in per day$78
Stops9

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 2 · Honduras

    Copán Ruinas 2 nights · $66

    Intricately carved Mayan stelae, a hieroglyphic stairway and a macaw sanctuary next to a cobbled colonial town.

    Budget tip: An easy shuttle ride from Antigua, Guatemala. Check current travel advice for elsewhere in the country.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 3 to 4 · El Salvador

    Santa Ana 2 nights · $62

    A Gothic cathedral, a grand old theater and the base for hiking Santa Ana volcano to its sulfur-green crater lake.

    Budget tip: The volcano hike starts from Cerro Verde with a required guide, usually in the morning. Bring cash for the park and guide fees.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 5 to 7 · El Salvador

    El Tunco 3 nights · $102

    Black-sand surf town on the Pacific with famous sunsets behind its rock formation, cheap hostels and lively beach bars.

    Budget tip: Board rental costs about $10 a day. Beginners can find gentler waves at nearby El Sunzal.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 8 to 10 · Nicaragua

    León 3 nights · $81

    Sled down the black slopes of Cerro Negro on a volcano boarding trip, then walk on the white roof of the cathedral.

    Budget tip: Book volcano boarding through a hostel. Fritanga street stalls serve cheap grilled dinners.

    Getting here: about $45 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 11 to 13 · Nicaragua

    Granada 3 nights · $78

    A colorful colonial town, kayaking among the isletas of Lake Nicaragua and a night look at Masaya volcano's glowing lava.

    Budget tip: Chicken buses to Masaya cost under $1 and leave regularly from near the market.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 14 to 16 · Nicaragua

    Ometepe 3 nights · $84

    Twin volcanoes rise out of Lake Nicaragua on this island of beaches, farm stays and jungle waterfalls.

    Budget tip: Rent a scooter to get around. Climbing Concepción takes a full day and a guide.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 17 to 18 · Nicaragua

    San Juan del Sur 2 nights · $66

    A lively surf town on a horseshoe bay, with daily shuttles to the breaks at Maderas and Remanso.

    Budget tip: Stay in town and ride the surf shuttle, which is much cheaper than a beachside hotel.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 19 to 21 · Costa Rica

    Monteverde 3 nights · $207

    Misty cloud forest reserves, resplendent quetzals, hummingbirds and night walks in search of tarantulas, frogs and sleeping sloths.

    Budget tip: Night tours are often the best wildlife value. Take the public bus up the mountain instead of a private shuttle.

    Getting here: about $40 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  9. Days 22 to 24 · Costa Rica

    La Fortuna 3 nights · $192

    Arenal Volcano's near-perfect cone, hanging bridges through the rainforest canopy and free hot river pools where locals soak after dark.

    Budget tip: Soak in the free hot river near Tabacón instead of paying for resort springs. Casados at local sodas are cheap, filling meals.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $750.
