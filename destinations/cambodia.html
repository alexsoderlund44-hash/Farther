Getting around

Buses and minivans connect Siem Reap, Phnom Penh and Kampot, and tuk-tuks handle short trips. Grab and PassApp work in the main cities and save haggling over fares. Bus tickets are easy to buy a day ahead through guesthouses or online.

Where to sleep

Hostel dorms and guesthouses are cheap in Siem Reap and Phnom Penh, and Kampot has riverside bungalows.

What to eat

  • Fish amok: fish steamed in coconut curry with lemongrass
  • Lok lak: stir-fried beef with a lime and pepper dip
  • Num banh chok: rice noodles with fish curry, a breakfast staple
  • Kuy teav: pork broth noodle soup eaten in the morning

Money

US dollars are used for most purchases, with riel given as small change, and ATMs dispense dollars but charge a fee per withdrawal. Tipping is appreciated but not required, and haggling is normal at markets.

Phone data

SIMs from Smart, Cellcard or Metfone are cheap and need your passport, and coverage is good in towns and along main roads.

Heads up

Avoid orphanage visits and volunteering with children, which can fuel harm to the kids they claim to help.

Cambodia travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Cambodia cost on a budget?

About $29 a day for one person, from $21 in Kampong Cham to $58 in Virachey National Park. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $203 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Cambodia?

November to March is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Cambodia?

Kampong Cham, at about $21 a day. Mekong town with the Angkorian-era Wat Nokor temple, the twin hills of Phnom Pros and Phnom Srei and rural Koh Paen island.

How long do you need in Cambodia?

Siem Reap, bus to Phnom Penh, then down to Kampot. 10 to 14 days.

How much is $1 in Cambodia?

One US dollar is about 4,053 US dollar and riel (KHR), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 81,069 KHR, and a typical budget day of $29 comes to about 120,000 KHR.

How do you get around Cambodia cheaply?

Buses and minivans connect Siem Reap, Phnom Penh and Kampot, and tuk-tuks handle short trips. Grab and PassApp work in the main cities and save haggling over fares. Bus tickets are easy to buy a day ahead through guesthouses or online.

What food should you try in Cambodia?

Fish amok, fish steamed in coconut curry with lemongrass. Lok lak, stir-fried beef with a lime and pepper dip. Num banh chok, rice noodles with fish curry, a breakfast staple. Kuy teav, pork broth noodle soup eaten in the morning.
