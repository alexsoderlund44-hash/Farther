Malaysia · Singapore

Malaysia and Singapore

Street food in Penang and Kuala Lumpur, tea hills in the Cameron Highlands, island time on the Perhentians and a final splurge in Singapore.

Length20 days
Total, flights included$1,775
All-in per day$89
Stops7

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Malaysia

    Kuala Lumpur 3 nights · $78

    The Petronas Towers, the rainbow steps of Batu Caves and sizzling satay on Jalan Alor make KL a great city for street food on a budget.

    Budget tip: Free Go KL buses cover the centre. Batu Caves is free to enter.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 5 · Malaysia

    Cameron Highlands 2 nights · $52

    Rolling tea plantations, mossy forest trails and cool mountain air make a welcome break from the lowland heat.

    Budget tip: Hike the numbered jungle trails yourself instead of joining a tour, and visit tea estates for free.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  3. Days 6 to 9 · Malaysia

    George Town, Penang 4 nights · $104

    Eat char kway teow at hawker stalls, hunt for street art murals and explore clan houses in George Town's colonial-era old town.

    Budget tip: Eat at hawker centres like Gurney Drive. The free CAT bus loops the old town.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  4. Days 10 to 13 · Malaysia

    Perhentian Islands 4 nights · $156

    Snorkel in clear water with sea turtles and blacktip reef sharks off white sand beaches.

    Budget tip: Long Beach on Kecil is the budget side. Islands close in the monsoon, roughly November to February.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  5. Days 14 to 15 · Malaysia

    Taman Negara 2 nights · $66

    Explore one of the world's oldest rainforests on a canopy walkway, jungle treks and overnight stays in wildlife hides.

    Budget tip: Stay in Kuala Tahan across the river from the park. Floating restaurants serve cheap meals.

    Getting here: about $18 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  6. Days 16 to 17 · Malaysia

    Melaka 2 nights · $46

    Dutch and Portuguese buildings, Peranakan shophouses and the Jonker Street night market fill this historic trading port.

    Budget tip: Try chicken rice balls and cendol. Walk the river path at night when it is lit up.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  7. Days 18 to 20 · Singapore

    Marina Bay 3 nights · $183

    Glowing Supertrees, the nightly light show over Marina Bay and a waterfront promenade show off Singapore's futuristic skyline.

    Budget tip: The Gardens by the Bay outdoor gardens and the Supertree light show are free. Skip the paid conservatories if money is tight.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,000.
