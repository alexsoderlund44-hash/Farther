Getting around

Intercity buses link Harare, Bulawayo, Masvingo and Victoria Falls, and kombis, shared minibuses, cover shorter routes. Getting into Hwange usually means a tour or a rental car. Book activities at Victoria Falls ahead in peak season.

Where to sleep

Victoria Falls has hostels and campsites, and Hwange has park campsites and chalets. Great Zimbabwe has a campsite and basic lodges nearby.

What to eat

  • Sadza: stiff maize porridge, the daily staple
  • Nyama: stewed or grilled meat served with sadza
  • Matemba: small dried fish fried with tomato
  • Mazoe: sweet orange cordial, a local classic

Money

US dollars in cash are used for most things, so bring plenty of small notes, as change is often short or given in ZiG. Cards work at bigger hotels and tour companies, and tips are appreciated.

Phone data

Econet and NetOne SIMs need your passport, and coverage is good in cities and tourist areas.

Heads up

Don't photograph government buildings, police or soldiers.

Zimbabwe travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Zimbabwe cost on a budget?

About $64 a day for one person, from $34 in Bvumba Mountains to $85 in Mana Pools. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $448 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Zimbabwe?

May to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Zimbabwe?

Bvumba Mountains, at about $34 a day. Misty highlands near Mutare, with botanical gardens, forest birding, tea rooms and views across the Mozambican plains.

How long do you need in Zimbabwe?

Victoria Falls, Hwange and Great Zimbabwe. 10 days.

How much is $1 in Zimbabwe?

One US dollar is about 26.77 US dollar and ZiG (ZWG), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 535 ZWG, and a typical budget day of $64 comes to about 1,700 ZWG.

How do you get around Zimbabwe cheaply?

Intercity buses link Harare, Bulawayo, Masvingo and Victoria Falls, and kombis, shared minibuses, cover shorter routes. Getting into Hwange usually means a tour or a rental car. Book activities at Victoria Falls ahead in peak season.

What food should you try in Zimbabwe?

Sadza, stiff maize porridge, the daily staple. Nyama, stewed or grilled meat served with sadza. Matemba, small dried fish fried with tomato. Mazoe, sweet orange cordial, a local classic.
