Italy vs Greece: which is cheaper?

Greece is the cheaper of the two, at about $61 a day against $87 in Italy, which is roughly 30% less each day. Two weeks costs about $854 in Greece and $1,218 in Italy, before flights. Both are at their best in May to June and September to October, so you could combine them on one trip.
