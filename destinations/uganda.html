Track mountain gorillas in Bwindi, raft the Nile near its source at Jinja and swim in Fort Portal's crater lakes. Uganda is green, friendly and good value outside the permit fees.

Getting around

Minibus taxis and big buses connect towns cheaply, and boda bodas, motorbike taxis, handle short hops. In Kampala, SafeBoda, Uber and Bolt give fixed prices. Gorilla permits for Bwindi must be booked well ahead through the Uganda Wildlife Authority or an agent.

Where to sleep

Kampala, Jinja and Fort Portal have hostels with dorms and camping, and many parks have campsites or simple bandas. Cheap rooms near Bwindi are limited, so book ahead.

What to eat

  • Rolex: omelet rolled in chapati, a street food favorite
  • Matoke: steamed green plantain, often mashed with sauce
  • Luwombo: meat or groundnut stew steamed in banana leaves
  • Groundnut sauce: rich peanut sauce served with matoke or rice

Money

Carry shillings in cash, since cards work mainly in Kampala and lodges, and ATMs are found in towns. Haggle with boda drivers and at markets, and tip guides and porters on treks.

Phone data

MTN and Airtel SIMs need passport registration at a shop, and coverage is good across most of the country.

Heads up

Insist on a helmet when riding a boda boda, as road crashes are common.

Uganda travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Uganda cost on a budget?

About $37 a day for one person, from $25 in Lake Bunyonyi to $120 in Rwenzori Mountains. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $259 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Uganda?

June to August and December to February is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Uganda?

Lake Bunyonyi, at about $25 a day. A calm lake with 29 islands, steep terraced hills and dugout canoes gliding through the morning mist.

How long do you need in Uganda?

Jinja, Murchison Falls, Fort Portal, then Bwindi for gorillas. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in Uganda?

One US dollar is about 3,933 Ugandan shilling (UGX), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 78,659 UGX, and a typical budget day of $37 comes to about 150,000 UGX.

How do you get around Uganda cheaply?

Minibus taxis and big buses connect towns cheaply, and boda bodas, motorbike taxis, handle short hops. In Kampala, SafeBoda, Uber and Bolt give fixed prices. Gorilla permits for Bwindi must be booked well ahead through the Uganda Wildlife Authority or an agent.

What food should you try in Uganda?

Rolex, omelet rolled in chapati, a street food favorite. Matoke, steamed green plantain, often mashed with sauce. Luwombo, meat or groundnut stew steamed in banana leaves. Groundnut sauce, rich peanut sauce served with matoke or rice.
