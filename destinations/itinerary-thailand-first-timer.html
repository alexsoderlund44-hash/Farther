Thailand

Thailand in Two Weeks

Bangkok temples, an old royal capital, Chiang Mai's night markets and two Andaman beaches, the classic easy start for new travelers.

Length14 days
Total, flights included$1,535
All-in per day$110
Stops5

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Thailand

    Bangkok 3 nights · $105

    Hop between riverside temples by ferry, graze on street food in Chinatown at night and lose a morning in the weekend Chatuchak market.

    Budget tip: Orange-flag river ferries cost under $1 and beat the traffic. Politely skip tuk-tuk drivers offering 'gem tours', which are a well-known scam.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 4 · Thailand

    Ayutthaya 1 night · $33

    Wander the brick ruins of the old Siamese capital, where a stone Buddha head peers out of fig tree roots at Wat Mahathat.

    Budget tip: The train from Bangkok costs under $1, making this a cheap day trip. Rent a bike to get between the ruins.

    Getting here: about $2 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  3. Days 5 to 8 · Thailand

    Chiang Mai 4 nights · $132

    Wander golden temples in the moated old city, eat khao soi at the Sunday walking street and ride a songthaew up to Doi Suthep.

    Budget tip: Shared red songthaews cost about 30 baht around town. Avoid burning season in March and April, when smoke haze is heavy.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  4. Days 9 to 11 · Thailand

    Ao Nang 3 nights · $114

    Krabi's beach strip and launch point for longtail boats to Railay, island-hopping to Hong and Poda islands and limestone sea cliffs.

    Budget tip: Longtail fares are posted at the beach ticket booth; Krabi Town has cheaper rooms, linked to Ao Nang by white songthaews.

    Getting here: about $45 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  5. Days 12 to 14 · Thailand

    Koh Lanta 3 nights · $117

    Long, quiet beaches and laid-back bungalows, cheaper than Phi Phi, with snorkel trips out to the reefs of Koh Rok.

    Budget tip: Stay on the west coast in shoulder season for half-price beach bungalows. Book snorkel trips locally rather than online.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $950.
