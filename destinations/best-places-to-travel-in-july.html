Where to go in July

Cheap places to travel in July

57 countries with good weather in July, cheapest first. Daily budgets are for one person in US dollars.

JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec

  1. Kyrgyzstan $24/day

    Wild alpine lakes, nomad yurts, horse treks and free camping almost anywhere, with cheap guesthouses and some of the best hiking in Central Asia.

    Cheapest stops: Osh ($19) · Ala-Kul ($22) · Bishkek ($23)

  2. Bolivia $25/day

    The cheapest country in South America, with high-altitude cities, the Uyuni salt flat, Amazon jungle and $2 market lunches.

    Cheapest stops: Copacabana ($20) · Sucre ($21) · Cochabamba ($21)

  3. Malawi $25/day

    The 'warm heart of Africa', with a freshwater lake as big as a sea, cheap lakeside lodges and few other travelers.

    Cheapest stops: Zomba Plateau ($21) · Nkhata Bay ($23) · Livingstonia ($23)

  4. Malaysia $26/day

    Street-food capitals, cool tea highlands, one of the world's oldest rainforests and easy-going islands, all linked by cheap buses and hawker centres.

    Cheapest stops: Ipoh ($21) · Melaka ($23) · George Town, Penang ($26)

  5. Kosovo $28/day

    Europe's youngest country, with Ottoman towns, strong macchiato, mountain trails and very low prices.

    Cheapest stops: Prizren ($28) · Pristina ($28) · Peja ($28)

  6. Ecuador $29/day

    Volcanoes, cloud forest, Andean markets and the Amazon in a country you can cross in a day, with cheap buses and set lunches everywhere.

    Cheapest stops: Otavalo ($24) · Vilcabamba ($24) · Cajas National Park ($24)

  7. Georgia $30/day

    Caucasus peaks, cheap natural wine, cheesy khachapuri and guesthouses run by famously generous hosts. Georgia on a budget is one of Europe's best deals.

    Cheapest stops: Chiatura ($25) · Mtskheta ($26) · Borjomi ($28)

  8. Bulgaria $31/day

    Roman ruins, painted monasteries, mountain lakes and some of the EU's cheapest food, from banitsa breakfasts to shopska salad.

    Cheapest stops: Veliko Tarnovo ($30) · Sofia ($31) · Plovdiv ($31)

  9. North Macedonia $32/day

    A lake older than most on Earth, Ottoman bazaars, mountain villages and almost no crowds, with cheap burek and private rooms.

    Cheapest stops: Tetovo ($28) · Skopje ($29) · Bitola ($29)

  10. Paraguay $32/day

    South America's least-visited country, with Jesuit mission ruins, tereré on every street corner and the wild Chaco, all very cheap.

    Cheapest stops: Asunción ($27) · Encarnación ($27) · Areguá ($27)

  11. Tajikistan $33/day

    The Pamir Highway, turquoise mountain lakes and homestay hospitality in one of the least-visited countries in Asia, where travel is cheap but rugged.

    Cheapest stops: Istaravshan ($23) · Dushanbe ($25) · Khujand ($25)

  12. Bosnia and Herzegovina $33/day

    Ottoman bazaars, emerald rivers and mountain villages at some of Europe's lowest prices, with ćevapi, strong coffee and welcoming guesthouses.

    Cheapest stops: Travnik ($30) · Banja Luka ($31) · Jajce ($32)

  13. Peru $35/day

    Inca ruins, high Andes treks and some of the best food in South America, with menu del día lunches and cheap hostels keeping Peru affordable on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Ayacucho ($25) · Huanchaco ($28) · Arequipa ($30)

  14. Colombia $35/day

    Friendly cities, coffee country, cloud forest and the Caribbean coast at budget prices, with good hostels and filling set lunches almost everywhere.

    Cheapest stops: Popayán ($27) · Cali ($28) · San Agustín ($29)

  15. Serbia $35/day

    Nightlife, river fortresses and some of Europe's lowest prices, with hearty grilled food and cheap hostels in Belgrade.

    Cheapest stops: Niš ($31) · Subotica ($31) · Sremski Karlovci ($32)

  16. Eswatini $35/day

    Eswatini is a small kingdom of game reserves, mountain hikes and cultural festivals, easy to reach from South Africa and cheap to explore on foot or by bike.

    Cheapest stops: Manzini ($28) · Sibebe Rock ($31) · Ezulwini Valley ($33)

  17. Indonesia $37/day

    Volcano hikes, ancient temples, rice terraces and island-hopping across thousands of islands, with cheap warungs and homestays almost everywhere you go.

    Cheapest stops: Lake Toba ($22) · Bukittinggi ($23) · Yogyakarta ($25)

  18. Kazakhstan $37/day

    Modern Almaty backed by the snowy Tian Shan, with canyons, alpine lakes and cheap homestays a short drive away.

    Cheapest stops: Turkistan ($28) · Shymkent ($28) · Big Almaty Lake ($30)

  19. Romania $37/day

    Medieval Saxon towns, Carpathian hikes, painted wooden churches and villages where time seems to have stopped, all at low prices.

    Cheapest stops: Timișoara ($34) · Cluj-Napoca ($35) · Sibiu ($36)

  20. Timor-Leste $37/day

    One of Asia's youngest and least-visited countries, with world-class coral reefs, mountain villages and quiet beaches free of crowds.

    Cheapest stops: Maubisse ($32) · Mount Ramelau ($34) · Baucau ($34)

  21. Kenya $37/day

    Classic safari country on a budget, with cheap camping safaris to the Maasai Mara, cycling among zebras at Hell's Gate and a laid-back Swahili coast in Lamu and Diani.

    Cheapest stops: Iten ($25) · Mombasa ($30) · Kakamega Forest ($31)

  22. Uganda $37/day

    Track mountain gorillas in Bwindi, raft the Nile near its source at Jinja and swim in Fort Portal's crater lakes. Uganda is green, friendly and good value outside the permit fees.

    Cheapest stops: Lake Bunyonyi ($25) · Fort Portal crater lakes ($27) · Sipi Falls ($27)

  23. Brazil $42/day

    Beaches, rainforest and waterfalls on a huge scale, with good-value hostels, pay-by-weight buffet restaurants and long-distance buses.

    Cheapest stops: Belém ($32) · São Luís ($32) · Olinda ($33)

  24. Mongolia $43/day

    Endless steppe, nomad gers, horse treks and the Gobi Desert, best seen on a shared multi-day jeep tour that splits the cost of driver, food and fuel.

    Cheapest stops: Ulaanbaatar ($26) · Terelj National Park ($33) · Kharkhorin ($33)

  25. Slovakia $45/day

    Castles and big mountains at Central Europe's lower prices, with mountain huts, cheap trains and some of the region's best hiking.

    Cheapest stops: Bardejov ($36) · Košice ($37) · Vlkolínec ($39)

  26. Lithuania $46/day

    Baroque Vilnius, a red-brick island castle and the sand dunes of the Curonian Spit, all at friendly Baltic prices.

    Cheapest stops: Hill of Crosses ($38) · Kaunas ($39) · Klaipėda ($39)

  27. Latvia $46/day

    Art nouveau Riga, Baltic beaches at Jūrmala and the forests and castles of Gauja National Park, all on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Daugavpils ($36) · Liepāja ($39) · Ventspils ($40)

  28. Poland $48/day

    Big-city culture, mountain hikes and Baltic beaches at a fraction of Western European prices, with cheap hostels, pierogi and milk bars.

    Cheapest stops: Lublin ($37) · Zamość ($37) · Toruń ($40)

  29. Angola $48/day

    Angola is one of Africa's least-touristed countries, with the huge Kalandula Falls, the Tundavala cliffs near Lubango, the Serra da Leba road and a wild desert coast.

    Cheapest stops: Benguela ($42) · Lubango ($43) · Baía Azul ($43)

  30. Mauritius $50/day

    Mauritius is cheaper than its resort image, with turquoise lagoons, the Le Morne hike, rainforest trails in Black River Gorges and Port Louis street food like dholl puri.

    Cheapest stops: Curepipe ($40) · Mahébourg ($41) · Pamplemousses ($44)

  31. Czechia $51/day

    Fairytale old towns, cheap Czech beer and sandstone national parks, an easy and affordable trip from Prague.

    Cheapest stops: Olomouc ($41) · Plzeň ($44) · Český ráj ($44)

  32. Samoa $52/day

    Open-sided beach fale huts with meals included, freshwater swimming holes and traditional Polynesian village life on two green islands.

    Cheapest stops: Apia ($46) · Manono Island ($48) · Savai'i ($50)

  33. Singapore $53/day

    A pricey city-state where hawker centres, free gardens and a cheap MRT keep a short stopover on a budget surprisingly affordable.

    Cheapest stops: Little India, Singapore ($49) · Kampong Glam ($49) · MacRitchie Reservoir ($50)

  34. Estonia $53/day

    A medieval walled capital, boardwalk bog walks and windmill-dotted islands, cheaper than its Nordic neighbors.

    Cheapest stops: Tartu ($44) · Viljandi ($44) · Narva ($45)

  35. Mozambique $54/day

    Mozambique has long Indian Ocean beaches, whale sharks off Tofo, dhow trips to the Bazaruto islands and the historic coral-stone town of Ilha de Moçambique.

    Cheapest stops: Inhambane ($29) · Gurúè ($31) · Xai-Xai ($31)

  36. Slovenia $62/day

    Alpine lakes, show caves and a green little capital, easy to see in a week by bus with hostels and guesthouses everywhere.

    Cheapest stops: Ptuj ($50) · Idrija ($55) · Ljubljana ($56)

  37. Germany $63/day

    Big-city culture at fair prices, fairytale castles, beer gardens and cheap regional train tickets that make Germany easy on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Leipzig ($52) · Quedlinburg ($53) · Saxon Switzerland ($55)

  38. Zimbabwe $64/day

    Zimbabwe has the best views of Victoria Falls, the medieval stone city of Great Zimbabwe and some of Africa's finest walking safaris along the Zambezi at Mana Pools.

    Cheapest stops: Bvumba Mountains ($34) · Chinhoyi Caves ($34) · Bulawayo ($35)

  39. Fiji $65/day

    Budget island resorts with meals included, kava ceremonies, friendly villages and coral reefs, especially out in the Yasawa Islands.

    Cheapest stops: Nadi ($38) · Suva ($41) · Levuka ($42)

  40. São Tomé and Príncipe $67/day

    São Tomé and Príncipe are tiny cocoa islands on the equator, with jungle peaks like Pico Cão Grande, old plantation houses, nesting turtles and empty beaches.

    Cheapest stops: Roça Agostinho Neto ($51) · São Tomé Island ($52) · São João dos Angolares ($52)

  41. Austria $69/day

    Imperial Vienna, Alpine lakes and hut-to-hut hiking, with cheap opera tickets and good regional trains.

    Cheapest stops: Lake Neusiedl ($56) · Graz ($58) · Gesäuse National Park ($58)

  42. Namibia $70/day

    Red dunes at Sossusvlei, rhinos at Etosha's waterholes and long empty roads. Namibia is best seen on a budget by splitting a rental car and camping under huge desert skies.

    Cheapest stops: Windhoek ($38) · Swakopmund ($46) · Walvis Bay ($51)

  43. Vanuatu $72/day

    An active volcano you can stand on, swimming blue holes and WWII wrecks, spread across friendly Melanesian islands.

    Cheapest stops: Port Vila ($55) · Champagne Beach ($60) · Espiritu Santo ($63)

  44. Finland $73/day

    Saunas, lakes and Lapland's northern lights, best done on a budget with free camping, wilderness huts and cooking your own meals.

    Cheapest stops: Nuuksio ($59) · Porvoo ($64) · Rauma ($64)

  45. Ireland $75/day

    Sea cliffs, green valleys and live music in pubs, with high prices offset by hostels, cheap buses and free museums.

    Cheapest stops: Achill Island ($69) · Cork ($70) · Wicklow Mountains ($70)

  46. Canada $76/day

    Turquoise Rocky Mountain lakes, French-speaking Montreal and Quebec City, and huge wilderness, with hostels and campgrounds keeping costs down.

    Cheapest stops: Quebec City ($67) · Ottawa ($67) · Montreal ($68)

  47. United Kingdom $80/day

    Pricey, but free museums, hostels, long-distance trails and cheap advance train and coach tickets make the UK doable on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Glasgow ($61) · Liverpool ($62) · Peak District ($64)

  48. Botswana $82/day

    Botswana is high-end safari country, but budget mokoro trips into the Okavango Delta, self-drive camping in Chobe and tours to the salt pans keep it within reach.

    Cheapest stops: Maun ($37) · Kasane ($41) · Gaborone ($41)

  49. Sweden $84/day

    Archipelagos, forests and right-to-roam camping in a pricey country, made affordable with lunch deals and free nature.

    Cheapest stops: Uppsala ($67) · Öland ($67) · Lund ($67)

  50. Denmark $84/day

    Bikes, harbor swims and Danish design in an expensive but easy country, with free nature shelters for camping.

    Cheapest stops: Rømø ($75) · Ribe ($76) · Odense ($77)

  51. Zambia $85/day

    Zambia offers wild walking safaris in South Luangwa and the other side of Victoria Falls at Livingstone, with rafting, Devil's Pool swims and cheap camps by the parks.

    Cheapest stops: Lusaka ($35) · Siavonga ($35) · Mpulungu ($38)

  52. Gabon $85/day

    In Gabon rainforest meets the ocean, and forest elephants walk on beaches. Wildlife at Loango and Lopé is spectacular, though it is one of Africa's pricier places to travel.

    Cheapest stops: Lambaréné ($55) · Libreville ($65) · Pongara National Park ($80)

  53. Rwanda $88/day

    Clean, compact and green, Rwanda packs gorillas, volcanoes, chimp forests and lakeside towns into short bus rides, with Kigali's calm, hilly streets as an easy, safe base.

    Cheapest stops: Huye ($25) · Nyanza ($28) · Kigali ($29)

  54. Norway $94/day

    The most dramatic fjords anywhere, at very high prices, so camping, cooking and free hiking help a lot on a budget.

    Cheapest stops: Odda ($84) · Røros ($84) · Trondheim ($87)

  55. Iceland $115/day

    Waterfalls, glaciers and hot rivers, very expensive but possible on a budget with campsites, supermarkets and road trips.

    Cheapest stops: Landmannalaugar ($90) · Akureyri ($94) · Skaftafell ($96)

  56. Switzerland $117/day

    The most expensive country in Europe, but the hiking is free, the trains are scenic and Coop and Migros supermarkets keep food affordable.

    Cheapest stops: Bern ($92) · Valle Verzasca ($94) · Appenzell ($96)

  57. Tanzania $190/day

    Climb Kilimanjaro, watch the Serengeti's great herds and wander Zanzibar's Stone Town. Park fees are the main cost, so shared camping safaris from Arusha keep Tanzania affordable.

    Cheapest stops: Arusha ($25) · Moshi ($26) · Bagamoyo ($27)
