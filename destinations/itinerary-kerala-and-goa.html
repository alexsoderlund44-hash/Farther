India

Kerala and Goa

Houseboats on the Kerala backwaters, tea hills in Munnar, cliff-top Varkala and the ruins of Hampi, ending on Goa's beaches.

Length20 days
Total, flights included$1,525
All-in per day$76
Stops6

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · India

    Fort Kochi 3 nights · $69

    Chinese fishing nets on the waterfront, Jew Town's antique shops and synagogue, Kathakali performances and fresh Kerala fish curry on banana leaves.

    Budget tip: Public ferries to Ernakulam cost a few rupees and beat the bridge traffic. Book Kathakali shows early to watch the make-up being applied.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 6 · India

    Munnar 3 nights · $75

    Rolling tea estates carpeting the Western Ghats, mist over Eravikulam National Park's Nilgiri tahr and spice gardens heavy with cardamom.

    Budget tip: Kerala state buses connect from Kochi cheaply. Hire a jeep only for distant viewpoints and walk the estate roads near town for free.

    Getting here: about $4 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  3. Days 7 to 8 · India

    Alappuzha 2 nights · $54

    Glide through Kerala's backwaters past palm-lined canals, rice paddies and wooden houseboats, then eat fish curry on a banana leaf.

    Budget tip: Take the public ferry to Kottayam for under $1 instead of hiring a houseboat. It follows the same waterways.

    Getting here: about $5 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  4. Days 9 to 12 · India

    Varkala 4 nights · $92

    Red laterite cliffs above a long sandy beach, clifftop cafés serving grilled seafood, surf lessons and the ancient Janardhana Swamy Temple.

    Budget tip: Guesthouses a few lanes back from the cliff are much cheaper. Trains from Thiruvananthapuram take under an hour and cost very little.

    Getting here: about $4 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  5. Days 13 to 15 · India

    Hampi 3 nights · $57

    Vijayanagara temple ruins lie scattered among giant golden boulders and banana fields, best seen from a hilltop at sunset.

    Budget tip: Cross the river to Hampi Island for cheap guesthouses. Rent a bicycle or scooter to reach the outlying temples.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  6. Days 16 to 20 · India

    Palolem 5 nights · $125

    Crescent bay in South Goa lined with palms and beach huts, calm swimming water, kayaking to Butterfly Beach and silent disco nights.

    Budget tip: Huts are cheapest in November and after February; rent a scooter to reach quieter Agonda and Patnem beaches nearby rather than paying for taxis.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,000.
