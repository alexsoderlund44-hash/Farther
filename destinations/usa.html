Know before you go

United States on a budget: the basics

Currency

United States uses the US dollar

No exchange needed. Bring clean, untorn notes, since small shops often refuse damaged bills.

Getting around

For the Southwest, renting a car is by far the most practical option, and splitting it with others keeps costs down. In New York, the subway is cheap and runs all night, and Uber and Lyft work almost everywhere. Book national park campsites and Zion or Grand Canyon lodging months ahead, and check whether a park needs a timed-entry reservation.

Where to sleep

Hostels exist mainly in big cities like New York and Las Vegas, and on the road, budget motels and park campgrounds are the cheapest beds.

What to eat

  • New York pizza: thin, foldable slice sold by the piece
  • Bagel: boiled then baked bread ring, often with cream cheese
  • Breakfast burrito: tortilla with eggs, potatoes, chili and cheese
  • Navajo frybread: puffy fried dough, served sweet or as a taco

Money

Cards and phone payments are accepted almost everywhere. Tipping 18 to 20% in sit-down restaurants and a dollar or two per drink at bars is expected, as listed prices exclude sales tax.

Phone data

Prepaid SIMs from T-Mobile, AT&T or Verizon need no registration and eSIMs are easy, but expect no signal in many canyons and remote parks.

Heads up

Medical care is extremely expensive, so travel insurance is essential, and carry plenty of water when hiking in desert heat.

United States travel: quick answers

How much does a day in United States cost on a budget?

About $95 a day for one person, from $74 in Las Vegas to $116 in Denali. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $665 before flights.

When is the best time to visit United States?

April to June and September to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in United States?

Las Vegas, at about $74 a day. Neon Strip of themed casinos and free fountain shows, the Neon Museum boneyard and nearby desert hiking at Red Rock Canyon.
