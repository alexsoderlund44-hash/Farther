Getting around

Tap-taps (shared pickups and minibuses) are the main public transport, but road travel between regions has been disrupted by armed groups and is not considered safe. Flights in and out of the country have been suspended at times, so any visit depends on current security advice and reliable local contacts. Ride-hailing apps are not a practical option.

Where to sleep

Lodging is mostly small hotels and guesthouses, and anyone visiting relies on places with good security and local hosts who follow the situation daily.

What to eat

  • Griot: marinated pork chunks, fried until crisp
  • Diri ak djon djon: rice cooked black with dried mushrooms
  • Soup joumou: squash soup eaten on Independence Day, January 1
  • Pikliz: spicy pickled cabbage and pepper relish

Money

Cash is essential, with US dollars widely used alongside gourdes, and card payments and ATMs are limited outside a few hotels and banks. Prices are often quoted in Haitian dollars, a unit equal to five gourdes, so confirm which currency is meant.

Phone data

Digicel and Natcom are the two carriers, and SIMs need ID registration, while network outages can happen during unrest.

Heads up

Drink only bottled or treated water, as cholera has returned in recent years.

Haiti travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Haiti cost on a budget?

About $55 a day for one person, from $44 in Jacmel to $61 in Île-à-Vache. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $385 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Haiti?

November to March is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Haiti?

Jacmel, at about $44 a day. A seaside arts town known for colorful papier-mâché carnival masks, craft workshops and old merchant houses.

How long do you need in Haiti?

Cap-Haïtien and the Citadelle. 5 days, if travel becomes safe.

How much is $1 in Haiti?

One US dollar is about 131 Haitian gourde (HTG), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 2,619 HTG, and a typical budget day of $55 comes to about 7,200 HTG.

How do you get around Haiti cheaply?

Tap-taps (shared pickups and minibuses) are the main public transport, but road travel between regions has been disrupted by armed groups and is not considered safe. Flights in and out of the country have been suspended at times, so any visit depends on current security advice and reliable local contacts. Ride-hailing apps are not a practical option.

What food should you try in Haiti?

Griot, marinated pork chunks, fried until crisp. Diri ak djon djon, rice cooked black with dried mushrooms. Soup joumou, squash soup eaten on Independence Day, January 1. Pikliz, spicy pickled cabbage and pepper relish.

Is it safe to travel to Haiti?

Most governments advise against all travel to Haiti because of gang violence, kidnapping and civil unrest. Check your government's current travel advice before planning a trip.
