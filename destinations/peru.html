Getting around

Overnight buses are the backbone of travel, and premium companies like Cruz del Sur and Oltursa are worth the small extra cost for safety and reclining seats. Uber, Cabify, DiDi and inDrive work in Lima, Arequipa and Cusco. Book Machu Picchu entry tickets and any train seats well ahead, since they sell out in the dry season.

Where to sleep

Hostel dorms and simple family guesthouses are easy to find in Arequipa and Cusco, and the Salkantay trek uses basic lodges or camping set up by tour operators. Huacachina has a handful of small hostels around the oasis.

What to eat

  • Ceviche: raw fish cured in lime juice with onion and chili
  • Lomo saltado: stir-fried beef with onions, tomatoes and fries
  • Ají de gallina: shredded chicken in a creamy yellow chili sauce
  • Chicha morada: sweet, spiced drink made from purple corn

Money

Carry soles in cash for markets, colectivos and small guesthouses, and use bank ATMs since many charge a per-withdrawal fee. Tipping around 10% is appreciated in sit-down restaurants, and polite haggling is normal at craft markets.

Phone data

Local SIMs from Claro, Movistar, Entel or Bitel need passport registration at an official store, so an eSIM is often simpler, and coverage thins out on high-altitude treks.

Heads up

Cusco sits at about 3,400 meters, so take your first days slowly and drink water to ease altitude sickness.

Peru travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Peru cost on a budget?

About $35 a day for one person, from $25 in Ayacucho to $173 in Inca Trail. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $245 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Peru?

May to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Peru?

Ayacucho, at about $25 a day. Andean city of more than thirty colonial churches, famous for Semana Santa processions, retablo craftwork and the nearby Wari ruins.

How long do you need in Peru?

Arequipa, a stop in Huacachina, then Cusco and the Salkantay trek. 3 weeks.
