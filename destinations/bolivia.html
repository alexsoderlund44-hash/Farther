Getting around

Long-distance buses are cheap, and paying a little more for a cama (reclining seat) overnight service is worth it. La Paz's Mi Teleférico cable cars are a cheap way across the city, and inDrive and Uber work in the larger cities. Compare several agencies in Uyuni before booking the salt flat jeep tour, and check reviews of the driver and vehicle.

Where to sleep

Hostel dorms and family-run hostales are cheap in La Paz and Sucre, while Uyuni tours use basic shared refuges, some built from salt, with limited heating.

What to eat

  • Salteña: juicy baked pastry filled with meat stew
  • Silpancho: thin breaded beef over rice and potatoes, topped with egg
  • Api con pastel: hot purple corn drink with a cheese-filled fried pastry
  • Sopa de maní: creamy peanut soup, often topped with fries

Money

Bolivia is mostly a cash country outside upscale places, so withdraw bolivianos in La Paz or Sucre before heading to remote areas. Small tips are welcome, and haggling is fine at markets but not in shops with fixed prices.

Phone data

Entel and Tigo SIMs are cheap but need passport registration, and coverage is good in cities but patchy or absent on the altiplano and salt flats.

Heads up

Road blockades (bloqueos) during protests can stop buses for hours or days, so leave slack in your plans and follow local news.

Bolivia travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Bolivia cost on a budget?

About $25 a day for one person, from $20 in Copacabana to $66 in Huayna Potosí. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $175 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Bolivia?

April to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Bolivia?

Copacabana, at about $20 a day. A lakeside pilgrimage town on Lake Titicaca, with sunset views from Cerro Calvario and boats out to Isla del Sol.

How long do you need in Bolivia?

La Paz, Sucre, then the Uyuni jeep tour across to Chile. 2 weeks.

How much is $1 in Bolivia?

One US dollar is about 12.06 Bolivian boliviano (BOB), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 241 BOB, and a typical budget day of $25 comes to about 300 BOB.
