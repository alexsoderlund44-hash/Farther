Namibia · Botswana · Zimbabwe

Namibia to Victoria Falls

Red dunes, desert coast, Etosha's waterholes and the Okavango Delta, ending at Victoria Falls, for travelers keen on wildlife overland.

Length20 days
Total, flights included$2,922
All-in per day$146
Stops8

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 1 · Namibia

    Windhoek 1 night · $38

    A small, orderly capital with German colonial buildings and a lively craft market, the starting point for most Namibian road trips.

    Budget tip: Hire your car here. Stock up on food, water and spare fuel cans for the desert, since prices rise in remote towns.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 2 to 4 · Namibia

    Sossusvlei 3 nights · $216

    Climb towering red dunes at sunrise, then walk across the cracked white clay of Deadvlei among its ghostly, centuries-old dead trees.

    Budget tip: Camp inside the park gate to reach the dunes for sunrise. Bring plenty of water, as the walk into Deadvlei gets hot fast.

    Getting here: about $40 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 5 to 7 · Namibia

    Swakopmund 3 nights · $138

    Sandboard down coastal dunes, kayak with seals and spot shipwrecks on the Skeleton Coast from a breezy town of German-era buildings.

    Budget tip: Book adventure activities through your hostel, which often get group rates. Stock up on supplies here before heading back inland.

    Getting here: about $45 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 8 to 10 · Namibia

    Etosha 3 nights · $210

    Elephants, black rhinos and lions gather at the waterholes on a vast white salt pan, perfect for slow self-drive safaris.

    Budget tip: Floodlit waterholes at the rest camps are great at night, and watching from a bench costs nothing extra once you're staying inside.

    Getting here: about $50 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 11 to 12 · Botswana

    Maun 2 nights · $74

    The dusty gateway town to the Okavango Delta, where mokoro canoe trips and scenic flights begin.

    Budget tip: Book a mokoro trip here; agencies are cheaper than lodges. Share scenic flights over the delta to cut the cost.

    Getting here: about $70 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 13 to 15 · Botswana

    Okavango Delta 3 nights · $300

    Glide through papyrus channels in a dugout mokoro canoe, camping on islands and walking with a guide to spot elephants and hippos.

    Budget tip: Budget mokoro trips leave from Maun and include camping. Bring your own food and sleeping bag to keep the price down.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 16 to 17 · Botswana

    Chobe National Park 2 nights · $164

    Huge elephant herds gather at the river, best seen on a sunset boat cruise along the Chobe River.

    Budget tip: Afternoon boat cruises are the best value. Stick to the riverfront section for the most wildlife.

    Getting here: about $35 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  8. Days 18 to 20 · Zimbabwe

    Victoria Falls 3 nights · $192

    One of the world's largest waterfalls, seen from rainforest paths opposite the main falls as spray drifts across the gorge.

    Budget tip: The KAZA visa covers Zimbabwe and Zambia. Bring a rain jacket or expect to get soaked in high-water season.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,300.
