Getting around

Distances are vast, so most people fly between regions and rent a car in the Rockies, though buses connect Vancouver, Banff and Jasper. VIA Rail links Montreal and Quebec City, and Uber and Lyft work in the major cities. Book Banff and Jasper beds and Parks Canada campsites early for summer, and use the park shuttle to reach Moraine Lake.

Where to sleep

HI hostels and independent dorms cover the main cities and Rockies towns, and Parks Canada campgrounds are the cheapest option in summer.

What to eat

  • Poutine: fries topped with cheese curds and gravy
  • Montreal smoked meat: spiced brisket piled high on rye bread
  • Butter tart: small pastry with a sweet, gooey filling
  • Nanaimo bar: no-bake layered bar of crumb, custard and chocolate

Money

Cards and tap payments are accepted almost everywhere. Tipping 15 to 20% in restaurants is expected, and taxes are added at the till.

Phone data

Canadian mobile plans are pricey, so an eSIM is often the simplest choice, and coverage is good in towns but absent on many mountain highways.

Heads up

Bears live throughout the Rockies, so make noise on trails, carry bear spray, and never leave food out.

Canada travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Canada cost on a budget?

About $76 a day for one person, from $67 in Quebec City to $147 in Churchill. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $532 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Canada?

June to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Canada?

Quebec City, at about $67 a day. The only walled city north of Mexico, with stone streets, the turreted Château Frontenac and a snowy winter carnival.

How long do you need in Canada?

Vancouver, Banff and Jasper, then Montreal and Quebec City. 3 weeks.

One US dollar is about 1.42 Canadian dollar (CAD), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 28.50 CAD, and a typical budget day of $76 comes to about 110 CAD.
