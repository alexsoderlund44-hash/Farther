Cheapest countries in the Middle East

What a typical day costs one person on a budget in 2026, in US dollars: a dorm bed or simple room, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees. Countries under a travel warning are left out.

Whole worldAsiaEuropeAfricaAmericasMiddle EastOceania

  1. Jordan $49/day

    Rose-red Petra, Mars-like Wadi Rum and a long-distance hiking trail that crosses the whole country, with falafel stands and Bedouin camps along the way.

    Best time: March to May and October to November. Cheapest stops: As-Salt ($33) · Little Petra (Siq al-Barid) ($36) · Karak ($37)

  2. United Arab Emirates $53/day

    Glitzy and expensive, but cheap if you stick to old Dubai, metro rides, free beaches and South Asian canteens.

    Best time: November to March. Cheapest stops: Al Ain ($47) · Sharjah ($48) · Abu Dhabi ($53)

  3. Turkey $54/day

    Bazaars and ferries in Istanbul, Cappadocia's fairy chimneys, a turquoise coast and Mesopotamian towns, with cheap street food and pensions throughout.

    Best time: April to June and September to October. Cheapest stops: Konya ($34) · Şanlıurfa ($35) · Lycian Way ($35)

  4. Oman $56/day

    Turquoise wadis, mud-brick forts and empty desert, best seen on a budget by rental car with free wild camping under the stars.

    Best time: November to March. Cheapest stops: Wadi Shab ($40) · Bimmah Sinkhole ($45) · Wadi Tiwi ($46)

  5. Saudi Arabia $90/day

    Open to tourists only since 2019, with carved Nabataean tombs, coral-stone old towns and vast desert landscapes.

    Best time: November to March. Cheapest stops: Taif ($58) · Al-Ahsa Oasis ($58) · Abha ($59)
