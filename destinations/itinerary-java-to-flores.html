Indonesia

Java to Flores

Temples near Yogyakarta, sunrise over Bromo, Ijen's crater, Bali's rice terraces, the Gilis and Komodo, an island chain for curious, flexible travelers.

Length22 days
Total, flights included$2,122
All-in per day$96
Stops9

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 3 · Indonesia

    Yogyakarta 3 nights · $75

    Explore Borobudur and Prambanan, Java's great temples, learn batik in local workshops and watch sunrise light up the Merapi volcano.

    Budget tip: Gudeg, the local jackfruit stew, costs under $2 from street stalls. Book Borobudur tickets online in advance, as climbing slots are limited.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  2. Days 4 to 4 · Indonesia

    Borobudur 1 night · $41

    Ninth-century Buddhist monument of carved relief galleries and bell-shaped stupas rising from the Kedu plain, ringed by volcanoes and village roads.

    Budget tip: Climbing the upper terraces needs a separate timed ticket with special sandals, so book online early; village homestays nearby suit a sunrise visit.

    Getting here: about $5 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  3. Days 5 to 5 · Indonesia

    Mount Bromo 1 night · $42

    Watch the sun rise over a smoking crater in a sea of sand from Penanjakan, one of Java's most famous volcano viewpoints.

    Budget tip: Walk to the viewpoint yourself from Cemoro Lawang instead of paying for a jeep. Bring warm layers for the pre-dawn cold.

    Getting here: about $25 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  4. Days 6 to 6 · Indonesia

    Kawah Ijen 1 night · $34

    Hike through the night to see blue sulfur flames glowing in the crater, then watch dawn reveal an acid-turquoise lake in East Java.

    Budget tip: Stay in cheap guesthouses in Banyuwangi. Bring a proper gas mask for the crater, since the fumes can be strong.

    Getting here: about $15 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  5. Days 7 to 10 · Indonesia

    Ubud, Bali 4 nights · $128

    Walk through emerald rice terraces, catch temple ceremonies and start a 2 a.m. hike up Mount Batur to watch the sunrise.

    Budget tip: Warungs off the main road are half the price of tourist cafes. Avoid the paid swing photo spots, which are overpriced.

    Getting here: about $20 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  6. Days 11 to 12 · Indonesia

    Amed, Bali 2 nights · $68

    Row of black-sand fishing villages on Bali's east coast with shore snorkelling, the USAT Liberty wreck at Tulamben and views of Mount Agung.

    Budget tip: Snorkel the small Japanese shipwreck straight from the beach with your own mask; rooms here cost much less than in south Bali.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  7. Days 13 to 15 · Indonesia

    Gili Air 3 nights · $93

    No cars, just bicycles and horse carts, with sea turtles grazing on seagrass a short swim off the beach.

    Budget tip: It's quieter and cheaper than Gili Trawangan. Snorkel gear rental is a couple of dollars, so skip the boat tours.

    Getting here: about $30 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  8. Days 16 to 18 · Indonesia

    Kuta Lombok 3 nights · $90

    Surf breaks and horseshoe bays line southern Lombok, a calmer and cheaper alternative to the busy beaches of Bali.

    Budget tip: Rent a scooter to reach Selong Belanak and Mawun. Warungs inland are the cheapest places to eat.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

  9. Days 19 to 22 · Indonesia

    Labuan Bajo 4 nights · $168

    Set sail to see Komodo dragons, snorkel off Pink Beach and swim with manta rays in the islands of Komodo National Park.

    Budget tip: Shared 2-day boat trips beat day tours on value. Park fees are high, so budget for them.

    Getting here: about $70 · Beds: Hostelworld · Agoda · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,200.
