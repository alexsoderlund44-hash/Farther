Getting around

The Dubai Metro and buses are clean and cheap with a Nol card, and intercity buses run from Dubai to Abu Dhabi and Ras al-Khaimah. Careem and Uber both operate across the Emirates. A rental car helps for the mountains of Ras al-Khaimah.

Where to sleep

Dorms are limited but a few hostels exist in Dubai, and budget hotels cluster in older areas like Deira and Bur Dubai. Camping is possible in some desert and mountain areas.

What to eat

  • Shawarma: spit-roasted meat wrap, the classic cheap meal
  • Machboos: spiced rice with chicken, lamb or fish
  • Luqaimat: fried dough balls drizzled with date syrup
  • Karak chai: strong tea boiled with milk and cardamom

Money

Cards and phone payments work almost everywhere, and ATMs are everywhere. Tipping around 10% is common in restaurants if no service charge is added, and haggling is expected in the souks.

Phone data

Tourist SIMs from du or e& (Etisalat) are sold at the airport with a passport, and eSIMs are easy too; coverage is excellent, though many voice and video calling apps are restricted.

Heads up

Public displays of affection, drunkenness and swearing or rude gestures can lead to fines or arrest, so keep behavior low-key.

United Arab Emirates travel: quick answers

How much does a day in United Arab Emirates cost on a budget?

About $53 a day for one person, from $47 in Al Ain to $77 in Mleiha. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $371 before flights.

When is the best time to visit United Arab Emirates?

November to March is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in United Arab Emirates?

Al Ain, at about $47 a day. A shady palm-grove oasis with mud-brick forts, falaj channels and the winding road up Jebel Hafeet for sunset views.

How long do you need in United Arab Emirates?

Dubai, Abu Dhabi and the mountains of Ras al-Khaimah. 4 to 5 days.

How much is $1 in United Arab Emirates?

One US dollar is about 3.67 UAE dirham (AED), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 73.45 AED, and a typical budget day of $53 comes to about 190 AED.
