Getting around

Minibuses and shared taxis called service are the cheap way to get around, running from hubs like Charles Helou and Cola in Beirut. Uber and Bolt operate in Beirut. There are no trains, and distances are short, so day trips to Byblos or Baalbek by minibus are easy.

Where to sleep

Beirut has hostels with dorms in Gemmayzeh and Mar Mikhael, and mountain villages offer family guesthouses. In the Qadisha Valley, simple guesthouses and monastery stays are the budget choice.

What to eat

  • Man'oushe: flatbread topped with za'atar and olive oil, a breakfast staple
  • Tabbouleh: parsley, bulgur, tomato and lemon salad
  • Kibbeh: bulgur and minced meat, fried, baked or raw
  • Ka'ak: sesame bread purse sold from street carts

Money

Lebanon runs largely on cash US dollars, and many places quote prices in dollars; cards are accepted at some hotels and restaurants, but don't rely on local ATMs. Tipping around 10% is normal in restaurants, and haggling is fine in souks.

Phone data

Prepaid SIMs from Alfa or Touch are available with your passport, but data is fairly expensive, so an eSIM is often simpler; coverage is good in cities and patchy in the mountains.

Heads up

Checkpoints are common, so carry your passport, and ask before photographing anything near army posts.

Lebanon travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Lebanon cost on a budget?

About $49 a day for one person, from $34 in Tripoli to $63 in Jeita Grotto. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $343 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Lebanon?

April to June and September to October is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Lebanon?

Tripoli, at about $34 a day. Mamluk souks, an old crusader citadel and famous sweet shops in Lebanon's second city, with far fewer visitors than Beirut.

How long do you need in Lebanon?

Beirut, Byblos, the Qadisha Valley and Baalbek. 1 week.

How much is $1 in Lebanon?

One US dollar is about 89,500 Lebanese pound (LBP), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 1,790,000 LBP, and a typical budget day of $49 comes to about 4,400,000 LBP.

How do you get around Lebanon cheaply?

Minibuses and shared taxis called service are the cheap way to get around, running from hubs like Charles Helou and Cola in Beirut. Uber and Bolt operate in Beirut. There are no trains, and distances are short, so day trips to Byblos or Baalbek by minibus are easy.

What food should you try in Lebanon?

Is it safe to travel to Lebanon?
