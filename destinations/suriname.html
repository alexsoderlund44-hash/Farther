Getting around

Cheap minibuses run from Paramaribo's central stops along fixed routes, and traffic drives on the left. River trips into the interior are usually arranged as tours that combine road transfer and a dugout boat, so book a few days ahead. Taxis don't use meters, so agree the price first.

Where to sleep

Paramaribo has a handful of guesthouses and hostel-style places, and interior lodges usually include meals and hammocks or simple huts.

What to eat

  • Roti: flatbread with curried chicken, potatoes and long beans
  • Pom: baked casserole of pomtajer root and chicken
  • Moksi alesi: mixed rice with beans and salted meat or fish
  • Saoto soup: Javanese chicken soup with bean sprouts and egg

Money

Carry Surinamese dollars in cash, changed at licensed cambios, and bring euros or US dollars as backup, as cards work mainly at larger businesses. Tipping is not strongly expected but welcome.

Phone data

Digicel and Telesur SIMs are easy to buy in Paramaribo, but coverage fades quickly upriver.

Heads up

Use insect repellent and cover up at dusk, as dengue and other mosquito-borne illnesses occur.

Suriname travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Suriname cost on a budget?

About $65 a day for one person, from $41 in Paramaribo to $97 in Raleighvallen. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $455 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Suriname?

February to March and September to November is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Suriname?

Paramaribo, at about $41 a day. A UNESCO-listed old town of white wooden buildings, where a mosque and a synagogue stand side by side.

How long do you need in Suriname?

Paramaribo, then a river trip to the interior. 1 week.

How much is $1 in Suriname?

One US dollar is about 37.66 Surinamese dollar (SRD), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 753 SRD, and a typical budget day of $65 comes to about 2,400 SRD.
