Mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. Cash machines and exchange desks pay a little less.

Getting around

The MRT and buses reach almost everywhere, and you can tap in with a contactless bank card. Grab, Gojek and TADA work for rides. Nothing needs booking ahead except buses into Malaysia on weekends and holidays.

Where to sleep

Hostel dorms and capsule-style pods are the budget choice, since hotels are expensive.

What to eat

  • Hainanese chicken rice: poached chicken with rice cooked in stock
  • Laksa: spicy coconut curry noodle soup
  • Kaya toast: toast with coconut jam, butter and soft eggs
  • Kopi: local coffee, strong and sweet

Money

Cards and mobile payments work almost everywhere, though some hawker stalls are still cash only. Tipping isn't expected, and haggling is not part of the culture.

Phone data

Tourist SIMs from Singtel, StarHub or M1 are sold at the airport with your passport, coverage is excellent, and an eSIM is just as easy.

Heads up

Fines for littering, jaywalking and eating or drinking on the MRT are real and enforced.

Singapore travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Singapore cost on a budget?

About $53 a day for one person, from $49 in Little India, Singapore to $61 in Sentosa. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $371 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Singapore?

February to August is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Singapore?

Little India, Singapore, at about $49 a day. Bright Hindu temples, garland stalls, spice shops and South Indian food fill the busy streets around Serangoon Road.

How long do you need in Singapore?

2 to 3 days as a stopover between Malaysia and Indonesia.

How much is $1 in Singapore?

One US dollar is about 1.28 Singapore dollar (SGD), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 25.59 SGD, and a typical budget day of $53 comes to about 68.00 SGD.

How do you get around Singapore cheaply?

The MRT and buses reach almost everywhere, and you can tap in with a contactless bank card. Grab, Gojek and TADA work for rides. Nothing needs booking ahead except buses into Malaysia on weekends and holidays.

What food should you try in Singapore?

Hainanese chicken rice, poached chicken with rice cooked in stock. Laksa, spicy coconut curry noodle soup. Kaya toast, toast with coconut jam, butter and soft eggs. Kopi, local coffee, strong and sweet.
