Spain vs Italy: which is cheaper?

Spain is the cheaper of the two, at about $68 a day against $87 in Italy, which is roughly 22% less each day. Two weeks costs about $952 in Spain and $1,218 in Italy, before flights. Both are at their best in April to June and September to October, so you could combine them on one trip.
