Madagascar

Madagascar on Route Nationale 7

Lemurs in Andasibe, highland towns, the rainforest of Ranomafana, the canyons of Isalo and the beach at Ifaty, on the island's classic road south.

Length16 days
Total, flights included$1,848
All-in per day$116
Stops7

Load this route into the planner

  1. Days 1 to 2 · Madagascar

    Antananarivo 2 nights · $56

    A hilly capital crowned by the Rova royal palace, with the sacred royal hill of Ambohimanga an easy day trip away.

    Budget tip: Use it as a base for 1 or 2 nights between flights and long bus rides. Taxis are cheap, but agree the price first.

    Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  2. Days 3 to 4 · Madagascar

    Andasibe 2 nights · $70

    Hear the haunting call of the indri, the largest lemur, echo through the rainforest, then spot chameleons on a night walk.

    Budget tip: Hire a local guide at the park gate. Night walks on the road outside the park cost less than inside.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  3. Days 5 to 6 · Madagascar

    Antsirabe 2 nights · $42

    A highland spa town of colorful pousse-pousse rickshaws, gem workshops and volcanic crater lakes like Tritriva nearby.

    Budget tip: Ride a pousse-pousse for under $1. It's a good overnight stop on the long road south, with cheap guesthouses.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  4. Days 7 to 8 · Madagascar

    Ranomafana 2 nights · $68

    Misty cloud forest home to the rare golden bamboo lemur, with hot springs to soak in back in the village.

    Budget tip: Go early in the morning for the best lemur sightings, and book guides at the park office.

    Getting here: about $10 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  5. Days 9 to 9 · Madagascar

    Fianarantsoa 1 night · $22

    Highland town with a hilltop old town of churches and brick houses, tea plantations and vineyards in the surrounding hills.

    Budget tip: Use it as a base for Ranomafana and the Sahambavy tea estate, and eat at the market for cheap local dishes.

    Getting here: about $4 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  6. Days 10 to 12 · Madagascar

    Isalo National Park 3 nights · $114

    Hike through sandstone canyons to natural swimming pools, with ring-tailed lemurs leaping through the trees above.

    Budget tip: Ranohira village has cheap guesthouses. Guides are mandatory and priced per circuit, so join other hikers to share.

    Getting here: about $12 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

  7. Days 13 to 16 · Madagascar

    Ifaty 4 nights · $124

    Fishing coast north of Toliara with Vezo outrigger pirogues, a barrier reef for snorkelling and a spiny forest of baobabs and octopus trees.

    Budget tip: Take a taxi-brousse from Toliara instead of a hotel transfer, and book snorkel trips with Vezo fishermen on the beach.

    Getting here: about $8 · Beds: Hostelworld · Booking.com

Costs are per person for a budget traveller: a dorm bed or simple room, local food and transport, and the usual entry fees. Flights to the start and home are estimated at $1,300.
