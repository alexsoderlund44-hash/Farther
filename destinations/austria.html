Getting around

ÖBB and Westbahn trains link Vienna, Salzburg and Innsbruck, and cheap Sparschiene fares appear when you book a few weeks ahead. Regional buses (Postbus) reach Hallstatt and the Alpine valleys, and Uber and Bolt work in Vienna.

Where to sleep

Hostel dorms are easy to find in Vienna, Salzburg and Innsbruck, while family-run Gasthof and Pension rooms fill the gap elsewhere. Hallstatt has very few beds, so stay nearby or book well ahead.

What to eat

  • Wiener Schnitzel: thin breaded veal or pork cutlet, fried golden
  • Käsekrainer: cheese-filled sausage from a Vienna street stand
  • Kaiserschmarrn: torn fluffy pancake with powdered sugar and fruit compote
  • Apfelstrudel: thin pastry rolled around spiced apple and raisins

Money

Cards work almost everywhere, but carry some cash for mountain huts and small bakeries. Round up or add about 5 to 10 percent in restaurants, and say the total when you hand over the money.

Phone data

Local prepaid SIMs from A1, Magenta or Drei are cheap, EU SIMs roam free, and coverage is strong except in some deep Alpine valleys.

Heads up

Most shops close on Sundays, so stock up on groceries on Saturday.

Austria travel: quick answers

How much does a day in Austria cost on a budget?

About $69 a day for one person, from $56 in Lake Neusiedl to $80 in Grossglockner. That covers a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, local food, local transport and the usual entry fees, so a week runs about $483 before flights.

When is the best time to visit Austria?

May to September is the best time, with the most reliable weather for getting around.

What is the cheapest place to visit in Austria?

Lake Neusiedl, at about $56 a day. Shallow steppe lake on the Hungarian border, ringed by reeds, birdlife, cycle paths and wine villages like Rust with storks nesting on chimneys.

How long do you need in Austria?

Vienna, Salzburg, Hallstatt and the Tyrol. 10 days.

One US dollar is about 0.889 Euro (EUR), going by the mid-market rate on 2026-10-05. So $20 is roughly 17.78 EUR, and a typical budget day of $69 comes to about 61.00 EUR.
