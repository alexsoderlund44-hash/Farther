About Farther

Built for travelling longer on less

A free planner with real daily costs, ready-made routes and a budget tracker. No ads and no paywall.

How we work out costs

Each place has a typical daily budget for one person: a bed in a hostel dorm or simple guesthouse, eating where locals eat, local buses and trains, and the usual entry fees, permits or tours.

The figures are checked against recent prices and reviewed once a year. They are starting points for planning, so check live prices before you book, especially in peak season and during festivals.

Travel warnings

Countries marked with a travel warning have current government advice against some or all travel. We summarise that advice, but it changes quickly, so always check your own government's travel advice and your insurance cover before you go.

How we make money

Booking links

Some links on Farther go to booking sites for hostels, hotels, flights, buses and trains, car hire, tours, travel insurance and mobile data. Many of these are affiliate links. When you book through one, the company you book with may pay us a small commission.

You pay the same price

The commission comes out of the booking site's own share. You pay exactly what you would pay going to that site directly, and you can always book anywhere you like.

What commissions never change

  • The daily costs we show. They come from real prices, not from who pays us.
  • Which countries and places we list, and the order we list them in.
  • Budget tips that tell you how to spend less, including ways to skip paid tours.

Who we link to

We link to well-known sites budget travellers already use, such as Hostelworld, Booking.com, Agoda, Skyscanner, Kiwi.com, 12Go, Omio, FlixBus, Busbud, DiscoverCars, GetYourGuide, Viator, SafetyWing and Airalo. We only earn from the ones we have an agreement with, and the list may change.

How you can tell

Booking links open the partner's site in a new tab. Where a link can earn us a commission, a note next to it says so.

Your privacy

Without a profile

Your trips are saved in your own browser on this device. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

With a profile

If you create a profile, we save your display name, home city, usual budget, travel styles and your trips to your account, so you can open them on any device. You can delete any trip at any time.

What we never do

We do not sell your data or share your trips with anyone.

Booking sites

When you follow a link to a booking site, that site's own privacy policy applies. It may set a cookie so the booking can be credited to us. See how we make money.
